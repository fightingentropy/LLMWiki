# Youtube

Saved YouTube videos. Most links open at one moment of a longer video, and the link title is the thought worth keeping, not the video's own title. The threads running through them: action over chasing happiness, delusion as a tool, and usefulness over truth. The top podcast picks close the page.

## Happiness and doing epic shit

Three moments from one long conversation, saved minutes apart, make one argument: happiness is not the goal, action is. See [[Philosophy & Meaning]].

- ["My Goal in life is not necessarily to be happy. My goal in life is to do epic shit."](https://youtu.be/Gk8EGWoGnEQ?t=8918) — At 2:28:38: agency and accomplishment as the goal, instead of optimising for feeling good.
- [Views on happiness](https://youtu.be/Gk8EGWoGnEQ?t=9084) — At 2:31:24: the same speaker on happiness.
- [The only way is through](https://youtu.be/Gk8EGWoGnEQ?t=9136) — At 2:32:16: no shortcuts; the path runs through the difficulty, not around it.

## Motivation and school

- [How can you lack motivation?](https://youtu.be/ONs2tlD1YgE?t=2074) — At 34:34: a reframe that treats lacking motivation as the odd case that needs explaining.
- [He means irrational, but makes a great point about school](https://youtu.be/PAWMoQNq3W0?t=611) — At 10:11: the speaker picks the wrong word, but his critique of school stands.

## Delusion, soul and usefulness

- [Why delusion is important](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pR86kI5aDFA&t=3349s) — At 55:49: self-belief that runs ahead of the evidence, as a tool for getting things done.
- [Soul in the game](https://youtu.be/a3YOMe4PnKE) — [[Nassim Taleb]]'s skin in the game, extended: caring about the output beyond your personal stake.
- ["What's useful is more important than what is true"](https://youtu.be/sYj0exUT_Mw?t=80) — At 1:20: judge an idea by what it lets you do, not only by whether it is accurate.

## Life, intimacy and money

- [Bukowski's advice is funny but might work for a lot of people](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mpRUnavb2_0) — Charles Bukowski's advice: crude and reductive, yet a workable rule of thumb for many people.
- [True intimacy](https://youtu.be/eLj9HrKfcYE?t=525) — At 8:45: on what true intimacy requires; see [[Dating & Relationships]].
- [What is inflation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lO5-N5NmAtQ) — A primer on inflation; background for [[Economics]].
