# Peptides

The source is a saved retatrutide preparation and injection guide. Its original quantities, tables and claims remain intact in the source record below. The earlier wiki generalized this guide to other research peptides; that was not supported by formulation evidence.

## What is established here

The [FDA's current statement](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-alerts-and-statements/fdas-concerns-unapproved-glp-1-drugs-used-weight-loss) identifies [[Retatrutide]] as unapproved in the US. A vendor's powder, an online schedule and correct dilution arithmetic do not establish product identity, safety or clinical suitability. (from [[Health Evidence Review - September 2026]])

## Unsupported precision in the guide

- Its universal 28-day refrigerated life and 24-hour sterile-water rule are not backed by a formulation-specific label or stability study.
- The stated percentage losses from shaking and room-temperature storage have no supporting study attached.
- A clear-looking solution does not establish sterility, potency or identity.
- The dose-escalation table is not an approved prescribing schedule.

[[Peptide Reconstitution]] and [[Bacteriostatic Water]] explain why vehicle compatibility and storage instructions must be specific to the actual medicinal product. Preserve this guide as a source to scrutinize, not as validated instructions for treatment.
