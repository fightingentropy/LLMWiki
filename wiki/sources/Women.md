# Women

A running collection on women, dating, attraction and "game": saved X posts, pickup-account one-liners, [[Heartiste]], [[DrDynamis]] and a Tucker Max excerpt. The voice is crude and red-pill and is quoted as written; most lines are other people's words. It contradicts itself often: women are "memoryless" yet "detach emotionally before they leave", and "Chicks don't care about money" sits beside "Avoid being poor." The wider argument lives on [[Dating & Relationships]] and [[Gender Dynamics]].

[Read the original note](/source/bba303a12f1befc4cdb9) for every post, clip and list.

## How men and women read actions

An essay by @minordissent sets the frame: men treat actions as disposable, women read them as identity.

> "EVERY action matters, EVERY action has a deeper story, a deeper moral weight to it. … To a woman, if you do a thing, you will never hear the end of it because to her it signals your IDENTITY and that is far harder to change." — @minordissent

> "Women are born valuable. Men must become valuable." — @minordissent

## Living in the moment

Women run on the present and on proximity; one handle calls that their edge.

> "I've learned after many disappointing situations that you can't build equity with women. Every day is like a brand new day with them. There's no past, nor future. Only now."

> "ur coworker is not Hot they're just within 10 ft of u 40 hrs/week. Called 'proximity infatuation'." — @Nia_mp4

> "ONLY thing chicks are better @ than men is being better grounded in the present. Most ambitious dudes live in some nebulous future." — @ArcadianRythms

## What attracts

Attraction follows threat, vibe and fun more than looks or money. Humor gets its own list: it de-escalates conflict, signals intelligence and shows you can read a room. "Slick talking husband gets your tribe out of mass beheadings."

> "the most attractive women are slightly male-brained and the most attractive men are slightly female-brained. it's yin and yang. a flicker of hardness in the feathers, a trace of softness in the steel" — @cookiecarver

> "Women aren't necessarily attracted to height. Women are attracted to men who are threatening to other men." — @TheEcho13

> "Chicks don't care about money, they care about fun. Money simply facilitates fun, but is not the root of it."

## Frame and indifference

Want her, but do not need her.

> "Banks give loans to people who can prove they don't need them. Girls sleep with men who don't need them. There's a big lesson in there."

> "Women love persistent men who are persistent from a position of want, not need" — Heartiste

> "Don't waste your time chasing butterflies. Take care of your garden and they will come."

## Approach and dates

Approach anyway, listen more than you talk, and ask the hard questions: "Her values matters, not her interests". Tucker Max's model in *What Women Want*: drop the sexual goal and chase fun; at 24 it tripled his results with half the time per woman. A woman's 14-point list from @WhySanaaWhy adds: "Women can sense your bullshit."

> "Smoking a cig outside a bar will yield you more dates than every dating app combined." — @bumbadum14

> "The most seductive thing you can do when on a date with a woman is listen. Listening is an overpowered seductive technique."

> "Tell yourself that your only goal is to meet new women and have as much fun with them as possible." — Tucker Max

## Heartiste's Sixteen Commandments

Roissy's list is the spine of the collection. Among the headings: never say "I love you" first; your mission, not your woman, is your priority; always keep two in the kitty; ignore her beauty; maintain your state control; never be afraid to lose her, because "Fear is the love-killer."

> "A man with options is a man without need."

> "You are an oak tree."

> "Success with women is more disillusioning than failure." — Heartiste

## Marriage and a man's life

The reading of 1 Corinthians 7:27-28 sets the default, and a pre-marriage checklist ends "Love alone is not enough." DrDynamis widens it into rules for a man's life.

> "Do not marry out of obligation. … The default path is being single, anything else is putting the cart before the horse. You must make peace with the uncertainly of not knowing if or when."

> "Number 1 killer of men isn't 'seed oils' But a fear of confrontation." — DrDynamis

> "Say 'I like u but I don't respect u' & u'll have made an enemy 4 life. Respect > Adoration" — DrDynamis

> "Only things that matter are 1. Did you get right with God? 2. Did you live like a G? 3. Did you leave an impact on this fallen earth?" — DrDynamis
