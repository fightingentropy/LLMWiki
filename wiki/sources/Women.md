# Women

Erlin's running note on women, dating, attraction and "game". It is a dump of saved X posts, pickup-account one-liners, Heartiste and DrDynamis quotes, a Tucker Max excerpt and a few long essays, with no headings; several lines recur, which reads as emphasis. Most lines are other people's words; the note rarely says which are Erlin's own. The voice is crude and red-pill-adjacent and is quoted as written, and the note contradicts itself often (see [Contradictions](#contradictions)). The synthesis lives on [[Dating_and_Relationships|Dating & Relationships]], [[Gender Dynamics]] and [[Masculinity_and_Men|Masculinity & Men]].

## Motherhood, politics and value

A long answer to the question "Why does so much of women's politics revolve around the right to murder their own children?" The note ties it to the same author as the essay above.

- "Becoming a mother is an all-consuming and irreversible trajectory change in life." Physically handicapped from pregnancy until a year after birth; children who cannot bear to be apart until about two; "utterly dependent on your husband (or some other resource provider)" until the youngest is five; then a decade-long gap that "permanently toasts your life time earnings and career growth".
- Women used to have no choice in whether, and little in when. Now they do, so hesitating or opting out is "a totally sane and rational response".
- The poster's thought experiment: even with a 90% chance of a 20X return, only about a third of respondents took the bet; with odds based on modern divorce rates, "effectively no one took it". Those respondents were risk-tolerant men; "What do you think the average woman (highly risk averse) would respond with?"
- Abortion politics are framed as the same thing as men's politics about not dying in wars, family courts, taxation and replacement, "and is also the same reason men are opting out of marriage".
- Men's wider political interests are not intellectual superiority: biology does not choose a man's job for him, so men have more variance in vocation and therefore in interest.
- "Women are born valuable. Men must become valuable." Both are double-edged. Women are valued without developing themselves but cannot opt out of the value biology assigns ("too bad. Sucks nerd."); men can become anything valuable but are "expelled from the tribe and left to die" if they do not. Defunct men romanticise women's "easy mode"; defunct women romanticise male agency.

**"Cherished property" and "disposable tool."** In a [later post](https://x.com/minordissent/status/1965159762024435791), Max (@minordissent) frames traditional roles as opposite bargains: women valued intrinsically but constrained as "cherished property", men freer but valued only for usefulness as "disposable tools". He argues that escaping these roles creates new dependence (women economically disposable, men consumers managed by large corporations) and proposes self-cultivation, provider status and traditional family formation instead. It is an ideological model, not a biological or sociological finding; the property and tool language is deliberately objectifying and the sex categories are reductive. It overlaps the "born valuable / must become valuable" argument above.

## On dates

**Listening.** "The most seductive thing you can do when on a date with a woman is listen. Listening is an overpowered seductive technique." Be present, ask questions that expand on what she said, don't be boring, and remember it is a date: "stimulate their emotions and escalate physically". The list saved with it:

- Let women do the majority of the talking.
- Don't directly reveal information about yourself.
- Be outwardly oriented (focused on her and how she feels).
- Make her laugh.
- Show you are listening with your body, particularly facial expressions.
- Ask questions that build off what she said; avoid "unrelated, rapid fire questions like it's a job interview".
- Ask questions that are funny and unpredictable.
- "Drop breadcrumbs for her to follow if there is something about yourself you want her to find out. Never explicitly state your value."
- Don't get distracted by anything in the background.

## Contradictions

- **Memoryless versus planning.** "Women are memoryless" and "no past, nor future" against "women detach emotionally before they leave" and "women spend half the relationship getting over it", which imply forward planning. Likewise "A woman's love lasts until she finds a better suiter".
- **Rationality.** "The most attractive women are slightly male-brained" against "Once a woman starts acting rational & like a grownup around you … she's not in love with you anymore." Rationality is coded both attractive and terminal.
- **Who manipulates whom.** "Women control men through tears, sex, and drama" against "women are far more cunning than the average man gives them credit for, and men are far less malicious than the average woman assumes them to be".
- **Porn.** "Porn is for cucks" against "Yes, I'm going to read a slutty little romance novel. No, my husband can't watch porn." The note's stance on the double standard is unclear.
- **Hard work.** "Hard work is pleb shit" against lifting and prayer as the only things that matter; hard work is recast as identity maintenance.
- **Strategy or self.** @AJA_Cortes says women do things for themselves, not to manipulate men; most of the note assumes female behaviour is legible strategy aimed at men.
- **Money.** "Chicks don't care about money, they care about fun" against "Things that ATTRACT girls: Being handsome, in shape, rich" and "Avoid being poor."
- **Two value bargains.** The "cherished property / disposable tool" model restates the "Women are born valuable. Men must become valuable" argument in more objectifying terms.
