- The ADHD/circadian bookmark is linked to a perspective article, not a new diagnostic definition. See the correction in [[ADHD]] and [[Circadian_Rhythm]].

## Source register

The following annotations are reproduced from the curated Brain notes. Dates record retention or revision, not independent verification. A revised annotation supersedes the earlier annotation for the same bookmark. Links, uncertainty, and missing-context labels are preserved.

- [ADHD, delayed body clocks, and circadian interventions](https://x.com/BrandonLuuMD/status/2102338491942347175) — Brandon Luu describes a link between ADHD and later biological sleep/wake timing, with early evidence that shifting the body clock earlier may ease symptoms for some people. The [quoted explanation](https://x.com/anishmoonka/status/2102284259553890448) and his 2025 perspective with Nicholas Fabiano cite delayed sleep/wake timing in up to 78%, sleep disturbances in up to 80% of adults and 82% of children, and an evening melatonin rise about 45 minutes later in children and 90 minutes later in adults. The attached graphic depicts later melatonin timing and a later, flatter morning cortisol rise. Their proposed approach includes screening for sleep/circadian problems, a consistent wake-up time, morning bright light, less evening light and screen exposure, regular routines, and selective low-dose melatonin when delayed timing is identified. They describe these measures as additions to standard ADHD care, with treatment evidence still developing. _([paper: ADHD as a circadian rhythm disorder](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2025.1697900/full); [[Medical|related existing Brain note]]; Brandon Luu, MD (@BrandonLuuMD); manually added 2026-09-22)_

- [Complete male fruit-fly central nervous-system connectome](https://x.com/JIMMYEDGAR/status/2095579582879383756) — Google Research describes a real map containing more than 166,000 neurons, extending connectomics work across the male fly's central nervous system. Preserve the scientific milestone; the repost's anthropomorphic claim that an AI “talked about rotting food” is a playful interpretation rather than a reported experimental conclusion. _([Google Research](https://research.google/blog/a-connectomics-milestone-mapping-the-complete-male-fruit-fly-brain/); Jimmy Edgar (@JIMMYEDGAR); retained 2026-09-05)_

- [Proclaim custom whole-mouth water flosser](https://x.com/paulbohm/status/2095259998297133215) — A personalized mouthpiece, produced from an intraoral scan, is advertised to clean around the whole mouth using multiple jets in about seven seconds. Keep as a dental-product and distribution lead, not a private-equity thesis or replacement for professional advice; independent effectiveness, adherence, consumables, cleaning, contraindications, and price still matter. _([product page](https://shop.proclaimhealth.com/products/additional-personalized-mouthpiece); Paul Böhm (@paulbohm); retained 2026-09-05)_

- [Peptides versus supplements as an experiential claim](https://x.com/minordissent/status/2094564810554052961) — The author reports that several peptides felt noticeable while most supplements felt like placebo, while conceding that peptides are overhyped. Preserve it as one person's experience and a prompt for compound-specific research—not comparative evidence of efficacy, safety, purity, dosing, or legality. _(Minor Dissent (@minordissent); retained 2026-09-05)_

- [Testosterone deficiency can overlap with depressive symptoms](https://x.com/wanted4mogging/status/2094567596490162625) — The post argues that prescribing antidepressants without testosterone screening proves systemic failure. The narrower point is useful: low mood, energy, libido, concentration, and sexual symptoms can overlap with hypogonadism and may justify clinical evaluation. Diagnosis requires compatible symptoms plus consistently low, accurately measured morning testosterone—not universal screening or assuming testosterone is the cause. _([Endocrine Society](https://www.endocrine.org/clinical-practice-guidelines/testosterone-therapy); retained 2026-09-05)_

- [Purpose and life-alignment as one dimension of depression](https://x.com/minordissent/status/2094327547672805434) — A motivational post frames depression as the psyche refusing an unaligned life and purpose as the cure. Keep the invitation to examine meaning, relationships, work, and environment, but reject the absolute: depression has heterogeneous biological, psychological, and social contributors, and purpose does not replace assessment, therapy, medication, safety planning, or other appropriate care. _(Minor Dissent (@minordissent); retained 2026-09-05)_

- [Demodex mites, rosacea, and topical ivermectin](https://x.com/SMB_Attorney/status/2093856369203888444) — An anecdotal post says prescription ivermectin transformed persistent skin problems after learning about Demodex. Demodex density can be elevated in rosacea and topical ivermectin is an established treatment for papulopustular rosacea, but mites normally inhabit healthy skin and are not a universal explanation for acne, redness, or irritation. Diagnosis and prescription choice belong with a clinician. _([National Rosacea Society](https://www.rosacea.org/rosacea-review/2025/spring/topical-ivermectin-may-improve-rosacea-indirectly); retained 2026-09-05)_

- [Sex differences in mental-rotation performance](https://x.com/antarte_/status/2093756333010952414) — The bookmark extrapolates from a water-bottle rotation question to broad claims about women, systemizing, and IQ-test construction. A 2026 meta-analysis does report an average male advantage on mental-rotation tests, with substantial heterogeneity; it does not substantiate the post's “40%” college-women statistic, general intelligence conclusion, or claim that psychometricians deliberately removed systemizing to equalize scores. _([meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41566416/); retained 2026-09-05)_

- [H. pylori and refractory levothyroxine absorption](https://x.com/FarvingCo/status/2093783673623740918) — A 2011 study found that treating H. pylori in patients with hypothyroidism who were not responding to high-dose levothyroxine substantially lowered TSH, plausibly by improving absorption; 21% developed iatrogenic thyrotoxicosis afterward. This does not validate the tweet's broad symptom list, belly-fat mechanism, low-T3 certainty, or self-directed supplement “kill stack.” H. pylori needs proper testing and clinician-directed eradication therapy. _([study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21435090/); retained 2026-09-05)_

- [Follicular fluid differentially attracts human sperm](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32517615/) — A 2020 in-vitro study using samples from couples undergoing assisted reproduction found that follicular fluid from different women consistently attracted sperm from particular men differently, and the stronger response did not systematically favor the woman's partner. This supports a possible gamete-mediated selection mechanism; it did not place eggs beside competing sperm, prove conscious choice, or establish a clinical test for unexplained infertility. A later [crude repost](https://x.com/mak_aura/status/2094854871212253473) turns the result into a joke about women making wrong decisions “molecularly”; preserve the source trail, not that inference. _(Discovered via Anish Moonka (@anishmoonka); updated 2026-09-05)_

- [Depression can affect concentration, memory, and practiced skills](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/publications/depression) — Difficulty concentrating, remembering, or making decisions is a recognized symptom of depression, and severe cases can involve prominent memory and thinking problems. This supports one [bookmark author's lived experience](https://x.com/Laiiyylaa/status/2085925048335126831) and a later [anhedonia-to-skill-loss model](https://x.com/thehowlchemist/status/2094249098312384915): reduced pleasure and practice can plausibly erode fluency. Neither post establishes inevitable permanent “brain damage,” neurotoxicity, or irreversible pruning; persistent cognitive symptoms deserve clinical assessment rather than self-diagnosis from a post. _(Laiiyylaa (@Laiiyylaa); selected 2026-08-09; updated 2026-09-05)_
