# Bookmarks - Health & Psychology

Health and psychology links saved from X and the web: ADHD and sleep, mood, habits, skin, screening, nutrition and new research. Many posts sit next to the study or guideline that tests them; [[Health Evidence]] covers how to weigh the two.

Saved posts and studies, described from their notes; the claims are the authors' own, and treatment decisions belong with a clinician.

## ADHD, sleep timing and medication

- [Brandon Luu on ADHD and the body clock](https://x.com/BrandonLuuMD/status/2102338491942347175) — Proposes a fixed wake time, morning light, less evening screen light and selective low-dose melatonin alongside standard ADHD care; [quoted explanation](https://x.com/anishmoonka/status/2102284259553890448). _(@BrandonLuuMD)_
- [ADHD as a circadian rhythm disorder](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2025.1697900/full) — 2025 perspective by Luu and Nicholas Fabiano: delayed sleep timing in up to 78% of people with ADHD; melatonin rises 45–90 minutes later.
  - Correction (22 September 2026): this is a perspective article describing a circadian subgroup, not a new diagnostic definition. See [[Circadian Rhythm]].
- [Negative experience with Vyvanse](https://x.com/LawrenceElliots/status/2102046696142238206) — Blames relationship problems, sensory irritation, isolation and personality change on 70 mg lisdexamfetamine; quotes a broader anti-medication post. _(@LawrenceElliots)_
- [Positive experience with Adderall](https://x.com/Gena_I_Gorlin/status/2101802695325528433) — Gena Gorlin says medication helped her feel more consistently herself; links her essay on moving from ADHD scepticism to treatment. _(@Gena_I_Gorlin)_
- [ADHD procrastination tips](https://x.com/apaji_x/status/2096141656789598398) — Thread opener promising ADHD-friendly ways to start tasks; the list is in the full thread. _(@apaji_x)_
- [Limerence](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/limerence) — Cleveland Clinic: an intense, involuntary fixation on another person, not a diagnosis, tied to insecure attachment and possibly ADHD hyperfocus.
- [Limerence as dopamine addiction](https://x.com/apaji_x/status/2091560421555614093) — The post that led to the Cleveland Clinic page; calls limerence a severe dopamine addiction. _(@apaji_x)_
- [Antihistamines for "ADHD brains"](https://x.com/be_like_legend1/status/2096567715519434845) — Compares Allegra, Zyrtec and Pepcid for people with ADHD, with no evidence given. _(@be_like_legend1)_
- [ADHD as a histamine and sleep problem](https://x.com/ArsAlendi/status/2096617680815980866) — Builds a histamine-and-sleep theory of ADHD from one person's response to an antihistamine. _(@ArsAlendi)_
- [Three-day depression and ADHD "cure"](https://x.com/oxidativestate/status/2097515710172025053) — Says a "dopamine reset" cured depression and ADHD in three days; kept as an example of health misinformation. _(@oxidativestate)_

## Depression, mood and cognition

- [NIMH on depression](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/publications/depression) — Trouble concentrating, remembering and deciding are recognised symptoms; severe depression can bring marked memory and thinking problems.
- [Depression and lost skills](https://x.com/Laiiyylaa/status/2085925048335126831) — First-person account of depression dulling concentration, memory and once-practised skills. _(@Laiiyylaa)_
- [From anhedonia to skill loss](https://x.com/thehowlchemist/status/2094249098312384915) — Lost pleasure means less practice, and less practice erodes fluency; the post calls the result brain damage. _(@thehowlchemist)_
- [Depression, anxiety and memory](https://x.com/DEUILSTEARS444/status/2096602733654552989) — Depression and anxiety can come with substantial memory problems. _(@DEUILSTEARS444)_
- [Depression as refusal of an unaligned life](https://x.com/minordissent/status/2094327547672805434) — Motivational post casting purpose as the cure; useful as a prompt to examine meaning, work and relationships. _(@minordissent)_
- [The isolation and rumination loop](https://x.com/sunnkssdseraph/status/2086173566710743236) — Staying home, worry and falling motivation make ordinary life feel distant; the way back starts with one small action. _(@sunnkssdseraph)_
- [Legitimate suffering and neurotic avoidance](https://x.com/aristomarinetti/status/2018034523141804336) — Jungian frame: dodging unavoidable pain manufactures larger, unnecessary suffering. _(@aristomarinetti)_
- [A wandering mind is an unhappy mind](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1192439) — Killingsworth and Gilbert, 2010: people felt less happy while mind-wandering, even to pleasant topics. Found via [Nicholas Fabiano](https://x.com/NTFabiano/status/2086444739805532641). _(@NTFabiano)_
- [Negative thinking and Alzheimer's](https://x.com/dhh/status/2101644926094373128) — DHH jokes about a study linking repetitive negative thinking to Alzheimer's-related protein build-up. _(@dhh)_
- [Gut microbiota from depressed people, in rats](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpsychires.2016.07.019) — 2016 study: microbiota from three depressed donors produced anhedonia- and anxiety-like behaviour and altered tryptophan metabolism in rats. _(via @NTFabiano)_
- [Cyclic sighing study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100895) — Month-long randomised study of five-minute daily breathing versus mindfulness; mood and breathing-rate benefits were strongest with cyclic sighing. Found via a [post](https://x.com/SahilBloom/status/2107476951908966700).

## Attention, habits and sleep

- [Neuroplasticity and deliberate repetition](https://x.com/alexeixbt/status/2086051211271196886) — Motivational essay: adult brains keep changing through repeated practice, attention, recovery and sleep. _(@alexeixbt)_
- [Acetylcholine and motor-cortex plasticity](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6673455/) — Human study of acetylcholine's role in motor-cortex plasticity; background for the neuroplasticity essay.
- [Resisting cravings at peak intensity](https://x.com/alexeixbt/status/2100716907108151736) — Argues that resisting a craving at its strongest gives the biggest "rewiring" effect. _(@alexeixbt)_
- [Two weeks without mobile internet](https://academic.oup.com/pnasnexus/article/4/2/pgaf017/8016017) — Preregistered PNAS Nexus trial: blocking smartphone internet for two weeks improved well-being, mental health and sustained attention; compliance was hard.
- [Fabiano on the mobile-internet trial](https://x.com/NTFabiano/status/2084270984022905286) — The post that shared the trial, with its "ten years younger" attention comparison. _(@NTFabiano)_
- [More on the mobile-internet trial](https://x.com/sentientist/status/2054591492162040312) — Further discussion of the same trial. _(@sentientist)_
- [The two-hour sleep thought experiment](https://x.com/waitbutwhy/status/1906866707534147681) — Tim Urban: people would take a drug giving two extra hours' benefit, yet give up two hours of sleep. _(@waitbutwhy)_
- [How much sleep adults need](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep/how-much-sleep) — NHLBI: adults generally need seven to nine hours, and needs vary.
- [Cold-shower "unbracing"](https://x.com/jonnym1ller/status/1958345432410845515) — Notice the tension before cold water hits and practise releasing it. _(@jonnym1ller)_

## Skin, hair, face and teeth

- [Tazarotene versus tretinoin](https://x.com/Hoostethics/status/2093649112352821464) — Argues tazarotene is the stronger retinoid, citing one before-and-after photo of a routine that also used azelaic acid. _(@Hoostethics)_
- [24-week tazarotene–tretinoin trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15203997/) — Randomised photoaging trial: some tazarotene formulations improved selected measures faster than tretinoin, with more early irritation.
- [Multicentre tazarotene concentration trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11735710/) — Photoaging trial comparing tazarotene concentrations with tretinoin; some concentrations did better on selected measures.
- [Demodex, rosacea and ivermectin](https://x.com/SMB_Attorney/status/2093856369203888444) — Personal account of prescription ivermectin clearing persistent skin problems after learning about Demodex mites. _(@SMB_Attorney)_
- [Topical ivermectin for rosacea](https://www.rosacea.org/rosacea-review/2025/spring/topical-ivermectin-may-improve-rosacea-indirectly) — National Rosacea Society: topical ivermectin is an established rosacea treatment; Demodex mites also live on healthy skin.
- [Red-light therapy](https://www.aad.org/public/cosmetic/safety/red-light-therapy) — American Academy of Dermatology: evidence for some skin-ageing concerns and hereditary hair loss; results vary by device and person.
- [Red-light device post](https://x.com/dantefofante/status/2091994204365009297) — Promotes an unnamed Amazon red-light device for recovery, mood, energy and general health. _(@dantefofante)_
- [Microneedling plus minoxidil](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23960389/) — 2013 blinded pilot in 100 men: 12-week hair counts rose 91.4 with microneedling plus minoxidil versus 22.2 with minoxidil alone. _(via @drmikehart)_
- [Microneedling meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40056230/) — Meta-analysis of RCTs: microneedling with minoxidil beats minoxidil alone on hair counts; protocols and study quality vary.
- [Proclaim custom water flosser](https://x.com/paulbohm/status/2095259998297133215) — Personalised mouthpiece from an intraoral scan, advertised to clean the whole mouth with multiple jets in about seven seconds. _(@paulbohm)_
- [Proclaim mouthpiece](https://shop.proclaimhealth.com/products/additional-personalized-mouthpiece) — Product page for the personalised mouthpiece.
- [Wide palate as a health signal](https://x.com/MagnusVigso/status/2083961118381256999) — Treats a naturally wide palate as a sign of a well-nourished childhood and a parasympathetic state. _(@MagnusVigso)_
- [Chewing gum against oral HPV](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41656354/) — Ex-vivo study: lectin and antimicrobial-peptide gum aggregated up to 93% of HPV in saliva samples. Found via [Polymarket](https://x.com/Polymarket/status/2088726104424976834). _(@Polymarket)_

## Screening, hormones, allergy and sinus

- [Testosterone and depressive symptoms](https://x.com/wanted4mogging/status/2094567596490162625) — Faults antidepressant prescribing without testosterone screening; low testosterone and depression share low mood, energy and libido. _(@wanted4mogging)_
- [Endocrine Society testosterone guideline](https://www.endocrine.org/clinical-practice-guidelines/testosterone-therapy) — Diagnosis needs compatible symptoms plus consistently low, accurately measured morning testosterone.
- [H. pylori and thyroid medication](https://x.com/FarvingCo/status/2093783673623740918) — Links H. pylori to poor levothyroxine absorption, belly fat and low T3, with a supplement "kill stack". _(@FarvingCo)_
- [H. pylori treatment and levothyroxine](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21435090/) — 2011 study: treating H. pylori in patients not responding to high-dose levothyroxine sharply lowered TSH; 21% then became thyrotoxic.
- [Intralymphatic immunotherapy for allergies](https://x.com/ctbeiser/status/2085089503908413470) — Chris Beiser reports a 65–70% drop in dust-mite allergy symptoms after ILIT; another reply reports no benefit. _(@ctbeiser)_
- [ILIT randomised trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34088322/) — Double-blind trial with dust-mite, cat and dog extracts: little advantage over placebo, plus pain and some systemic reactions.
- [ILIT 11-person pilot](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28293934/) — Small pilot: symptoms improved, but two patients had anaphylaxis.
- [Household-bleach sinus rinse](https://x.com/EdaG889915/status/2052346899600998694) — Says adding diluted household bleach to a sinus rinse helped; [mixing follow-up](https://x.com/EdaG889915/status/2052350686268887512). _(@EdaG889915)_
- [Hypochlorous acid sinus rinse trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27853946/) — Small randomised study of device-made, low-strength hypochlorous acid in chronic rhinosinusitis; a different product from household bleach.
- [FDA on neti pots](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/rinsing-your-sinuses-neti-pots-safe) — Rinse with saline made from distilled, sterile or previously boiled water.
- [UK prostate-cancer numbers](https://news.sky.com/story/one-man-diagnosed-with-prostate-cancer-in-the-uk-every-eight-minutes-13575557) — Macmillan: over 68,000 UK diagnoses a year, one every eight minutes; about 600,000 men live with it. Shared in [this post](https://x.com/bertdernernert/status/2091055197405557210).
- [UK prostate screening recommendation](https://view-health-screening-recommendations.service.gov.uk/prostate-cancer/) — UK National Screening Committee: no population PSA screening; targeted screening for a high-risk BRCA2 group.

## Nutrition, weight and peptides

- [Thirty years of nutrition knowledge simplified](https://gilcarvalhomdphd.substack.com/p/30-years-of-nutrition-knowledge-simplified) — Gil Carvalho on nutrition basics, diets tied to lower chronic-disease risk, and spotting fads. Shared in [this post](https://x.com/NutritionMadeS3/status/2081319985096384566).
- [Water before meals and weight loss](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2859815/) — 12-week trial in 48 adults aged 55–75 on a calorie-cut diet: 500 mL water before meals added about 2 kg loss.
- [Water preloading post](https://x.com/SmartNotHard1/status/2083606184594919582) — Presents water before meals as a standalone fat-loss method with a 24–30% metabolism boost. _(@SmartNotHard1)_
- [Peptides versus supplements](https://x.com/minordissent/status/2094564810554052961) — Several peptides felt noticeable and most supplements placebo-like, though peptides are overhyped. See [[Peptide Reconstitution]]. _(@minordissent)_
- [Peptides Lab UK](https://peptideslabuk.com/) — UK peptide vendor.
- [Magnesium L-threonate trial](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2025.1729164/full) — Six weeks, 100 adults, 2 g/day compound: some cognitive gains, no objective sleep change; industry-funded. Found via a [post](https://x.com/oasishealthapp/status/2109312645098615190).

## Alcohol, tobacco and COVID

- [Drink less, smoke more?](https://x.com/algekalipso/status/2086859052181786862) — Uses convex-versus-concave marginal-risk curves to suggest some people should drink less and smoke more. _(@algekalipso)_
- [WHO on tobacco](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/tobacco) — Tobacco is harmful at every level of use and damages nearly every organ.
- [WHO on alcohol](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/alcohol) — Alcohol is carcinogenic and not risk-free.
- [Smoking and long COVID clip](https://x.com/Bitcoin_Teddy/status/2086392291862995011) — Says smokers get mild COVID, cannot get long COVID, and nicotine patches are harmless. _(@Bitcoin_Teddy)_
- [CDC on long COVID](https://www.cdc.gov/long-covid/about/index.html) — Anyone infected with COVID can develop long COVID.
- [CDC conditions raising COVID risk](https://www.cdc.gov/covid/hcp/clinical-care/underlying-conditions.html) — Clinical list of conditions tied to severe COVID, including smoking.

## Reproduction, sex differences and social psychology

- [Resemblance, psychopathy and paternal investment](https://x.com/houseofhereaftr/status/2103150600753950923) — 2020 study of 158 Romanian father–teen pairs: perceived resemblance tracked paternal investment, more strongly at higher psychopathy scores. _(@houseofhereaftr)_
  - Sources: [the paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.paid.2020.109879) · [quoted post](https://x.com/Psikobilim_/status/2102824105024332196) · [university abstract](https://www.research.unipd.it/handle/11577/3359563) · [methods excerpt](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0191886920300684) · images: [photo](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HS_kUviWsAAdr6Z.jpg), [facial-resemblance model](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HS66opaWUAAT8-F.jpg), [personality-similarity model](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HS660e4XkAA9fPR.jpg), [first page](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HS67LxgWsAAhV9K.jpg)
- [Follicular fluid attracts sperm selectively](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32517615/) — 2020 in-vitro study: different women's follicular fluid attracted different men's sperm, not systematically favouring the partner's. _(via @anishmoonka)_
- [Crude repost of the sperm study](https://x.com/mak_aura/status/2094854871212253473) — Turns the follicular-fluid result into a joke about women choosing wrong "molecularly". _(@mak_aura)_
- [Mental rotation and sex differences](https://x.com/antarte_/status/2093756333010952414) — Extrapolates from a water-bottle rotation question to sweeping views on women, systemizing and how IQ tests were built. _(@antarte_)_
- [Mental-rotation meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41566416/) — 2026 meta-analysis: an average male advantage on mental-rotation tests, with substantial heterogeneity.
- [Body-odour disgust and xenophobia](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10090875/) — Preregistered study in nine countries (6,836 people): body-odour disgust sensitivity tracked more negative attitudes toward fictitious refugee groups.
- [Body-odour study meme](https://x.com/Basedgoymemes/status/2093361464119566353) — Crude punchline built on the body-odour study. _(@Basedgoymemes)_
- [Birth-control harms clip](https://x.com/benwehrman/status/2093113877688906147) — Video on contraceptive harms, reposted with threatening rhetoric about its promoters; no study or transcript given. _(@benwehrman)_

## Neuroscience, genetics and medical research

- [Fruit-fly connectome repost](https://x.com/JIMMYEDGAR/status/2095579582879383756) — Shares the complete male fruit-fly nervous-system map, joking that an AI version "talked about rotting food". _(@JIMMYEDGAR)_
- [Mapping the male fruit-fly brain](https://research.google/blog/a-connectomics-milestone-mapping-the-complete-male-fruit-fly-brain/) — Google Research's map of more than 166,000 neurons across the male fruit fly's central nervous system.
- [Custom gene editing for CPS1 deficiency](https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa2504747) — NEJM: a personalised base editor let one infant tolerate more protein and need less medication over seven weeks. _(via @IterIntellectus)_
- [Streaming brain-to-voice neuroprosthesis](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-025-01905-6) — Nature Neuroscience, 2025: cortical recordings let one paralysed woman stream synthesised speech in her pre-injury voice. _(via @IterIntellectus)_
- [Cannabis and blood-DNA methylation](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12890-025-03634-9) — Cross-sectional study of 93 older adults: cannabis smokers showed thousands of methylation differences in ageing and cancer pathways. _(via @chamath)_
- [AI-assisted symptom investigation](https://x.com/amydeng_/status/2066928566961639716) — Amy Deng tracks symptoms and metrics, tests hypotheses with models, and leaves medical decisions to her physician. _(@amydeng_)_
