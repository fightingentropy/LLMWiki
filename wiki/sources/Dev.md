## July 2026 additions

### UI and product references

- Canvas UI — WebGL and experimental HTML-in-canvas component registry for React, Vue, Svelte, and vanilla TypeScript.
- Native Bloom — React Native pattern and component library; evaluate free material before paying for Pro.
- Buzz — open-source group chat for teams of people and agents.
- Slate — native iPhone voice journal using on-device transcription and local storage.

### Agent practice and technical writing

- `pstack` “show me your work” pattern — record what changed, why, the evidence, and result for important decisions rather than every generated line.
- ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English — controlled-language rules for clearer documentation and agent output.
- Inkling — open-weight multimodal model watchlist item; reassess against current models before adoption.
