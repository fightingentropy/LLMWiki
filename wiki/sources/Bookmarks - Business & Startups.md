# Bookmarks - Business & Startups

Posts, essays and interviews on building companies: founder principles, personal leverage, product taste, AI and robotics business models, venture, and company setup. Saved posts, described from their notes; the claims are the authors' own. The reusable ideas are drawn together in [[Business Strategy]].

## Founder principles and teams

- [Peter Thiel's Zero to One principles](https://x.com/FoundersPodcast/status/2078495325632008332) — David Senra's chapter-by-chapter synthesis: definite plans, monopoly, power laws, small first markets, recruiting and distribution. _(@FoundersPodcast)_
- [Marc Andreessen interview](https://x.com/davidsenra/status/2033181422081429882) — Long conversation on company building, venture design and technology history. _(@davidsenra)_
- [Naval's "Build a Team that Ships"](https://nav.al/build-a-team-that-ships) — Naval's 2012 AngelList model: small self-managing teams, weekly releases and individual accountability, with the trade-offs he saw.
- [Hiring people who are already rich](https://x.com/speedrun/status/1896979082036044155) — Ben Horowitz: normal pay may not keep a wealthy hire committed once the work gets hard. _(@speedrun)_
- [Grindslop versus output-driven intensity](https://x.com/WillManidis/status/2057094527236665598) — Will Manidis separates long hours that serve real output from cultures that make suffering the product. _(@WillManidis)_
- [Why corporate innovation labs become theatre](https://x.com/signulll/status/1902038636054618468) — Insulated senior staff with no customer pressure produce slide decks instead of shipped learning. _(@signulll)_
- [Team behind a major rebrand](https://x.com/alex_barashkov/status/2032423155558425004) — Staffing map across strategy, identity, motion, web development and delivery. _(@alex_barashkov)_
- [Change environment when thinking stalls](https://x.com/thecapitalistt0/status/2094714732624408908) — When stuck, work from an excellent hotel or similar setting to widen the options you can see. _(@thecapitalistt0)_

## Wealth, leverage and public positioning

- [How to Get Rich (without getting lucky)](https://x.com/naval/status/1002103360646823936) — Naval's 2018 thread: specific knowledge, accountability, ownership, leverage through code and media, and judgment. _(@naval)_
- [Build a distinctive public persona](https://x.com/hosseeb/status/1905354768387899815) — Haseeb: copying influencer voices makes you forgettable; amplify real quirks and domain perspective instead. _(@hosseeb)_
- [Industry-facing creators as influence](https://x.com/signulll/status/1905662475959185715) — A creator whose audience is the industry gains sway over operators without owning anything. _(@signulll)_
- [Reputation cost of sponsorships](https://x.com/TheCryptoNexus/status/2031975362423636144) — How to price an endorsement beyond the immediate fee. _(@TheCryptoNexus)_
- [Three portfolio strategy lessons](https://x.com/sergyproject/status/2031790304622162318) — Positioning, project selection and continual revision. _(@sergyproject)_

## Product judgment and taste

The design side is in [[Design & Aesthetics]].

- [Taste as resistance to market optimization](https://www.vogue.com/article/do-you-have-good-taste-its-more-important-than-ever) — Vogue piece: Tony Wang says durable taste comes from conviction that refuses to optimize for the average.
- [Taste Labs' design-judgment thesis](https://x.com/thaiscbranco_/status/2066912871649574945) — Treats design quality as infrastructure: training data, RL environments and verification tools for agents. _(@thaiscbranco_)_
- [Advantage moves to human insight](https://x.com/signulll/status/1902705566109114508) — As models make building cheap, the edge shifts to understanding people and spotting change early. _(@signulll)_
- [Why artificial scarcity fails after abundance](https://x.com/signulll/status/1906719652228501748) — Limits feel broken once users know abundance; curate it, or make constraints part of the mechanic. _(@signulll)_
- [Everything-trends chart](https://x.com/levelsio/status/2075356658738278873) — A trend thesis expressed as a living, explorable chart instead of an essay. _(@levelsio)_
- [Consumer information-market thesis](https://x.com/gabri3l/status/2026034684514431019) — Narrative charts, domain expertise and changing trader identity. _(@gabri3l)_
- [Hybrid stationery, café, plant, music and book shop](https://x.com/ben8128/status/2069236362470199582) — Ben Eidelson on a proposed all-in-one shop with mid-century and wabi-sabi styling. _(@ben8128)_
- [Instagram-advertised products turn out good](https://x.com/gametheorizing/status/2042121890467344789) — Heavily advertised products worked well; maybe recommendation algorithms changed seller incentives. _(@gametheorizing)_
- [Make the product legible to its buyer](https://x.com/rdominguezibar/status/2105946900855701825) — Enterprise buyers judge by perception: show their problem, credible evidence and the outcome. _(@rdominguezibar)_
