# Bookmarks - Personal & Life

Erlin's bookmarks for everyday life: posts on living more openly and spending attention well, frameworks for career and money decisions, outings in London and further afield, purchase shortlists for the home, and sign-in links for accounts and side projects. The strongest material is the practical frameworks (unstimulated walks, chosen friction, balancing across seasons, the safe-career repeated game, generative conversation) and the water-filter research that [[Water Filtration]] builds on. Aporia (@aporia9n) is the one recurring author; the rest are single posts from X plus product, travel and account pages.

## Openness, play and everyday experiments

These are motivational framings and personal practices, not promises about outcomes.

- [Flirt with life](https://x.com/adele_bloch/status/2094282784458358891) — Adele Bloch-Bauer's practical invitation to do things alone, talk to strangers, try hobbies, act a little silly and pursue a bucket list. Keep the openness and experimentation while respecting safety, consent, money and other people's boundaries. _(@adele_bloch)_
- [Treat life as a game with learnable mechanics](https://x.com/_masterymindset/status/2093949337617482081) — A young woman's video uses game language to frame setbacks, skill acquisition and experimentation. Useful as a motivational metaphor, not a claim that structural constraints or serious consequences are imaginary. _(@_masterymindset)_
- [Daily low-stakes social exposure practice](https://x.com/ruebytwo/status/1924272358623228396) — rue yi reports trying to make one stranger laugh each day at age 20, gradually becoming less anxious about approaching people and better at reading conversations. A personal exposure-style exercise for manageable discomfort, not a clinical protocol or a reason to ignore consent and boundaries. _(@ruebytwo)_

## Friendship, conversation and relationships

- [The quiet grief of adult friendship](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/blogs/civil-irony/the-quiet-grief-of-adult-friendship/) — Pranav Jain's Times of India essay, found through an excerpt Manosai highlighted. It argues that adult friendship erodes when institutions stop providing regular proximity, and survives through deliberately recurring, unoptimized presence. _(via @manosaie)_
- [Generative conversation versus default critique](https://x.com/signulll/status/1903184016272134385) — signüll argues that high-bandwidth collaborators catch tentative ideas, extend them and return something new, while immediate flaw-finding can stop exploration before an idea has enough shape to evaluate. The practical norm is to separate divergent exploration from convergent review. The author's claim that default critics are mostly careerist employees is unsupported and unnecessary. _(@signulll)_
- [Playful honesty over romantic strategy](https://x.com/sighswoon/status/1902248396234355005) — After experimenting with calculation and strategy in relationships, the author found playfulness, honesty and a willingness to look a little foolish more rewarding, even when the immediate outcome felt worse. One person's useful posture toward intimacy, not a universal rule or evidence that boundaries and judgment are unnecessary. See [[Dating_and_Relationships|Dating & Relationships]]. _(@sighswoon)_
- [Tolkien on becoming less cynical](https://x.com/DylanoA4/status/1898765886942507069) — The wording comes from J.R.R. Tolkien's Letter 250 to his son Michael, dated 1 November 1963. In context, Tolkien compares institutional failings in universities and the Church and argues that remembering one's own faults tempers cynicism about other people. Read it as a religious and personal reflection from _The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien_, not a context-free maxim. Shared by Dylan O'Sullivan. _(@DylanoA4)_

## Attention, time and agency

- [Boredom and unstimulated walks](https://x.com/bluewdrift/status/2078280757714256161) — A practical attention reset from aether: walk without audio or other inputs and give unstructured thought enough room to generate ideas and independent judgment. A [second post from Lessons](https://x.com/onlypreneurs/status/2086133832164708755) makes the same case for leaving quiet moments unfilled. Treat the promised return of ideas and clarity as a useful experiment, not a guaranteed neurological effect. _(@bluewdrift; @onlypreneurs)_
- [Use X for bounded information grabs](https://x.com/TheFlowHorse/status/2018807043873026397) — A simple attention-management workflow from [[TheFlowHorse]]: scan briefly, save deliberate follow-ups, then leave the feed. See [[Personal Knowledge Management]] for how saved links are handled. _(@TheFlowHorse)_
- [Urgency as respect for time](https://x.com/josbjohnson/status/2077012864380919989) — jo johnson reframes high-agency speed as reverence for finite time: answer while context is warm, finish small tasks before they accumulate, and move on ideas before hesitation turns into drift. _(@josbjohnson)_
- [Preserve chosen friction to retain everyday agency](https://x.com/aporia9n/status/2090604149595357399) — Outsourcing repetitive drudgery can save time, but eliminating every inconvenience can also remove small, frequent practice in navigating, cooking, improvising, asking for help and tolerating disrupted plans. Keep high-value, chosen difficulty and remove genuinely wasteful friction. Hardship is not automatically meaningful, and paid adventure does not substitute for respecting people who face involuntary constraints. The post quotes a [reflection by Sahil Bloom](https://x.com/SahilBloom/status/2090478737573711950). See [[Technology_Critique|Technology Critique]]. _(@aporia9n)_
- [Balance a life across seasons, not every day](https://x.com/aporia9n/status/2086213665573704091) — Ambitious projects, fitness, travel, relationships and family can require periods of deliberate over-allocation rather than keeping every metric green each day. Make the season explicit, preserve hard health and ethical constraints, define an exit or review date, and rebalance across years. Intensity is not permission to normalise preventable burnout or neglect. See [[Self_Improvement|Self-Improvement]]. _(@aporia9n)_

## Career and money decisions

- [The repeated game of leaving a safe career](https://x.com/moneyfetishist/status/2039528758936207757) — A substantive decision framework covering diminishing learning, golden handcuffs, status-quo bias and asymmetric regret. See [[Decision-Making]]. _(@moneyfetishist)_
- [A practical recession-readiness checklist](https://x.com/blknoiz06/status/1910372160012771395) — Ansem's list: keep the résumé and interview muscles current, maintain industry relationships before a layoff, cut discretionary spending toward three to six months of liquidity, and identify backup income and housing options. His suggestion to buy depressed assets carries risk and depends on personal circumstances; it is not a default step. _(@blknoiz06)_
- [Revolut balance-based transfers](https://x.com/ownsomeshares/status/2097765302599647728) — The author describes automatically pulling or sending money when an account crosses a chosen balance. An author report; check current app menus, eligible accounts, timing, limits and terms before relying on it. _(@ownsomeshares)_

## Outings, travel and places

- [Design a night out around what makes it worthwhile](https://x.com/worldofmaart/status/2104514885673443709) — Choose music and company you enjoy, make the earlier evening worthwhile, and decide a departure time that respects the following day. A practical planning heuristic. Skip the post's claim that water sobers you up and its forced early-wake advice. Also collected in [[Evergreen Lessons from X - September 2026]]. _(@worldofmaart)_
- [London route 11 front-seat tour](https://x.com/Pivot2Centre/status/2039928061395657074) — A simple local outing: ride the upper-deck front row of bus route 11 for a low-cost view through central London. _(@Pivot2Centre)_
- [London's 24/7 Corgi Cafe](https://x.com/Alex_Reinicke_/status/2085593767847813575) — Alex Reinicke's opening announcement for a 24-hour, laptop-friendly workspace in Shoreditch from the Corgi insurance startup. Despite the name, it is a branded cafe, not a dog cafe. Check current hours, access rules, menu and location before travelling. _(@Alex_Reinicke_)_
- [Syros Cats volunteering in Greece](https://syroscats.com/volunteering/) — A concrete version of the [Greek-island cat-care opportunity](https://x.com/Polymarket/status/2093342336957006028) posted by Polymarket. The charity offers accommodation, breakfast and utilities for about six hours of work a day, five days a week, with a minimum one-month stay. Volunteers cover travel and remaining food and share living facilities. As of 28 August 2026, 2026 applications were closed; applications for January–June 2027 open on 1 September and for July–December on 1 November. Structured animal-care work, not an unconditional free holiday; confirm current terms and availability before planning travel.
- [Eurostar Snap](https://snap.eurostar.com/uk-en) — Eurostar's discount-travel booking entry point (UK site).
- [Ascend travel concierge](https://www.joinascend.com/#ac-pricing) — A premium travel-concierge service; the link opens its pricing section. Compare the current annual price with realistic usage.
- [Odessia AI travel concierge](https://odessia.com/) — A lower-commitment alternative to Ascend: an AI travel-planning tool.
- [Howard de Walden Estate](https://www.hdwe.co.uk/) — The estate's website, kept as a property and local-area reference.

## Water filtration and air quality

Shortlist items and research leads, not purchases. [[Water Filtration]] sets out how to compare filters: start from the contaminants in the local supply, then check model-specific certification and running costs.

- [Fluoride-removal filter comparison lead](https://x.com/oasishealthapp/status/2093501078465904775) — Oasis Health lists dozens of countertop, under-sink, whole-house, bottle and reverse-osmosis products with claimed fluoride-removal percentages and unexplained scores. A shopping-research lead, not a verified ranking: no accessible methodology, laboratory reports, influent concentration, flow rate, filter age, capacity or common scoring rubric could be found. Before buying, check each exact model's current independent certification or performance data, replacement cost, wastewater, installation, maintenance, and the contaminants relevant to the local water supply. It complements the under-sink RO versus countertop question below. _(@oasishealthapp)_
- [Under-sink RO versus a countertop water filter](https://x.com/itsactuallytad/status/2092637354150510953) — The useful part is the purchasing question, not the post's gadget dismissal: compare the specific contaminants addressed, certified reduction claims, installation constraints, counter space, replacement-filter cost and maintenance. [Ninja's HydraSense launch information](https://newsroom.sharkninja.com/ninja-raises-the-standard-for-home-water-with-the-launch-of-ninja-hydrasense/) describes a no-plumbing countertop filter certified to NSF/ANSI 42, 53 and 401; it is not interchangeable with an RO system merely because prices are similar. The post's predictions that the screen will fail and that a complete installed RO setup costs the same are not established. _(@itsactuallytad)_
- [Ninja HydraSense retailer listing](https://bargainshopuk.co.uk/ninja-hydrasense-countertop-water-filtration-system-in-black/) — A UK retailer's listing for the black Ninja HydraSense countertop filter, part of an active water-filter comparison. For evidence, prefer the manufacturer's information (the launch page above) over the listing.
- [Hommix SanitRO countertop RO](https://www.hommix.co.uk/products/hommix-sanitro-3-in-1-luxury-countertop-reverse-osmosis-filtration-system-space-grey?variant=46684755329256&country=GB&currency=GBP) — Hommix's 3-in-1 countertop reverse-osmosis system in space grey. Compare certification, waste water, filters, cleaning and price.
- [Hommix Sparo countertop RO](https://www.hommix.co.uk/products/hommix-sparo-black-5-in-1-countertop-reverse-osmosis-filtration-system-uv?variant=56307986301307&country=GB&currency=GBP) — Hommix's black 5-in-1 countertop reverse-osmosis system with UV. Compare it with the SanitRO and EspressRO on the same points.
- [Hommix EspressRO countertop RO](https://www.hommix.co.uk/products/hommix-espressro-5-in-1-countertop-reverse-osmosis-filtration-system?variant=44065246609640&country=GB&currency=GBP) — Hommix's 5-in-1 countertop reverse-osmosis system. Compare it with the SanitRO and Sparo on the same points.
- [Klense Filter Showerhead](https://tryklense.com/en-gb/products/klense-filter-showerhead?shpxid=0c00fffe-5556-498c-bf5b-2ed1974e3631) — A filtered showerhead on the home-equipment shortlist. Its claims about reducing limescale, chlorine, metals and water use, and about skin and hair, are vendor claims; compare independent filtration evidence, replacement-filter cost and compatibility before buying.
- [Airthings View Plus](https://www.airthings.com/en-gb/view-plus) — Airthings' home air-quality product, on the shortlist until a purchase decision is made.
