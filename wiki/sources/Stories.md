## Dignity and endurance

**Taleb's father.** In *Antifragile*, [[Nassim Taleb]] tells how a militiaman insulted his father at a Lebanese road check. His father refused to comply, and was shot in the back as he drove away. The bullet stayed in his chest for life. Taleb's lesson: "dignity is worth nothing unless you earn it".

**David Cifaldi on Granite Peak.** On 20 July 2026 the emergency-room nurse slipped near the summit of Granite Peak, Montana, and fell onto a trekking pole that passed through his torso and missed his vital organs. He checked he could breathe, feel and was not bleeding heavily, alerted authorities by satellite, then walked ten miles down in about six and a half hours with his two friends. Surgeons removed the pole later. It is a story of calm, not a model for treating impalement.
