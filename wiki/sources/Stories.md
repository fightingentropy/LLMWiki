# Stories

A personal anecdote bank mixing hospitality work, travel, family, dating, psychedelic experiences, dark humor, and externally sourced stories. Many entries are prompts rather than finished narratives; preserve the first-person voice and avoid converting anecdotes into general claims.

## Recurring clusters

- Restaurant and hospitality incidents: customers, managers, lost bills, improvised service, and workplace banter.
- Travel stories from Albania, Germany, Iceland, Amalfi, and Mykonos.
- Family vignettes contrasting a quiet father with a highly talkative mother.
- Sexual and bodily humor, including reposted internet anecdotes.
- Agency and improvisation: the hotel-scarf story, “don't chase, attract,” and acting immediately when a situation opens.
- Dissatisfaction as a driver of achievement: the source's long counterpoint to jokes about successful but restless people.

## Dignity and endurance

[[Nassim_Taleb]]'s account of his father argues that dignity has content only when someone is willing to pay a price for it.

[[David Cifaldi]]'s Granite Peak self-rescue adds a modern account of calm assessment, teamwork, communication, and endurance after a severe injury. It is not medical or rescue guidance.
