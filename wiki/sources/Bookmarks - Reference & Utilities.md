# Bookmarks - Reference & Utilities

Practical reference links and small utilities saved from X and the browser: account security, privacy, web tools, dashboards, interactive explainers and a reading queue.

Saved posts and tools, described from their notes; the claims are the authors' own.

## Accounts, devices and home network

See [[Digital Security]].

- [Karpathy's digital-hygiene checklist](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/digital-hygiene/) — Password manager, hardware keys, random security answers, disk encryption, fewer IoT devices, encrypted messaging and tracker controls. _(@karpathy)_
- [iPhone theft protection](https://support.apple.com/120340) — Apple's Stolen Device Protection guide. The [original thread](https://x.com/jaxoncoder/status/2085158362162057638) adds shoulder-surfing and number-transfer-lock tips; follow Apple where they conflict. _(@jaxoncoder)_
- [Family account-security checklist](https://x.com/EzraJNewman/status/2085348692593115306) — Against automated phishing: password manager, passkeys, app-based MFA, updates, and verifying unexpected calls independently. _(@EzraJNewman)_
- [Proton Pass and Bitwarden versus 1Password](https://x.com/uwukko/status/2095452240639074312) — Cheaper, open-source alternatives with free tiers; compare features before switching. _(@uwukko)_
- [Disabling Apple Intelligence in iOS 27](https://x.com/mysk_co/status/2099460590649446549) — Use Screen Time restrictions to turn off Writing Assistance. _(@mysk_co)_
- [Samsung TV content recognition](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10010616/) — How to withdraw consent for "Viewing Information Services", which fingerprints what you watch. _(via @heynavtoor)_
- [usbliter8](https://ps.tc/pages/blog-usbliter8.html) — Hardware and security article on the ps.tc blog.
- [UniFi Express 7](https://x.com/Ubiquiti/status/2095153222990401894) — Ubiquiti's demo of a Wi-Fi 7, 10-gigabit whole-home mesh setup. _(@Ubiquiti)_

## Privacy and identity

- [VPNs are a technique, not a product](https://x.com/brivael/status/2066598980335890939) — Anyone can self-host WireGuard or OpenVPN, so banning providers does not ban VPNs. _(@brivael)_
- [nadanada](https://nadanada.me/) — Pay-as-you-go VPN and eSIM for privacy and travel.
- [Temp Number](https://temp-number.com/) — Disposable phone numbers; never use one for an account whose recovery matters.
- [KYC identity-misuse post](https://x.com/ImmortalXelf/status/2098383180646523065) — Offers identity details and images for bypassing KYC; kept only as a fraud-risk example. _(@ImmortalXelf)_
- [Reverse-image-search story](https://x.com/zoomerfemales/status/2097459473136664865) — A viral image whose subject stayed unidentified; provenance tools are not licence to identify private people. _(@zoomerfemales)_
- [ICO: right of access](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/right-of-access/what-is-the-right-of-access/) — A UK subject access request after an interview gets existing notes and scores, not new feedback; see the [employment SAR guide](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/employment/subject-access-request-q-and-as-for-employers/). Found via a [post](https://x.com/Shattubatu/status/2105956963993669855).
