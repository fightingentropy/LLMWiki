## Key takeaways

The September utilities include password-manager comparisons, network products, search directories, image compression, file transfer, and reading queues.

- Proton Pass, Bitwarden, and 1Password are comparison leads; the source does not establish complete feature parity.
- Apple Intelligence settings and media add-ons are version-sensitive references, not instructions verified against the user's devices.
- The KYC-misuse post is retained only by link and a privacy-risk summary. Exposed identity details were excluded from the Brain source and are not introduced here.
- Reverse-image search is a provenance tool, not permission to identify or surveil a private person.
- The unidentified free tool remains unresolved. Temporary numbers and privacy-service claims need scrutiny before use for sensitive accounts.
- Reading-queue entries remain sources to read; this page does not pretend their linked articles have already been analysed.

The following annotations are reproduced from the curated Brain notes. Dates record retention or revision, not independent verification. A revised annotation supersedes the earlier annotation for the same bookmark. Links, uncertainty, and missing-context labels are preserved.

- [Proton Pass and Bitwarden as 1Password alternatives](https://x.com/uwukko/status/2095452240639074312) — A comparison lead emphasizing free tiers, open-source clients, and lower prices. Do not assume complete feature parity: compare family sharing, passkeys, emergency access, SSH keys, platform integration, export, recovery, audits, and support against the exact current plans before migrating. _(wukko (@uwukko); retained 2026-09-05)_

- [UniFi Express 7 whole-home Wi-Fi reference](https://x.com/Ubiquiti/status/2095153222990401894) — Ubiquiti's product demonstration combines Wi-Fi 7, 10-gigabit networking, mesh placement, and controller features. Preserve it as a home-network product lead; validate independent radio performance, wired backhaul needs, coverage geometry, thermal behavior, support, and total hardware cost before buying. _(Ubiquiti (@Ubiquiti); retained 2026-09-05)_

- [PlayTorrio V3 multi-media application](https://x.com/leviackermanft/status/2094236227117453676) — A claimed open-source, cross-platform interface for movies, manga, audiobooks, music, torrents, video-on-demand, and Stremio. Treat third-party manifests, media providers, binaries, and torrent sources as untrusted until code, permissions, signatures, licences, and local copyright rules are checked. _(levi (@leviackermanft); retained 2026-09-05)_

- [PlayTorrio Stremio add-on manifests](https://x.com/leviackermanft/status/2094240889787060650) — Direct configuration references accompanying the V3 announcement. Keep them with the parent tool rather than as an endorsement; availability, safety, legality, and compatibility can change independently for every add-on. _(levi (@leviackermanft); retained 2026-09-05)_

- [Recognize carmine and cochineal on ingredient labels](https://x.com/TipOfTheBanana/status/2100362346145501450) — Carmine and cochineal extract are colourants derived from cochineal insects. US rules require their specific names on food ingredient lists; FDA explains this exception partly through possible allergic reactions. Useful label literacy for dietary choices, without the post's insect-count, lobbying-history or blanket health claims. References: [21 CFR 73.100: identity and food labelling](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-73/subpart-A/section-73.100); [FDA consumer colour-additive guidance](https://www.fda.gov/food/color-additives-information-consumers/color-additives-questions-and-answers-consumers). _(@TipOfTheBanana; evergreen synthesis retained 2026-09-30)_

- [Molasses is central to brown sugar's character](https://x.com/TipOfTheBanana/status/2103804906356224076) — Molasses content distinguishes brown sugar's flavour and colour. Manufacturing processes vary, so do not assume every brown sugar is simply white sugar recoloured in the same way. Keep this as cooking and ingredient knowledge; the post's fixed percentages, calorie comparison and health framing are omitted. References: [Domino: brown sugar and molasses](https://www.dominosugar.com/brown-sugar). _(@TipOfTheBanana; evergreen synthesis retained 2026-09-30)_
