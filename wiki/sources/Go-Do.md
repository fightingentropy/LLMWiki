# Go Do

[[Go Do]] is Erlin's running bucket list: things to try, places to go, and content to study. Each line is an intention, not a record of something done.

## Why Japan

The note credits a list to _@AlpacaAurelius_ on X, headed "Japan gets it:". The items, as listed:

- forest bathing
- wagyu
- ikigai
- wabi sabi
- kaizen: continuous improvement
- tea ceremonies
- kimonos
- Omotenashi: hospitality and selflessness
- tatami mats
- raw food
- kintsugi
- onsen (hot springs)

The note closes with "cannot wait to visit". The list reads as one package of craft, continuous improvement, hospitality and impermanence rather than separate curiosities. Each concept is expanded in [[Japanese_Philosophy|Japanese Philosophy & Cultural Concepts]].

## Themes

- **Imperfect action** — the one idea the list asks to study closely, from the Hormozi and Williamson episode. See [[Self_Improvement|Self-Improvement]].
- **Japan as an aesthetic and ethical reference point** — craft, continuous improvement, hospitality, impermanence. See [[Japanese_Philosophy|Japanese Philosophy & Cultural Concepts]].
- **Experience over optimisation** — the items are physical, sensory and travelled-to. This fits the "do epic shit" posture in [[Philosophy_and_Meaning|Philosophy & Meaning]].
