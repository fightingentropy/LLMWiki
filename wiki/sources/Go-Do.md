# Go Do

A running bucket list of things to try, places to go and one thing to study. Each line is an intention, not a record of something done. The thread through it is experience over optimisation: physical, sensory, travelled-to.

[Read the original note](/source/0d14742fb7fb9759ff05).

## Things to try

- A Japanese pressure-points massage.
- "Learn some dancing from YouTube at home. Try to learn shuffling".
- The Gibson, a cocktail bar in London.

## To study

The Chris Williamson episode with Alex Hormozi, to "Watch and analyse", above all Hormozi's point on ["Imperfect action"](https://youtu.be/Gk8EGWoGnEQ?t=3590). More moments from the same episode are saved in [[Youtube]]; the hosts are in [[Podcast Hosts]].

## Travel shortlist

"Chile (Patagonia, Atacama), Tanzania (Serengeti, Zanzibar) or Japan (whole country)".

## Why Japan

Under Japan sits a list credited to _@AlpacaAurelius_, headed "Japan gets it:": forest bathing, wagyu, ikigai, wabi sabi, kaizen ("continuous improvement"), tea ceremonies, kimonos, omotenashi ("hospitality and selflessness"), tatami mats, raw food, kintsugi and onsen. It ends: "cannot wait to visit". Read together, it is one package of craft, steady improvement, hospitality and impermanence; the concepts are explained in [[Philosophy & Meaning]].
