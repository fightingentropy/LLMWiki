# Health Evidence Review - September 2026

This review corrects recurring claims in [[Body]], [[Medical]], and [[Peptides]]. The full dated research record, including every official link, is attached below.

## Checked corrections

- Ordinary fluoride toothpaste remains part of [NHS dental guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/healthy-teeth-and-gums/how-to-keep-your-teeth-clean/). The thyroid-suppression rationale in the saved note is unsupported for normal toothpaste use.
- Sunlight is not a required reagent for dopamine synthesis; see the [NCBI biochemical pathway](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pathway/BioCyc%3AHUMAN_PWY66-301). Light and circadian timing are a separate question.
- The push-up result was an observational firefighter cohort, not an exercise prescription that lowers anyone's risk by 96%. [[Fitness]] preserves the study's scope.
- The [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-alerts-and-statements/fdas-concerns-unapproved-glp-1-drugs-used-weight-loss) still identifies retatrutide as unapproved in the US. A preservative and dilution calculation do not validate research-vial dosing or stability.

## Still unresolved

The blueberry cure, cold-shower medication equivalence, magnesium stack and tongkat-ali/pomegranate protocol lack adequate evidence in the saved notes. They are leads for investigation, not established treatment guidance. See [[Supplements]], [[Body_Optimization]] and [[Health Evidence]].
