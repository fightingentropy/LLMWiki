# Markets - Articles

Erlin's saved reading list for markets and the thinking around them. The core is macro: a June 2028 crash scenario, the Citadel Securities rebuttal to it, and a handful of threads on what comes next. Around that sit trading and market-structure threads, crypto-cycle essays, and a long tail of pieces on careers, self-improvement, AI and culture. Recurring authors are Citrini, @systematicls, @spicyofc, @xydotdot and @thedankoe. Most entries are saved by title alone; the quotes the note carries are kept verbatim beside their links. When a saved argument is developed into a claim, run it through [[Trading Evidence and Review Framework]]: a scenario stays a scenario even when its numbers are precise.
