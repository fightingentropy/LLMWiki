# Markets - Articles

A reading list for markets and the thinking around them. The core is macro: Citrini's June 2028 crash scenario, the Citadel Securities rebuttal, and threads on what comes next. Around it sit pieces on trading, crypto cycles, careers, self-improvement and AI. Descriptions come from the titles; the arguments are the authors' own.

## Macro scenarios and what comes next

- [JUNE 2028 Market Scenario](https://x.com/Citrini7/status/2025653614430023864) — Citrini's hypothetical in which AI beats every expectation and markets crash anyway. _(@Citrini7)_

  > "JUNE 2028. The S&P is down 38% from its highs. Unemployment just printed 10.2%. Private credit is unraveling. Prime mortgages are cracking. AI didn’t disappoint. It exceeded every expectation. What happened?"

- [Citadel rebuttal to the June 2028 scenario](https://www.citadelsecurities.com/news-and-insights/2026-global-intelligence-crisis/) — Citadel Securities' reply to the Citrini scenario.
- [From "Adoption" to Annexation: The End State of Institutional Adoption](https://x.com/Melt_Dem/status/2025776665603772459) — On the end state of institutional adoption. _(@Melt_Dem)_
- [Global Macro Trading for Idiots: Part Three](https://www.citriniresearch.com/p/global-macro-trading-for-idiots-part-611) — Third part of Citrini's beginner series on global macro; see [[Macro Trading]].
- [A Framework for What Comes Next](https://x.com/radigancarter/status/2035073252134129757) — A framework for reading what comes next. _(@radigancarter)_
- [End Game Positions](https://x.com/michaelxbloch/status/2020238247977242727) — On end-game positioning. _(@michaelxbloch)_
- [Capital Allocation Is Dead](https://x.com/kwharrison13/status/2078513579461079246) — Argues that capital allocation is dead; a lead for [[Trading Philosophy]]. _(@kwharrison13)_
- [Why 99% Of People Are Getting Poorer](https://bitmund.substack.com/p/why-99-of-people-are-getting-poorer) — Bitmund essay on why most people are getting poorer.
- [The Doomer Is Extremely Mid-Curve](https://apewoodx.substack.com/p/the-doomer-is-extremely-mid-curve) — apewoodx essay arguing that the doomer view is a mid-curve take.
