# Bookmarks - Media & Inspiration

Erlin's saved media and inspiration links from X and Chrome: film, series and book recommendations; creative-coding, motion and 3D references; streaming front-ends, IPTV directories and download utilities; and a long run of memes, clips and image-only posts kept for amusement. The strongest material is the creative-technology work (Corentin Berndu's WebGL studies, the Houdini and After Effects case studies, InSpatio and OpenArt Worlds) and the short, specific film and TV recommendations. Recurring authors are Corentin Berndu (@CorentinBerndu), aescripts (@aescripts), Trail Cams (@Trail_Cams) and @LordMasterIvan. This is a reference and entertainment collection, not a body of evidence. Creative references connect to [[Design_and_Aesthetics|Design & Aesthetics]]; reusable humour patterns connect to [[Humor]].
