# Bookmarks - Media & Inspiration

Film, series and book picks; creative-coding, motion and 3D references; streaming, IPTV and download tools; and memes and clips kept for a laugh. The creative-technology work (Corentin Berndu's WebGL studies, the Houdini and After Effects case studies, InSpatio and OpenArt Worlds) is the strongest material; see [[Design & Aesthetics]].

Saved posts and sites, described from their notes; streaming and download sites change hands often, so treat them as untrusted.
