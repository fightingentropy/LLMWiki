# Quotes

A running quote file: aphorisms, passages from novels, film lines, trader one-liners and saved X posts, mostly in English with some Greek. The same few ideas return from different mouths: act before you feel ready, time is shorter than it looks, the worst sin is betraying yourself, and the story you tell is how the world meets you. Most authors have a line on [[People Quoted]].

[Read the original note](/source/9cfc754f783fc9d022a6) for every quote and link.

## Courage and action

Anxiety is a courage deficit, and action produces the information that thinking cannot.

> "Man has it all in his hands and it slips away out of sheer cowardice." — Dostoevsky

> "Anxiety is what happens when your brains are bigger than your balls. … The only way to demolish anxiety is to choke it out with ruthless action."

> "Brilliant thinking is rare, but courage is in even shorter supply than genius." — Peter Thiel

> "Action produces information. If you're unsure of what to do, just do anything, even if it's the wrong thing."

> "aim. fire. correct later."

## Time and mortality

Subjective time is front-loaded, and you will be forgotten. Remembering that is the tool, not the threat.

> "The trouble is that you think you have time." — Jack Kornfield

> "Remembering that you are going to die is the best way I know to avoid the trap of thinking you have something to lose. You are already naked." — Steve Jobs

> "Decline starts with the replacement of dreams with memories and ends with the replacement of memories with other memories." — [[Nassim Taleb]]

## Self-betrayal and becoming

Lying to yourself is the root failure; small daily choices compound into who you are.

> "Your worst sin is that you betrayed yourself for nothing." — Dostoevsky

> "Man cannot remake himself without suffering, for he is both the marble and the sculptor." — Alexis Carrel

> "Addiction is a progressive narrowing of the things that bring you pleasure. A good life is a progressive expansion of the things that bring you pleasure."

> "All addictions are a low level search for God." — Carl Jung

> "Good and evil both increase at compound interest." — C. S. Lewis, *Mere Christianity*

## Money, risk and trading

Concentration, optimism and respect for risk, mostly from traders.

> "I like putting all my eggs in one basket and then watching the basket very carefully." — [[Stanley Druckenmiller]]

> "The cynics get to be right and the optimists get to be rich."

> "risk is like energy, it cannot be destroyed, it can only be transferred"

> "Nobody is atheist at 50x leverage." — @heart_

> "I spend my entire career in intangibles, it's nice to have something tangible to hold on to." — @briand_armstrong ([clip](https://youtu.be/79CAKmqnRt8?t=8142))

## Love

Love as risk, as conversation, and as something that can drown what it waters.

> "You fall in love with the people who make you love the person you are when you're around them."

> "I believe sex isn't the most intimate thing, conversation is. If I give you my body, I can take it back. But if I give you my thoughts, you walk away with a real piece of me."

> "The way to love anything is to realise that it might be lost." — G. K. Chesterton

> "I killed a plant once because I gave it too much water. Lord, I worry that love is violence." — José Olivarez

> "My therapist once said 'People don't abandon the people they love, they abandon the people they're using' and that was all the closure i needed."

## Parents and home

Parents seen late, as tired young people doing their best.

> "Ask us about the colour of a mother's eyes, and we'd reply, 'They're not brown. They aren't green. They're neither blue nor gray. They're red.' … And that's how we're connected: through the hurt we inflict on one another." — Carsten Jensen, *We, The Drowned*

> "Sometimes we work so hard to give our kids what we didn't have, we forget to give them what we did."

> "At some point you become the age that your parents were in your childhood memories, only you don't feel like an adult, and you realize your parents were still just kids trying to figure it out too."

## Storytelling and attention

The story is the interface; the feed is a slot machine; making beats consuming.

> "storytelling is the ultimate compression algorithm for human attention. … if you want to accomplish anything meaningful, the story is the interface between it & the world."

> "Doomscrolling is gambling for revelation, pulling the lever of an infinite slot machine, persuaded that after a billion worthless posts, the next one will finally justify all the others." — @graveair

> "The most valuable real estate in the world is the graveyard. There lie millions of half-written books, ideas never launched, and talents never developed." — Derek Sivers
