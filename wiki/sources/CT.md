# Crypto Takes

"CT" is a running archive of crypto-twitter posts: trader threads, replies and one-liners, kept word for word with the handle that posted them. The voice is the trader's, jokes and copium included. The takes disagree with each other: crypto as ingenuity or as degradation, narratives invented after price or needed before it, sit on your hands or sacrifice your weekends. Trader profiles are on [[Traders Directory]].

[Read the original note](/source/a10026c64022c9158e9c) for every post, thread and link.

## How price moves

Price leads the story, leverage amplifies the move, and crashes alone tell you nothing.

> "The price of any commodity tends to gravitate toward the production cost." — Satoshi Nakamoto, via [[0xaporia]]

> "the simple truth is that cascades occur as a RESULT of prices going down within a levered environment. in other words a cascade is a secondary effect." — @fewseethis

> "If volume starts to increase during rally, not bullish. Means market is running into sellers, much ammo from buyers being spent."

> "crashes may come in the middle of a bull run, at the top, or at the bottom. That is right. There's no signal." — Alex Krüger

> "'The orderflow frontruns the newsflow' — Marcus Aurelius" — @HsakaTrades

## Crypto as a bet on degeneracy

The darkest thesis: crypto grows because people are desperate, lonely and want a casino.

> "you think crypto is a bet on human ingenuity, financial self sovereignty and the natural progression of civilization. i think crypto is a bet on degradation of society, failure of trust and trad economies, forced speculation, desperation and human greed — we are not the same" — @DegenSpartan

> "i'm long humans being desperate, greedy, degenerate, lonely, and trapped in the metaverse" — [[GCRClassic]]

> "It is simply the most engaging multi player money game" — [[Rewkang]]

> "Everyone died, but the machines did their jobs perfectly. A beautiful world." — [[cobie]]

## Take the money out

Paper gains belong to the market until you withdraw them.

> "If you make life changing money. Take it out of the market. Many of you will make life changing money, but few will keep it." — [[Pentosh1]]

> "The only money that is yours is what you take out into the real world. Making money is easy. Keeping money isn't." — [[Pentosh1]]

> "I have this rule that I stole from SalsaTekila, where any money that I withdraw from exchanges, I don't deposit back." — @LomahCrypto

## Sit still and trust the process

Trade less, judge decisions rather than outcomes, and ask whether you would take the position fresh today.

> "What Traders should be like: 'Sit on my ass, then a few times a year make massive bank then sit on my ass again'" — Lord Ashdrake. [[cobie]]: "took me like 8 years to learn this toh"

> "you act too quickly when you are trying to grow capital, and you act too slowly when you are trying to protect capital" — a line [[cobie]] says changed his life

> "If I didn't have a position, would I want the exposure I have now?" — [[CryptoCred]]

> "Navigating cycles requires switching between childlike idealism and sagely cynicism" — [[CryptoCred]]

> "Decouple Process from Outcome." — [[0xaporia]], on strategy hopping and losing streaks

## Tops, tourists and blow-ups

Late money, quitting normies and anonymous new founders mark the end of a cycle.

> "Low conviction money enters extremely late and offsets their lack of belief by bidding only 'new projects' so they can be 'early'" — [[cobie]]

> "Normies showing up isn't a top signal. When they start quitting their IRL jobs for crypto or starting funds that's when we need to worry" — @0xSisyphus

> "when the tide goes out, you see who is swimming naked" — [[GCRClassic]]. @insiliconot: "I always swim naked."

> "Likely a 46 year old man living in his 5th country and 6th ponzi" — [[GCRClassic]], on new accounts with 50k followers and no past

## Keep quiet, and why trade at all

Never flex, never talk crypto in real life, and know that trading is a boss of its own. Asked why they trade, traders answered with games, ego and "the lamentations of their women"; the archive's own answer is "Crypto: insane ROI, intellectually interesting".

> "NEVER FLEX. EVER. Hide what you have." — [[trading_axe]]

> "Trust no one and learn to keep your mouth shut." — @coinfessions

> "now you have a new boss that's the invisible preferences of a bunch of psychos … congrats on your freedom" — [[goodalexander]]

> "Best computer game ever. Pure PvP where the leaderboard is your P&L."

> "Some of you guys are allright. Don't ever trade again." — @insiliconot
