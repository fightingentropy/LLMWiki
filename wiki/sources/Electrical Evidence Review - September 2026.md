# Electrical Evidence Review - September 2026

This dated source review covers the corrections in [[Building Regulations]], [[Part P]], [[Electrical Safety]], [[RCD Protection]], [[BS 7671]] and [[GN3 Guidance Note 3]]. The attached research record contains the official links and their scope.

## Verified distinctions

- The IET edition checker gives the A4 transition date; the current GN3 listing is tenth edition, not fifth.
- Part P is a statutory requirement. Approved Document P provides guidance. England's notification rules should not be described as uniform UK rules.
- A 30 mA RCD alone does not make an arbitrary concealed cable route acceptable.
- HSE does not require blanket annual PAT testing; maintenance and test intervals depend on risk.

## Evidence still missing

The original inspection/testing and PAT webinar transcripts cannot be found in the repository, its Git history or the current vault. Pages derived from them retain explicit source-gap records. This review does not authenticate their quoted test limits, exam formats, chapter numbers or model forms.
