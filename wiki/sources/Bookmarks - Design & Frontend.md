# Bookmarks - Design & Frontend

A design and frontend reference library: interface polish, motion, components, typography, AI-assisted design, native UI, WebGL, Gaussian splats and inspiration galleries. Saved posts and tools, described from their notes; check licence and accessibility before borrowing anything. Wider design reading is in [[Design & Aesthetics]] and [[Design Resources]].
