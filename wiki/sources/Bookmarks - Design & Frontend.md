# Bookmarks - Design & Frontend

September update to **130 structured entries** in the Brain category (previous wiki snapshot: 112). This page preserves all **18 added or revised entries**; earlier material remains in the August source and [[X-Bookmarks]]. Counts describe note entries, not unique URLs or X posts.

## Key takeaways

The additions extend the design reference library with interaction examples, component directories, stationery and material references, and [[Compositor]].

- The Compositor launch and v1.1 update are one product with two retained source posts; both links remain below.
- Wallet boarding-pass motion, navigation animation, and transitions are references for hierarchy and feedback. A polished clip does not establish accessibility or platform support.
- Component galleries, brand SVG collections, and generators remain leads whose licences and implementation quality need review.
- The iPhone keypad complaint is one user's UX observation, not proof of an operating-system change. OpenDesign performance claims are vendor reports.
- Bare links remain a reading queue; they have not been silently turned into product endorsements.
