# Body

Erlin's Body note is a scrapbook: fitness links, supplement and hormone claims, motivational passages pasted from X, and one long attack on self-improvement podcasters. Most lines are copied posts with the link hidden on a single word, and the author is often unnamed. Read it for the habits and the mindset; treat the health claims as attributed claims, not medical advice. What the evidence does and does not support is under [Evidence check](#evidence-check).

## Through-lines

- Repeated small decisions compound; willingness to be an awkward beginner helps sustain practice.
- Movement, skills and meaningful relationships recur as personal priorities.
- The critique of productivity personalities questions whether optimisation supports a fuller life. See [[Self-Improvement Culture Critique]].

[[Body_Optimization|Body Optimization]] connects the usable habits to [[Self-Improvement]], while [[Health Evidence]] separates claims from clinical findings.

## Evidence check

- **Blueberries.** The instant brain-fog cure is a source claim without clinical evidence attached.
- **Supplement stacks.** The magnesium, tongkat-ali/pomegranate and caffeine/theanine combinations were not validated as general treatment stacks. [[Supplements|Supplements & Biohacking]] records the remaining questions.
- **Vitamin D.** NHS guidance says adults should consider 10 micrograms of vitamin D daily in autumn and winter, and some groups year-round ([NHS vitamin D guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/vitamins-and-minerals/vitamin-d/)). That supports one specific recommendation, not the whole stack.
- **Cold showers.** The mood effects are anecdotes. The comparison to a pill is a subjective description.
- **Push-ups.** The statistic comes from an observational cohort: a 2019 study of 1,104 active male firefighters found greater baseline push-up capacity was associated with fewer cardiovascular events ([Yang et al., JAMA Network Open, 2019](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2724778)). The 96% figure compares groups; it is not a reduction caused by doing forty push-ups, and push-ups are not a universal cardiac screening test. Explained in [[Fitness]].
- **Protocols.** The neck, hormone, breathing and physique protocols are recorded as the note gives them, not endorsed.
- **Correction, 22 September 2026:** sunlight was incorrectly introduced as a necessary step in dopamine synthesis in an earlier synthesis of this note. The tyrosine-hydroxylase reaction uses tyrosine, oxygen and tetrahydrobiopterin; sunlight is not a reagent ([NCBI PubChem catecholamine pathway](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pathway/BioCyc%3AHUMAN_PWY66-301); [enzyme kinetics study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6117320/)). Light does affect circadian timing, which is a separate mechanism. See [[Medical|Medical & Health]] and [[Health Evidence Review - September 2026]].
