# Body

The original note combines fitness links, supplement claims, motivational passages and criticism of self-improvement culture. The complete source is accessible below with its wording unchanged.

## Durable themes

- Repeated small decisions compound; willingness to be an awkward beginner helps sustain practice.
- Movement, skills and meaningful relationships recur as personal priorities.
- The critique of productivity personalities questions whether optimisation supports a fuller life. See [[Self-Improvement Culture Critique]].

## Claims and corrections

- The instant blueberry brain-fog cure is a source claim without clinical evidence attached.
- The magnesium, tongkat-ali/pomegranate and caffeine/theanine combinations were not validated as general treatment stacks. [[Supplements]] records the remaining questions.
- Cold-shower mood effects are anecdotes. The source's comparison to a pill is a subjective description.
- The push-up statistic concerns an observational cohort, explained in [[Fitness]].
- Sunlight was incorrectly introduced as a necessary step in dopamine synthesis in an earlier synthesis. See [[Medical]] and [[Health Evidence Review - September 2026]].
- The specific neck, hormone, breathing and physique protocols are retained in the source, not endorsed by this summary.

[[Body_Optimization]] connects the usable habits to [[Self-Improvement]], while [[Health Evidence]] separates claims from clinical findings.
