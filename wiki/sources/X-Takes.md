# X Takes

A loose file of X posts, replies and jokes, mostly in other people's words. It keeps the contradictions: tech pessimism beside AI optimism, self-improvement beside mockery of it. Read it as what stuck, not as a set of positions.

[Read the original note](/source/99519dc2fb35793edcb4). It also holds racist, misogynistic and transphobic posts; they are rhetoric, not evidence, and are left out here.

## Place shapes you

Where you live, and who you spend time with, decides who you become.

- "You're not just the average of the 5 people closest to you. You're the average of the 5 places, the 5 routines, and the 5 inputs you're exposed to most." And: "Every space you occupy is voting on who you become." _(@tishray)_
- "Japan is doing fine. People are happy. Turns out chasing economic growth is horseshit." _(@DeLicious_Tacos_)_
- "Convinced only way to succeed is to separate entirely. 10-20 people you really like and hangout with regularly. Everyone else you see only 2-4x a year" _(@callicrates_)_

## Technology and attention

Technology adds capability and takes dignity and focus.

- "Never, never take these computer people at face value." Their machines make life "a little bit more undignified every year". "Their bag is secured by your future head injuries" _(@laserboat999)_
- "wake up and go directly to work. Do not check anything" — checking the world first "loads my RAM with a ton of distractors". _(Andrej Karpathy)_
- "luxuries will just become more expensive. only the sons and daughters of AGI companies will be able to afford a timeshare of the proxima centauri system" _(@tszzl)_
- "the AIs like writing react more than I do, and I like drinking beer and jumping into lakes more than the AIs" _(@yacineMTB)_

## Work and agency

Agency is the trait that matters; lacking it leaves you stuck between founder and employee.

- "the worst position to be in is lacking both the agency to be a founder and the tolerance to be an employee." _(@keshavchan)_
- "Sometimes you'll see a guy working at a supermarket, cashier or whatever who seemed genetically destined for great things & you can tell he walks around very haunted by what should have been." _(@Casagrandezz)_
- "My toxic trait is that I don't feel any sense of accomplishment after achieving something. Just a mild sense of relief that it's done." _(@quesadaaa_)_
- From a trader who has worked from home for over a decade: "Get dressed." "Something about putting real clothes on in the morning signals your brain that the day is serious." _(@TheFlowHorse)_

## Optimisation culture

The mockery of life-hacking: routines in place of family, discipline in place of living.

- Ferriss, Huberman and Fridman as "cult leaders of sterile optimisation" whose mantra is "hack your life — not live your life": "dopamine schedules, journaling routines, five-hour morning rituals — but no sons, no daughters, no wives, no lineage." _(@xirtman)_
- "I am incredibly grateful that there is a population of young men who have decided to go to bed early and take methamphetamine and forego sexual relations entirely just to prop up my 401k. Keep it up guys."

## Regret and meaning

- "Never entertain the thought that you might have ruined your life." "The seemingly detrimental L may be a necessary bridge to long term success. The seemingly massive W may be setting the stage for another harsh lesson."
- "reality distortion field is essential." "The trick isn't escaping the illusion-it's choosing one that doesn't destroy you." _(@signulll)_
- "Ingratitude is the most potent information about someone's future. I've never see an ingrate who did'nt end up a bitter *loser*." _(@nntaleb)_
- "You need to be getting weirder." "You're still going to be replaced. But at least for one brief and fleeting moment, you'll be more interesting." _(@CWingUexkull)_

## Therapy and men

- "Saying "Ball up top" when your world is crashing down is exactly why men don't need therapy. A therapist would tell me to cry smh."
- "I listened to one episode of "call her daddy" and no joke actually immediately invested in some Pharma companies working on new classes of antidepressants."
- "subtext is just text now because nobody trusts the audience any more for some reason" _(@arod_twit)_

## Love and dating

Attraction runs on chance and proximity; the poster who saved "ur coworker is not Hot they're just within 10 ft of u 40 hrs/week" named it proximity infatuation.

- "I genuinely believe love happens under precise and fleeting conditions, in moments of chance. You only get a few opportunities in life to capture it, and if you miss them, they're gone"
- "Taking a girl out to dinner on the first dates was only possible under the zero interest rate policy." "Now that the funny money's gone, be prepared for 3-5 years of coffee dates" _(@aniiyengar)_
- On fearing you could not satisfy Dua Lipa: "What if she subsequently wrote an English-Albanian disco hit about how bad you were? High risk play".
- A reply on a cold German girlfriend: "We have sex now. Sextime is over. Auf wiedersehen."
