# X Takes

A loose archive of X posts, replies, jokes and screenshots that Erlin saved, mostly in other people's words. Unlike the curated [[X-Bookmarks]] collection, it keeps the rough edges and the contradictions: technology pessimism beside agent optimism, self-improvement beside mockery of optimisation culture, intimacy beside hostile gender rhetoric, and social observation beside unsupported group claims. Read it as a record of what was funny or sticky online, not as a set of positions. Handles are given where the note gives them.

## Recurring ideas

- Place and routine shape identity; see [[Culture and Environment]].
- Technology can increase capability while degrading attention, dignity and ordinary social life; see [[Technology Critique]].
- Sterile optimisation can become a substitute for family, duty and lived experience; see [[Self-Improvement Culture Critique]].
- High agency, immediate action and choosing your environment recur as practical ideals.
- Proximity manufactures attraction; see [[Proximity Infatuation]].
- Status, gender, work, therapy, dating and crypto are repeatedly compressed into abrasive jokes; see [[Humor]].

## Place, environment and society

- **Places rewire you** _(@tishray)_. Every country has an energy that changes you: people move to Japan and become minimal, to Mexico and their relationship with time softens, to New York and they cannot sit still. "You're not just the average of the 5 people closest to you. You're the average of the 5 places, the 5 routines, and the 5 inputs you're exposed to most." And: "Every space you occupy is voting on who you become." The post calls choosing where you live one of the most important decisions you will make, more important than a job title, "Because the place shapes the plan."
- **Japan is not lonely.** A New Yorker who had read the loneliness and hikikomori books found the opposite in Japan: subways full of kids and teens moving in big groups, laughing, going places together, which you rarely see in NYC. The post quotes _@DeLicious_Tacos_ (31 May 2025): "Japan is doing fine. People are happy. Turns out chasing economic growth is horseshit." The note saves [a post by @kangminlee](https://x.com/kangminlee/status/1928304128926568735) directly below it.
- **London.** "I walked through central London last night and realised something that made me genuinely sad: The city I once loved—vibrant, safe, recognisably English—is gone." ([Here's what I saw](https://x.com/CF_Farrow/status/1933053157971525731), _@CF_Farrow_.) A claim about a city's character from one walk.
- **Air conditioning.** "My [lasting](https://x.com/levelsio/status/1940877091601522768) legacy will be getting AC installed in every last building in Europe reviving their eco brain rotted minds back to high IQ and ambition" _(@levelsio)_.
- **Pick your people.** "Average people are completely cooked at this point. Can't even have prolonged exposure to them anymore without frying braincells. Convinced only way to succeed is to separate entirely. 10-20 people you really like and hangout with regularly. Everyone else you see only 2-4x a year" _(@callicrates_)_.
- **Spatial awareness.** "Noticing a steady increase in people who are seemingly unaware of their existence in three dimensional space. Just listlessly loitering in doorway and in front of things, shocked and startled when someone says 'excuse me'" _(@GathererSkull)_.
- **High trust society.** "A relic from days long gone, / Nostalgic".
- **Monarchy.** A reply (to @annastewartenn and @sullydish) calls monarchy "so weird and cult like" and says this is why Americans broke away from those traditions. The answer to it quotes C. S. Lewis: where men cannot honour a king they honour millionaires, athletes, film stars, even famous prostitutes or gangsters, because spiritual nature "will be served; deny it food and it will gobble poison."
