# Live X Bookmark Inventory - 30 September 2026

This is the complete list of the 70 posts bookmarked on Erlin's X account, @entropyholdings, on 30 September 2026, read to its end through the Bird CLI. For each post the record keeps the post ID, the author, the link and the publication time. It does not keep post text or media, so it is a list of what was saved, not a content backup. It leaves out alleged identities of private people and sexual allegations.

The posts are grouped by theme below. The number before each link is its position in the list and matches the item numbers in [[X Bookmark Review - 30 September 2026]], which gives a recommendation for every post. The 15 posts that became [[Evergreen Lessons from X - September 2026]] are marked "Evergreen".

## What the record holds

- 70 records and 70 unique post IDs. The list was read to its end; the count describes the account at that moment and could change afterwards.
- Each entry has the post ID, author, link and publication timestamp. Publication dates are not the dates the posts were saved.
- 68 of the posts were published between 14 and 30 September 2026. The two older ones are the inner-work essay (8 August 2025) and the microplastics-filter post (2 April 2026).
- For the review, all available post text and quoted text was read, and selected screenshots and author threads were inspected. Videos and most attachments were not reviewed.

## What happened to the bookmarks

- [[X Bookmark Review - 30 September 2026]] records a recommendation and its evidence limits for every post. [[X-Bookmarks]] describes the separately maintained canonical archive; the review did not modify it. [[Personal Knowledge Management]] explains why keeping a source is not the same as endorsing it.
- Erlin then asked to keep only evergreen knowledge and clear the list. The 15 posts marked "Evergreen" above became the 14 entries in [[Evergreen Lessons from X - September 2026]].
- All 70 bookmarks were then removed from @entropyholdings, and every Bird removal reported success. Bird's after-reads could not refresh X's client bundles, so the X web interface was the independent final check: freshly loaded and reloaded with Bookmarks selected, it showed its empty state at `2026-09-30T21:51:47.209378+00:00`. The removal receipt is in [[X Bookmark Cleanup - 30 September 2026]].
- This 70-post list now stands as a historical audit. What was kept lives in [[Evergreen Lessons from X - September 2026]].
