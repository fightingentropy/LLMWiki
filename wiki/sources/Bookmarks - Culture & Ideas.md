# Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas

This category holds the X bookmarks and articles Erlin keeps on culture, ideas and argument: how people reason, how status and attention work, how platforms shape behaviour, and how political, criminal and demographic claims get made online. The strongest material is the reasoning heuristics (Taleb on group statistics, Perell on anecdotes versus aggregates, threadguy on consensus) and the sourced corrections that sit under the more inflammatory posts. Recurring voices are Nassim Taleb, signull, threadguy, David Perell, Boring_Business and Aporia. Many entries are screenshots, clips or insinuations; the note on each says what the post claims and what it would take to treat it as fact. Screenshot-only discussions and unidentified essays need their original context before they are quoted. Each item can also be opened by title or post ID in [Bookmarks](/bookmarks).

## Reasoning and evidence

Heuristics for telling argument from evidence. These are the entries most worth rereading; see [[Decision-Making]] and [[Evergreen Lessons from X - September 2026]].

- [Persuasiveness is not evidence](https://x.com/ArtemisConsort/status/2104638750022402356) — A fluent argument can support incompatible positions. Ask what observation would distinguish them, what would falsify the claim, and whether contrary evidence changes the conclusion. A reasoning heuristic, not a measured claim about personality or intelligence. _(@ArtemisConsort)_
- [When anecdotes and aggregate data disagree](https://x.com/david_perell/status/1927958351025164788) — A diagnostic principle: a vivid counterexample can reveal that an aggregate metric is measuring the wrong thing. _(@david_perell)_
- [Statistics describe groups without determining individuals](https://x.com/nntaleb/status/2091287066520805533) — Taleb's warning that a population average does not fix any one person's outcome, and that within-group variance can dominate a selection decision. His claim that statistics "don't work" is rhetoric: relevant group evidence can update an individual prior, but sound judgment also uses direct individual evidence, measurement quality, selection effects, uncertainty, and the cost of errors. See [[Nassim Taleb]]. _(@nntaleb)_
- [Why consumer ancestry percentages are not independent](https://x.com/nntaleb/status/2068778914751693238) — Taleb argues that national and regional ancestry categories overlap, so percentage labels depend on the reference populations chosen. A useful methodological caution; the PCA summary is simplified. _(@nntaleb)_
- [Decision-making with EV, Bayes and Kelly](https://x.com/polydao/status/2030404732817187273) — A reusable framework for treating career, investment, relationship, and risk choices probabilistically. _(@polydao)_
- [Curiosity and corrective feedback](https://x.com/0xaporia/status/2032146903945666731) — A nuanced reflection on autodidacts, feedback, and intellectual humility. _(@0xaporia)_
- [Markets as training in first-principles thinking](https://x.com/0xaporia/status/1896237754985414745) — Aporia argues that investing can train self-accountability, explicit assumptions, hypothesis revision, problem definition, systems thinking, and attention to trade-offs, and that those habits transfer to other domains. A reflective essay, not evidence that investors become wiser or more philanthropic: keep the practices, discard the universal claims. See [[0xaporia]]. _(@0xaporia)_
- [Intelligence amplifies core beliefs](https://x.com/minordissent/status/2017850291048124827) — A compact mental model for how reasoning ability can strengthen either self-defeating narratives or adaptive problem-solving. _(@minordissent)_
- [LLMs as consensus amplifiers rather than truth generators](https://x.com/i/article/1922079593638526976) — threadguy's essay models information as a chain from primary events through outlets and influencers into language models, and argues that convenience can make consensus feel like verified truth. The actionable lesson is to trace important claims back to evidence; its admitted invented concentration figures and swagger are not evidence. _(@notthreadguy)_
- [Critical mass for idea production](https://x.com/xenoaesthetics/status/2030372153489555962) — A model of people who generate multiple new ideas from each idea they encounter. _(@xenoaesthetics)_
- [A small excess can spoil the whole experience](https://x.com/david_perell/status/1927949324505194675) — An editing and product-design principle: stop before good material becomes tiresome. _(@david_perell)_

## Status, character and ambition

Who gets admired, which status games are worth playing, and what action looks like from the outside.

- [Substance rarely looks like its aesthetic stereotype](https://x.com/GenghisReborn/status/2094740193140212051) — Worthwhile people are often "sleeper agents", while those who perfectly wear a subculture's uniform may be performing it. An anti-signalling heuristic; appearance is weak evidence in both directions. _(@GenghisReborn)_
- [Character as a better criterion for role models](https://x.com/nntaleb/status/2092574217371898028) — Taleb revisits a 2022 criticism of influencer role models and contrasts celebrity appeal with nobility, strength of character, and chivalric conduct. The question worth keeping: what habits and behaviour does admiration actually teach? His insults and mental-health labels for named public figures are rhetorical judgments, not clinical findings or evidence about a new legal case. _(@nntaleb)_
- [Status fragments into niches and pockets](https://x.com/CEOLandshark/status/2088742065819517111) — A single legible society-wide status hierarchy has weakened into overlapping communities, cities, professions, and online scenes. A useful social hypothesis: broad celebrity and institutional hierarchies still exist, and the degree of fragmentation varies with place, class, platform, and community. Saved via [this quotation](https://x.com/ThomBrady5/status/2088932587704037598). _(@CEOLandshark)_
- [Choose status games whose winning move improves you](https://x.com/Kpaxs/status/2091103346950013070) — Status competition may be hard to eliminate, but its incentives differ: lifting, shipping useful work, and helping specific people can build capacity as a side effect, while consumption-only games reward display without the same compounding return. A selection heuristic, not a claim that every pursuit must become competition. _(@Kpaxs)_
- [Action defeats imagined disqualification](https://x.com/asparagoid/status/2091127526705832158) — Ordinary, imperfect people routinely build businesses, improve physically, and form relationships, which undermines the belief that exceptional credentials or confidence must precede action. Keep the courage-and-repetition lesson; discard its insults, invented IQ judgments, and the metaphysical claim that reality was constructed to humiliate the inactive. _(@asparagoid)_
- [America as permission to fail wildly](https://x.com/teachrobotslove/status/2073502768698986824) — A cultural portrait of experimentation: the freedom to pursue strange ambitions includes the freedom to fail quickly, extravagantly, and repeatedly. _(@teachrobotslove)_
- [Why billionaires struggle to stop](https://x.com/BoringBiz_/status/2069564475535946090) — Boring_Business argues that the traits required to become a billionaire can also make stopping work difficult after wealth removes the financial need. _(@BoringBiz_)_
- [San Francisco versus New York tech culture](https://x.com/jonwu_/status/2093463094156828880) — Jon Wu satirically contrasts New York's commitment to desire and desirability with a San Francisco tech culture willing to suppress sex appeal in order to focus on difficult problems. A later [counterpost](https://x.com/_johnsonator/status/2093745991182647766) calls the "tech hot girl renaissance" a sad retrospective rewrite of high-school status. Both are city-and-industry caricatures and competing status narratives, not sociology or evidence about either population. _(@jonwu_; @_johnsonator)_
- [Fatherhood, childlessness, and continuity](https://x.com/lichthauch/status/2012256648870457675) — A paired portrait: the exhausted parent embedded in continuity against the comfortable child-free intellectual increasingly detached from the future. _(@lichthauch)_

## Platforms, attention and design

How social products shape self-presentation, longing and taste. See [[Technology Critique]] and [[Social Commentary]].

- [Social platforms as identity theater](https://x.com/signulll/status/1901702724766367988) — Instagram rewards performances of an exceptional personal life, LinkedIn of career success, TikTok of charisma, and X of intellectual authority. A useful lens for platform incentives and one's own self-presentation; the claim that this consumes half of people's free time is rhetorical, not measured. _(@signulll)_
- [Hyperconnectivity removes the absence that creates longing](https://x.com/signulll/status/1902502840389743063) — Frictionless messaging, instant gratification, and permanent archives reduce the gaps in which missing someone or nostalgically reconstructing the past once developed. A hypothesis for social and product design (deliberate absence may preserve attention and meaning), not an established causal finding. _(@signulll)_
- [Media affinity versus design principles](https://x.com/ShimazuSystems/status/2031323051208368389) — A useful distinction when evaluating interfaces inspired by fictional media. _(@ShimazuSystems)_
- [AI imitation can commodify hard-won aesthetics](https://x.com/cpaik/status/1905019117326729546) — The 2025 wave of Ghibli-like generations shows how an aesthetic developed through decades of craft can become a low-friction content template. The tension between creative access and cultural flattening is a design and authorship question; the observation does not settle consent, copyright, attribution, or whether individual outputs are artistically valuable. _(@cpaik)_
- [Is the iPhone Birth Control?](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35310) — A June 2026 NBER working paper uses AT&T's 2007–2011 iPhone exclusivity and coverage variation to estimate that early smartphone diffusion explains 33–52% of the decline in the US general fertility rate. A new identification claim relying on AT&T coverage as a proxy, not settled consensus; awaits replication and peer review. _(via @IterIntellectus)_
- [Hacker News "truth-bombs" screenshot](https://x.com/paraschopra/status/2100925357012738352) — Screenshot-led technology commentary whose argument depends on the pictured discussion. Community perspective; identify the original thread before quoting it as evidence. _(@paraschopra)_

## Surveillance, privacy and digital evidence

How online traces are collected and how they are used against people. See [[Digital Security]].

- [Ambient surveillance and behavioral biometrics](https://x.com/varien/status/2030116279206420892) — A compact map of lesser-known sensing and identification channels, worth researching from primary papers. _(@varien)_
- [Privacy critique of proposed UK child-device nudity blocking](https://x.com/mullvadnet/status/2064342870937509988) — The [8 June government announcement](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/new-plans-to-stop-children-taking-sharing-or-viewing-nude-images) asked Apple and Google to implement device-level nudity blocking for children within three months and threatened legislation if progress proved inadequate; adults could disable it through age assurance, and government said processing should remain on-device without data collection. Mullvad argues that device-level enforcement creates surveillance and user-control risks. As of 25 July 2026 this remained a proposal and implementation demand, not enacted scanning law. _(@mullvadnet)_
- [Digital history used in a courtroom](https://x.com/rstormsf/status/2101438718083203082) — Roman Storm describes prosecutors presenting detailed online history and framing benign searches negatively, and argues that a defendant can tell a competing account only by testifying. His firsthand perspective on surveillance and trial narrative, not a neutral summary of the evidence or law. _(@rstormsf)_
- [Christine Maxwell, Chiliad, and the FBI's Investigative Data Warehouse](https://www.eff.org/issues/foia/investigative-data-warehouse-report) — Christine Maxwell, Ghislaine Maxwell's sister, co-founded the search-software company Chiliad. EFF's records-based report says Chiliad was among the contractors that helped develop the FBI's Investigative Data Warehouse, which aggregated many investigative and intelligence datasets and supported link and pattern analysis. That substantiates a narrow historical company–FBI connection; it does not establish the [original bookmark's](https://x.com/mandyarthur/status/2083667607564267957) broader claim that the FBI and DNI have continuously used Chiliad to "data mine Americans since 9/11". _(@mandyarthur)_
- [Inferring defendants' ethnicity from public court lists](https://x.com/QuantMashine/status/2095855260711416188) — The author says they scraped daily XHIBIT court lists and inferred ethnicity from first names and surnames to challenge official crime reporting. A case study in unofficial public-data analysis, not reliable demographic evidence: names are noisy proxies, court appearances are not convictions, list coverage and duplicate handling matter, and the method raises privacy, bias, and validation questions. _(@QuantMashine)_

## Economics, wealth and work

Asset ownership versus labour, the British economy, and what work is for. See [[Economics]].

- [Asset inflation and the politics of have-nots](https://x.com/BoringBiz_/status/2017679999671619909) — A structural lens connecting currency debasement, unequal asset ownership, and populist demand for redistribution. _(@BoringBiz_)_
- [How To Get Rich in America](https://x.com/kylascan/status/2087970767304872295) — Kyla Scanlon examines an economy in which owning appreciating assets is rewarded more than labour, widening the generational divide, encouraging speculative "casino" behaviour, and reshaping attitudes toward work and politics. A dated essay whose figures and argument should be tested against wealth, income, housing, mobility, and cohort data; not proof that young people's future is uniformly bleak. Saved via [this post](https://x.com/zeroxkyle/status/2088071373373702237). _(@kylascan)_
- [Britain's weak return to education reflects a weak economy](https://ft.trib.al/RvpW1Vg) — The Financial Times argues that Britain's declining graduate wage premium is not simply a story of too many graduates: weak demand for skilled labour and long-running economic stagnation compress wages across skill levels. Its OECD PIAAC chart places the hourly wage of the lowest-literacy US group near the average British worker, but that is an aggregate, purchasing-power-adjusted comparison, not a prediction about an individual worker or proof that education has no value. Saved from [this post](https://x.com/an0n_Nic/status/2085707887884558733). _(@an0n_Nic)_
- [The hollowed-out UK high street](https://x.com/TheCriticalDri2/status/2085371797763998127) — The Critical Drinker describes a high street dominated by vape shops, nail salons, phone retailers, and barbers as an atmosphere of commercial decline and resignation. An attributed cultural observation to compare with vacancy, business-formation, rent, footfall, and sector data; the insinuation that quiet businesses are illegitimate is unsupported. _(@TheCriticalDri2)_
- [Bukele on dismantling a criminal economy](https://x.com/thatsKAIZEN/status/2085154066322079862) — Nayib Bukele says advisers warned that rapid suppression of gangs could hurt legitimate consumption because illicit income supported households and local spending, but that El Salvador accepted the short-term risk and the economy still grew. His political-economy argument and self-reported outcome, not proof of general competence or a causal evaluation; his security model also involves mass detention, suspended civil liberties, due-process concerns, and documented allegations of abuse. _(via @thatsKAIZEN)_
- [Nike's "End of Men" thesis](https://houseofstrauss.substack.com/p/nikes-end-of-men) — Ethan Strauss argues that Nike's cultural and marketing choices alienated parts of its historically male core audience. A provocative business-and-culture case, not established causation: separate brand symbolism from product cycles, channel inventory, pricing, competition, geography, management execution, and measured customer behaviour. Saved via [this post](https://x.com/notthreadguy/status/2089730151844127138). _(@notthreadguy)_
- [Cardano and the early mathematics of probability](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Cardan/) — Girolamo Cardano drew on gambling experience to study dice and probability in _Liber de Ludo Aleae_, probably completed around 1563 and published posthumously in 1663. This helped open probability theory, but the [original bookmark](https://x.com/betraidx/status/2090447521717858775) overstates the lineage: modern insurance does not simply run on one Cardano equation, the industry predates Peter Bernstein's 1996 history, and probability developed through many later contributors. _(@betraidx)_
- [Careers can also solve social isolation](https://x.com/allie__voss/status/1904892220559282299) — One reason paid work stays attractive is routine adult contact, especially where reduced neighbourhood and family social infrastructure would make full-time caregiving isolating. A lens for designing work, family, and community support, not demographic evidence about what all women want. _(@allie__voss)_
- [Academic job-market image](https://x.com/NC_Renic/status/2100978444511195452) — Image-led commentary on academic employment; humour and labour-market culture, not a statistical source. _(@NC_Renic)_
- [Thatcher's "other people's money" line in a public-ownership debate](https://x.com/anthonyboutall/status/2094695832171765761) — The familiar quotation is used against Andy Burnham's argument for public ownership. Political rhetoric and a prompt to examine financing, governance, pricing, externalities, and distribution; the slogan alone does not settle whether a particular public or private model performs better. _(@anthonyboutall)_

- [What the UN's "Replacement Migration" report actually is](https://www.un.org/development/desa/pd/content/replacement-migration) — The UN's 2000 report models how different migration levels would affect population decline, working-age population, and ageing in eight countries and two regions. - [CIA family cookbook as institutional folklore](https://x.com/cortadoedison/status/2094927731628589541) — An anecdote says former-CIA parents used an internal recipe book: food as an unusually human lens on institutional history. - [London Underground durability](https://x.com/PositivFuturist/status/2099763829504622907) — Appreciation of the network's engineering resilience despite heavy use and perceived neglect. The post ends with offensive commentary; the infrastructure observation stands apart from that language and its political framing. _(@PositivFuturist)_
- [Returning through a British airport](https://x.com/stefanroberts/status/2098694726945816958) — A compact observation about feeling both disgust and comfort when returning through a British airport. _(@stefanroberts)_

## Crime, race and contested claims

Most of these posts make criminal, demographic or historical claims from screenshots, clips or insinuation. They are listed with attribution because they show how a story becomes a narrative; a post is not a source for the claim it makes, and bookmarking does not turn it into fact. Where reporting, scholarship or the underlying study corrects the post, the correction is attached. See [[Online Extremism and Evidence]].

- [Bank of America executive killed in Times Square stabbing](https://x.com/TheognisOMegara/status/2094892546975584263) — A post about Erin Piacenti's death adds an ethnic and conspiratorial frame to a violent-crime report. Separate any verified event from the author's insinuation; identity, motive, employer connection, and criminal responsibility require authoritative reporting and court records. _(@TheognisOMegara)_
- [Las Vegas suspected biolab case and an overstated dismissal claim](https://x.com/catgirlprostate/status/2092948078055579817) — The bookmark frames two alleged Israeli-run US labs with COVID samples as a suppressed conspiracy and quotes a post claiming that all federal charges against Ori Solomon for manufacturing deadly pathogens were dropped. Contemporary local reporting ([FOX5 Las Vegas](https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/05/26/federal-gun-complaint-dropped-against-man-tied-las-vegas-bio-lab-case/), [KTNV](https://www.ktnv.com/news/crime/federal-charges-for-property-manager-connected-to-las-vegas-illegal-biolab-dropped)) says the dismissed federal complaint was a firearms case, dismissed without prejudice, while a Nevada hazardous-waste matter remained pending; it did not report a dismissed federal pathogen-manufacturing charge. A case study in how a real investigation becomes an ethnic and conspiratorial narrative; refresh the live court status before citing it. _(@catgirlprostate)_
- [Robert Trivers email to Jeffrey Epstein about transgender people](https://x.com/JayDyer/status/2093089535580131370) — The screenshot presents a December 2018 email attributed to evolutionary biologist Robert Trivers containing speculative and dehumanising discussion of transgender people. [Reporting on the released correspondence](https://www.them.us/story/jeffrey-epstein-robert-trivers-transgender-research-emails) supports that Trivers and Epstein exchanged such messages, while the [DOJ library](https://www.justice.gov/epstein) cautions that its search can be unreliable for some formats. An attributed historical lead; it does not by itself establish the surrounding reply's broader claim that Epstein secretly financed a movement, nor does association make the email's assertions scientifically valid. _(@JayDyer)_
- [Poverty framing around antisocial public behavior](https://x.com/kangminlee/status/2094225897943400457) — A subway video is used to mock explanations that connect poverty and disorder. An example of how a contextless clip becomes political evidence: one recording cannot establish the participants' circumstances, prevalence, causes, or the effect of poverty, policing, norms, intoxication, or mental health. _(@kangminlee)_
- [Cooking clip circulated as cultural mockery](https://x.com/TKDTJDTND/status/2091432649223241814) — A video of a cooking method, paired with a repost ridiculing the culture presumed to be behind it. The post gives too little context about the dish, setting, constraints, or technique to support its civilisational judgment; neither the setting nor the broader conclusion is verified. _(@TKDTJDTND)_
- [Nick Fuentes clip: provocation through slurs](https://x.com/AngloGroypr/status/2091607653231288387) — A short political-media clip contrasts religious out-group labels with an anti-Black slur for shock value.
