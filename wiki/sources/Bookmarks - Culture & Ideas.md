- [Inferring defendants' ethnicity from public court lists](https://x.com/QuantMashine/status/2095855260711416188) — The author says they scraped daily XHIBIT court lists and inferred ethnicity from first names and surnames to challenge official crime reporting. - [Seneca on scattered reading and information abundance](https://x.com/oldbooksguy/status/2094109372410741105) — The screenshot modernizes a real theme from Letter 2 to Lucilius: constant movement among many books and authors can prevent ideas from taking root. Preserve the durable depth-over-breadth principle without pretending Seneca anticipated smartphones or modern recommendation systems in their current form. _(The Cultural Tutor (@oldbooksguy); retained 2026-09-05)_

- [Poverty framing around antisocial public behavior](https://x.com/kangminlee/status/2094225897943400457) — A subway video is used to mock explanations that connect poverty and disorder. Keep it as an example of how a contextless clip becomes political evidence; one recording cannot establish the participants' circumstances, prevalence, causes, or the effect of poverty, policing, norms, intoxication, or mental health. _(Kangmin Lee (@kangminlee); retained 2026-09-05)_

- [Brothels and the decline-of-genius provocation](https://x.com/_theorycel/status/2093499827573215652) — The post claims that criminalizing prostitution deprived exceptional men of an outlet and thereby reduced great intellectual work. Preserve it as a provocative sexual-politics thesis, not history or causal evidence; it ignores exploitation, health, consent, family life, selection effects, and the many social and institutional determinants of achievement. _(@_theorycel; retained 2026-09-05)_

- [San Francisco versus New York tech culture](https://x.com/jonwu_/status/2093463094156828880) — Jon Wu satirically contrasts New York's commitment to desire and desirability with a San Francisco tech culture willing to suppress sex appeal in order to focus on difficult problems. Preserve both as city-and-industry caricatures and competing status narratives, not representative sociology or evidence about either population. _(Jon Wu (@jonwu_); @_johnsonator; retained 2026-08-29; updated 2026-09-05)_
