# Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas

Saved posts and articles on how people reason, how status and attention work, how platforms shape behaviour, and how political and criminal stories spread online. The reasoning heuristics are the part most worth rereading; recurring voices are Nassim Taleb, signull, threadguy, David Perell, Boring_Business and Aporia.

Saved posts described from their notes; the claims are the authors' own, and several are inflammatory.

## Reasoning and evidence

- [Persuasiveness is not evidence](https://x.com/ArtemisConsort/status/2104638750022402356) — A fluent argument can support opposite positions; ask what observation would separate them and what would falsify the claim. _(@ArtemisConsort)_
- [When anecdotes and aggregates disagree](https://x.com/david_perell/status/1927958351025164788) — A vivid counterexample can show that an aggregate metric is measuring the wrong thing. _(@david_perell)_
- [Statistics describe groups, not individuals](https://x.com/nntaleb/status/2091287066520805533) — [[Nassim Taleb]]: a population average does not fix one person's outcome, and within-group variance can dominate a selection decision. _(@nntaleb)_
- [Ancestry percentages are not independent](https://x.com/nntaleb/status/2068778914751693238) — National and regional ancestry categories overlap, so the percentages depend on which reference populations are chosen. _(@nntaleb)_
- [Decision-making with EV, Bayes and Kelly](https://x.com/polydao/status/2030404732817187273) — Framework for treating career, investment, relationship and risk choices probabilistically. _(@polydao)_
- [Curiosity and corrective feedback](https://x.com/0xaporia/status/2032146903945666731) — Reflection on autodidacts, feedback and intellectual humility. _(@0xaporia)_
- [Markets as training in first-principles thinking](https://x.com/0xaporia/status/1896237754985414745) — [[0xaporia]]: investing trains self-accountability, explicit assumptions, hypothesis revision and systems thinking, habits that transfer to other domains. _(@0xaporia)_
- [Intelligence amplifies core beliefs](https://x.com/minordissent/status/2017850291048124827) — Reasoning ability strengthens whichever story you already hold, self-defeating or adaptive. _(@minordissent)_
- [LLMs as consensus amplifiers](https://x.com/i/article/1922079593638526976) — threadguy's essay: information flows from events through outlets and influencers into models, so convenience makes consensus feel verified. _(@notthreadguy)_
- [Critical mass for idea production](https://x.com/xenoaesthetics/status/2030372153489555962) — Some people generate several new ideas from each idea they meet. _(@xenoaesthetics)_
- [A small excess spoils the whole](https://x.com/david_perell/status/1927949324505194675) — Editing and product principle: stop before good material becomes tiresome. _(@david_perell)_
