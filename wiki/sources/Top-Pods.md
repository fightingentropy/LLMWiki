# Top Pods

A short list of podcast and YouTube videos pinned as top picks. The note holds titles and links only, with no commentary. The list leans hard toward masculinity discourse and long-form celebrity conversation. For the wider video list, see [[Youtube]].

## Masculinity and the "war on men"

- [The performative Male epidemic](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w6PNOlTVFlM) — YouTube video that, by its title, names the "performative male" as a cultural type. Pairs with the sterile-optimization critique in [[Masculinity_and_Men|Masculinity & Men]] and the secular-priesthood frame on [[Andrew_Huberman|Andrew Huberman]], [[Lex_Fridman|Lex Fridman]] and [[Tim_Ferriss|Tim Ferriss]].
- [The war on Men](https://youtu.be/2nDyZH0-uz0) — YouTube video; the title frames men as targets of systemic cultural pressure, not beneficiaries of it. See [[Masculinity_and_Men|Masculinity & Men]].

## Health and the brain

- [How to reclaim your brain in 2026 - huberman](https://youtu.be/YvWU4Zd-IMc) — Andrew Huberman on how to reclaim your brain in 2026. Saved without a note. See [[Andrew_Huberman|Andrew Huberman]] and [[Mental_Health|Mental Health]].

## Celebrity conversations

- [JRE Matt Damon & Ben Affleck](https://youtu.be/AVEZBy1uAk8) — [[Joe_Rogan|Joe Rogan]] with Matt Damon and Ben Affleck, the longtime Boston collaborators. Saved without a note; the value is JRE as the room where A-list actors talk at length, more than the specific content.

## Andrew Tate

Two clips from [[Andrew_Tate|Andrew Tate]], saved side by side.

- [Tate about Fame](https://youtu.be/YjZaLqC9_z8) — Andrew Tate on fame. Saved without a note; this list reads it as fame as a cost or trap rather than a reward.
- [Tate about Fall of Empire (Second Half)](https://youtu.be/-CaV3ePx-LE) — The second half of Tate on the fall of empire: civilizational decline. See [[Political_Commentary|Political Commentary]] on entropy and extropy, and [[Masculinity_and_Men|Masculinity & Men]] on decline narratives.

## Themes across the list

- **Performative vs. generative masculinity.** The "performative male epidemic" and "war on men" videos are two sides of the same diagnosis: modern masculinity has been hollowed into signalling, with no load-bearing role left.
- **Huberman as reference point.** The list returns to him for brain and attention protocols, even while the culture around him is contested.
- **Celebrity long-form.** JRE keeps surfacing as the room where A-list actors talk at length without PR scaffolding.
- **Tate as civilizational commentator.** The Tate picks are the fame and empire takes, not the self-help.
