# X-Bookmarks

Saved X posts and selected Chrome bookmarks, filed into ten category pages. Each page lists its links by theme with a one-line description. Start from a category, or [browse all bookmarks](/bookmarks) to filter and search.

## Categories

- [[Bookmarks - AI & Agents]] — Prompting, coding-agent workflows, agent runtimes, AI security, voice, local inference and AI-lab economics.
- [[Bookmarks - Developer Tools]] — Self-hosting, browser automation, macOS and Linux utilities, local AI tools, web graphics and data APIs.
- [[Bookmarks - Design & Frontend]] — Interface components, motion, typography, WebGL and Three.js experiments, and design galleries.
- [[Bookmarks - Markets & Crypto]] — Hyperliquid mechanics, trading process and risk, market tools, microstructure, and macro and AI-cycle theses.
- [[Bookmarks - Business & Startups]] — Founder principles, product taste, AI and robotics business models, investors and company setup.
- [[Bookmarks - Health & Psychology]] — ADHD and sleep, mood and attention, training, skin and hair, hormones, nutrition and peptides.
- [[Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas]] — Reasoning and evidence, status and attention, platforms, politics and contested social claims.
- [[Bookmarks - Media & Inspiration]] — Film, series and book picks, creative-coding references, streaming tools, and memes.
- [[Bookmarks - Reference & Utilities]] — Account security, privacy services, web tools, dashboards, interactive explainers and a reading queue.
- [[Bookmarks - Personal & Life]] — Life experiments, career frameworks, outings, home shortlists and account consoles.

## What gets kept

Since 30 September 2026, only posts worth knowing long after the timeline moves on are kept: principles, mechanisms, reference tools and well-argued theses. Release news, product hype, forecasts, rumours, gossip and entertainment are dropped, though earlier saves of that kind stay where they were filed. Keeping a post does not make it true; each page describes it as its author framed it.

The lasting lessons drawn from these bookmarks are in [[Evergreen Lessons from X]]. [[Personal Knowledge Management]] covers how the bookmarks fit the wider note system.
