# trading_axe

trading_axe (@trading_axe) is a crypto trader who posts in an all-caps Remilio shitposting voice. Under the jokes the advice is about discipline and discretion. Quotes come from [[Crypto Takes]].

## Key ideas

- **Everything is an indicator.** A parody of contrarian sentiment reading: "Eventually EVERYTHING IN YOUR LIFE becomes an INDICATOR. I'm shopping and see TOO MANY bananas in random people's carts? I'm COUNTER TRADING them and buying apples instead." It ends "EVERYTHING IS AN INDICATOR."
- **Never flex.** "NEVER FLEX. EVER. Hide what you have." It follows a passage blaming FTX's collapse on the "EVIL EYE" of people envying traders who "ROUTINELY flexed their PnL's all over Twitter."
- **Don't talk crypto in real life.** "Crypto is the biggest L to talk about it IRL (literally NEVER do this - act oblivious). I even mispronounce 'terms' on purpose, "What's bite-coins at now?"" And: "Crypto is an INCEL trait."
- **Trade your own plan.** He mocks traders who narrate other people's orders ("Binance bidder back in the books! He's layering bids!"): "Why are you E-STALKING other men?" Then Kanye: "I'd rather be a leader than a follower."
- **Drill your answers.** He talks to himself in the car as if interviewed: "You need to be READY to ANSWER ANY QUESTION at ANY MOMENT IN TIME." Like a fighter shadow boxing, "I have DRILLED responses for almost every attack, even if it never presents itself."
- **Instinct.** A reply to him from @MatriXBT: "my BEST TRADES were PURE INSTINCT based trades. no TA, Orderflow or ANYTHING, NO TOOLS."
