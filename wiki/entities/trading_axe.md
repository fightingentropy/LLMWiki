# trading_axe

@trading_axe is a crypto trader on X with a Remilio shitposting voice: all-caps emphasis, with several posts signed off "Remilio." Erlin's [[CT]] note keeps his takes on hiding wealth, never talking crypto in real life, counter-trading everyday life, and ignoring what other traders do. Under the jokes, the advice is about discipline and discretion.

## Key ideas

### Everything is an indicator

> "When you've been trading for so long, eventually EVERYTHING IN YOUR LIFE becomes an INDICATOR … TOO MANY bananas in random people's carts? I'm COUNTER TRADING them" (from [[CT]])

The post runs the same joke through water (too many people drinking it, so "I'm headed STRAIGHT to the SAUNA. DEHYDRATE ME.") and traffic (too many people driving, so walk), and ends "EVERYTHING IS AN INDICATOR." It parodies contrarian [[Sentiment]] reading.

### Never flex

> "NEVER FLEX. EVER. Hide what you have." (from [[CT]], cited in [[Trading_Psychology]])

In the note the line sits directly after an unattributed passage blaming the FTX collapse on the "EVIL EYE" of onlookers envying traders who "ROUTINELY flexed their PnL's all over Twitter."

### Don't talk crypto IRL

> "Crypto is the biggest L to talk about IRL … I even mispronounce 'terms' on purpose, 'What's bite-coins at now?' … CHAD made ALL his money building SUCCESSFUL BUSINESSES. Crypto is an INCEL trait." (from [[CT]])

The original adds "(literally NEVER do this - act oblivious)."

### Ignore the orderflow stalkers

> "Orderflow guys gotta be the gayest people on CT … Why are you E-STALKING other men? … Why would I change what I'm doing based on some GEEK doing different?" (from [[CT]])

He mocks the running commentary of orderflow traders ("This Mex guy is closing his position!", "Binance bidder back in the books! He's layering bids! I know his print, that's him!") and borrows Kanye: "I'd rather be a leader than a follower." The point is to trade your own plan rather than follow other people's positions. Compare the case for reading flows on [[Flow Analysis]].

### Self-interview and schizomaxxing

> "Every time I sit in my car alone, I talk to myself as if I'm being interviewed … You need to be READY to ANSWER ANY QUESTION at ANY MOMENT IN TIME." (from [[CT]])

He jokes that it "Really helps with the schizomaxxing," then makes the serious point with a fighting analogy: drilled responses and shadow boxing. "I have DRILLED responses for almost every attack, even if it never presents itself."

### SBF transformation theory

He speculates that SBF's dishevelled look was deliberate: looking "p much homeless" means he can only improve, so to escape later he could "Lose weight, cut his hair, change style & no one will ever be able to tell." A reply in the thread adds: "All calculated. You don't have 14 billion to your name and look like that not purposely." (from [[CT]])
