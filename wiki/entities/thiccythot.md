# thiccythot

@thiccythot is a crypto-twitter commentator whose takes are archived in [[CT]]. The note spells the handle @thiccythØt. The [[Satoshi Nakamoto]] page cites the account alongside [[0xaporia]] as a canonical voice on how price-discovery mechanics evolve across asset cycles.

## Key ideas

### Price discovery moves from blunt proxies to precise expressions

In [[CT]] the commentary follows the [[0xaporia]] post of Satoshi Nakamoto's 2010 passage on price gravitating toward production cost, and is signed @thiccythØt. The argument is that each wave of a market uses a more precise instrument than the last:

- First wave: buy any oil. WTI outperforms Brent because it is the default proxy.
- Second wave: a better read on the mechanics of the supply shock. Brent is the cleaner expression.
- Crypto does the same: each cycle becomes more selective with asset quality.

The post closes on the system's adaptiveness: "feels like modern art".

> its interesting how price discovery moves from blunt proxies to more precise expressions with each wave  
> first wave -> buy any oil -> wti  
> outperforms brent be its the default proxy  
> second wave -> better read on mechanics of the supply shock -> brent cleaner expression than wti  
> you see it with crypto too with each cycle becoming more selective with asset quality  
> ...  
> it's so cool to witness adaptiveness of the system, feels like modern art
