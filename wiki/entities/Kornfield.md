# Jack Kornfield

American Buddhist teacher (b. 1945). One of the key figures in bringing Theravada/vipassana practice to the West; co-founder of Spirit Rock and the Insight Meditation Society. He is in this wiki for one line about time.

## Key ideas

### The trouble is that you think you have time

> "The trouble is that you think you have time." (from [[Quotes]], cited in [[Philosophy_and_Meaning|Philosophy & Meaning]] in the mortality and time cluster)

The closing themes of [[Quotes]] group it under "Time as scarce resource" with Sam Altman, [[Steve_Jobs|Steve Jobs]] and [[Marcus_Aurelius|Marcus Aurelius]]. All four converge on mortality as the orienting device for action.

### The companion lines

- Sam Altman: "Adjusted for the subjective increase in how fast time passes, life is half over by 23 or 24. Don't waste time."
- [[Steve_Jobs|Steve Jobs]]: "Remembering that you are going to die is the best way I know to avoid the trap of thinking you have something to lose. You are already naked."
- [[Marcus_Aurelius|Marcus Aurelius]]: "Soon you will have forgotten all and all will have forgotten you."
