# TradeXYZ

RWA/equities-oriented HIP-3 DEX associated with the `trade.xyz` documentation. It is in this wiki mainly for one open question: does its growth benefit HYPE holders? (from [[Bookmarks - Markets & Crypto]])

## Relationship to Hyperliquid

TradeXYZ's FAQ says Hyperliquid supplies the blockchain, and that independent, unaffiliated core contributors deployed the XYZ DEX. See [[Hyperliquid]] for the chain and [[Trading Platforms]] for how it sits among other venues.

## Open due-diligence questions

Jordi Alexander (dnap, @dnapway) asks what TradeXYZ's alignment with Hyperliquid means economically:

- Do fees from RWA growth accrue to HYPE holders?
- Are tokens bought, burned, or distributed?
- What arrangements exist between the parties?

The FAQ does not answer these. They stay due-diligence questions that need current contracts, fee destinations, disclosures, and governance. They are not an allegation, a confirmed relationship or a trading thesis. (from [[Bookmarks - Markets & Crypto]])

## Market structure

The @tradexyz account posted Discovery Bounds V2, a primary explanation of dynamic off-hours price bands for 24/7 perps. For a HIP-3 market set against its traditional venue, see [[HIP-3 Silver Microstructure]], which compares silver on Hyperliquid and CME. (from [[Bookmarks - Markets & Crypto]])
