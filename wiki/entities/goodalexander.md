# goodalexander

Alexander (@goodalexander) is a crypto and macro commentator on X. He is here for political-economy readings of crypto, a checklist for spotting hated assets, and blunt lines about what trading does to the trader.

## Key ideas

- **A silent debt default.** "At some point you'll need to sleep at night knowing the elites just used cryptocurrency as a silent debt default mechanism. And you won a zero sum game vs the uneducated and willfully ignorant masses. So at least do your best to build something interesting that helps people" (from [[Crypto Takes]])
- **Trading's new boss.** "'I want to trade so I don't have a boss' — now you have a new boss that's the invisible preferences of a bunch of psychos … congrats on your freedom" (from [[Crypto Takes]])
- **How to tell if something is contrarian.** Eleven checks, without reading any tweets: raw and relative valuation, growth at a reasonable price, where each sits against its own history, crypto cost of attack, sell-side ratings, borrow cost or funding, earnings revisions against price, expected deceleration, macro consensus, and options skew. Together they are "a much more reliable guide to positioning than what people say publicly." See [[Positioning and Sentiment]].
- **AI disappointment, on-chain upside.** A scenario essay: AI returns disappoint, fiscal stress follows, and stablecoins, tokenized debt and leveraged on-chain markets benefit. A later essay ties tokenization and stablecoins to demand for sovereign debt. See [[AI Infrastructure Economics]].
- **One-liners.** "look for belief, cult like community" (from [[X Takes]]). He also parodied a Druckenmiller article that read as AI-written ([[Humor]]).

Correction (15 October 2026): the Bessent "white hat hacking" thread was once credited to him here; it links to a @SuitablePolitic post, not his.
