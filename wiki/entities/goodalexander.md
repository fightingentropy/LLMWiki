## Overview
Twitter: @goodalexander. Crypto macro commentator known for political-economy framings — crypto as elite debt-default tool, Bessent as "white hat hacker" of the dollar.

## Recurring Themes (from [[CT]])

### Crypto as silent debt default
> "At some point you'll need to sleep at night knowing the elites just used cryptocurrency as a silent debt default mechanism. And you won a zero sum game vs the uneducated and willfully ignorant masses. So at least do your best to build something interesting that helps people" (from [[CT]])

### Bessent "white-hat-hacking" the dollar
Long thread framing Bessent's tariff volatility as intentional controlled detonation — deleveraging hedge funds, boxing in China, forcing a Yuan collapse → flight to Treasuries → lower rates for $9T refinancing. Preserved verbatim in [[CT]].

### Trading as new boss
> "'I want to trade so I don't have a boss' — now you have a new boss that's the invisible preferences of a bunch of psychos. he controls your mood, thoughts, wakes you up in the middle of the night" (from [[CT]])

### AI disappointment and on-chain finance scenario

The latest retained essay links slow AI economic returns, hacking, addictive media, labor displacement, and political conflict to fiscal stress, capital flight, stablecoins, tokenized debt, FX, and leveraged on-chain markets. It is a scenario map, not a forecast; test each premise against productivity, margins, rates, regulation, security, adoption, and fee growth. (from [[X-Bookmarks]])

## September 2026 tokenization scenario

The [September retained essay](https://x.com/goodalexander/status/2100700327003467985) links tokenization, stablecoins, CBDCs, privacy, ZK systems, institutional settlement, and agentic finance to sovereign-debt demand. This extends the earlier on-chain-finance scenario; it does not resolve its regulatory, technical, or economic premises. Keep it as an attributed scenario and distinguish adoption from token-holder value capture. (from [[Bookmarks - Markets & Crypto]])
