# DrDynamis

Pseudonymous X voice posting as @DrDynamis, @DrdynamisA, @dynamis_dr and @ArcadianRythms, treated here as one author. He is the third pole in the men's-culture material in [[Women]]: not a game coach like [[Heartiste]], not a sterile optimizer like the hosts on [[Podcast Hosts]], but a Mediterranean-stoic moralist who is pro-God, pro-lifting and pro-suit, and anti-nerd and anti-porn.

- **Fear is the killer.** "Number 1 killer of men isn't 'seed oils'. But a fear of confrontation. Can't even get the bartenders attention when she skips u for the 8th time & serves someone else. Wait like a little doggie. That weakness bleeds into everything in life." (from [[Women]])
- **It is ennui, not depression.** "What you dudes are feeling is not depression. It is called ENNUI. Look it up: 'A feeling of listlessness & dissatisfaction arising from a lack of occupation and excitement'"
- **Lifting and prayer.** "Nothing comes close pound for pound & time invested vs output gained wise than 1. Lifting 2. Prayer. For just 5 hours of lifting a week you can change ur entire existential experience on earth. Just 20 minutes of true prayer a day brings in more luck it feels like cheating."
- **Work should feel like adventure.** "Hard work is pleb shit. Only hard work men truly enjoy is 1. Hard work that feels like Adventure. Like Biz/ranching 2. Hard work that feels like Alchemy (Writing)"
- **Respect over being liked.** Tell a man "I don't like u but I do respect u" and he buys you a beer; reverse it and you have an enemy for life. "Respect > Adoration. For men."
- **Against the optimizers.** The most boring man he knows is a bodybuilder with "25 supplements & got a literal shit transplant to improve gut health. Gets 0 pussy."
- **The final accounting.** "90% of us are gonna be rotting in a grave in 50 years. Only things that matter are 1. Did you get right with God? 2. Did you live like a G? 3. Did you leave an impact on this fallen earth?"
