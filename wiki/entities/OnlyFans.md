# OnlyFans

Subscription creator platform. It is in this wiki because of a post saved in [[Women]] that argues women voluntarily turned a general premium-content product into a pornography platform. The note keeps that claim next to a correction of the platform's history.

## Key ideas

- **The claim.** The post says OnlyFans began as a general premium-content platform and was transformed into a pornography platform by women acting voluntarily. From that it concludes that accounts of women as victims of the porn industry are disproved. (from [[Women]])
- **Correction.** OnlyFans did support general creator subscriptions. But contemporary histories describe it as primarily hosting pornography early on, its founder had prior adult-industry businesses, and adult performers were deliberately recruited. Patreon also does not categorically ban all adult content.
- **What agency does not settle.** Creator choice does not by itself settle coercion, exploitation, platform incentives, payment-processor power, or individual victimization.
- **Both can be true.** A platform can deliberately recruit adult creators while individual creators also exercise agency. Neither fact alone resolves the question (from [[Gender_Dynamics|Gender Dynamics]]). Neither a clean victimhood story nor a clean voluntarist story fits every case (from [[Dating_and_Relationships|Dating & Relationships]]).
- **How to read it.** An agency-and-affordances debate. It is not a general conclusion about women, and it is not a complete platform history.
