# OnlyFans

Subscription creator platform discussed in [[Women]] through a claim that women voluntarily transformed a general premium-content product into a pornography platform. The retained correction is that OnlyFans did support general subscriptions, but contemporary histories describe pornography as central early on, its founder had prior adult-industry businesses, and adult performers were deliberately recruited.

Creator choice does not by itself settle coercion, exploitation, platform incentives, payment-processor power, or individual victimization. Preserve the item as an agency-and-affordances debate, not a general conclusion about women or a complete platform history.
