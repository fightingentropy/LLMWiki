# 0xaporia

0xaporia (@0xaporia) is a crypto trader who writes about variance, strategy hopping and exits in a systems-minded way. His posts are in [[Crypto Takes]], [[X-Bookmarks]] and [[Bookmarks - Markets & Crypto]].

## Key ideas

- **Accept variance.** Poker players "accept variance as part of the game", so they can fold. "Novice traders often conflate constant activity with progress."
- **Don't strategy-hop.** "Strategy hopping is one of the most under discussed issues on CT imo. It's mostly a flight from the grueling, nonlinear phase where most progress happens." Losing streaks are normal: over 100 trades at a 50% win rate, a run of five or more losses has about an 80% chance; at 40%, about 98%. His rule: "Decouple Process from Outcome."
- **Exits look forward.** "No one ever went broke taking profits" fails for strategies that lose more often than they win, because the big winners pay for the losses. "The market has no memory of your cost basis".
- **Backtest hygiene.** Let AI agents do the clerical work, not the judgement: freeze the evaluation protocol, hold out unseen data and include costs. Before trusting a pattern, ask what economic service the strategy provides.
- **Calls are not trades.** A correct top or bottom call can still lose money through sizing, leverage or a bad exit.
- **Price discovery.** He posted Satoshi's 2010 line: "The price of any commodity tends to gravitate toward the production cost." thiccythot's reply on WTI and Brent is in [[Traders Directory]].
- **Curiosity and optimism.** "I've come to believe that curiosity is more important than most other traits." And: "Life is fucking electric bro. Don't fall for the doomer shit."
