# Alex Hormozi

Entrepreneur and business author (Acquisition.com, "$100M Offers", "$100M Leads"). He is in this wiki as a guest on [[Chris_Williamson|Chris Williamson]]'s podcast. His framing of **imperfect action** is the point Erlin's notes flag to watch and analyse.

## Key ideas

### Imperfect action

The [[Go Do]] note puts it as a to-do:

> Watch and analyse Chris Williamson pod with Alex Hormozi and his point on "Imperfect action" (from [[Go Do]])

The note names the idea but does not spell it out. The wiki's summary of Hormozi's argument: execution beats planning, and perfect-looking plans are usually procrastination dressed as preparation. It matches the "Imperfect Action > Perfect Planning" section in [[Self_Improvement|Self-Improvement]] — "aim, fire, correct later."

### One episode, four saved moments

The same episode (video `Gk8EGWoGnEQ`) is the source of three more bookmarks in [[Youtube]]: views on happiness, the only way is through, and "My Goal in life is not necessarily to be happy. My goal in life is to do epic shit." The "epic shit" idea is filed under [[Philosophy_and_Meaning|Philosophy & Meaning]]. The episode appears to be a load-bearing reference for Erlin's action-over-optimization stance. All four moments are linked below.

### Buying time, skills and distraction

One line is credited to him in the notes:

> "Rich people buy time. Poor people buy stuff. Ambitious people buy skills. Lazy people buy distraction." (@AlexHormozi, from [[Quotes]])
