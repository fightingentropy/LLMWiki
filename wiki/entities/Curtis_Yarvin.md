# Curtis Yarvin (Mencius Moldbug)

Curtis Yarvin, known as Mencius Moldbug. In this wiki he is the source of the entropy-versus-extropy reframe of left and right, which turns political philosophy into a thermodynamic metaphor. It comes from his February 2026 interview on Peter McCormack's podcast.

## Key ideas

### Entropy versus extropy

The note records "the left is entropic the right is extropic" as "a direct (or very close) quote" from the interview. It gives the fuller version as approximate: "Fundamentally it's entropy versus extropy. The left is entropic, so things can just decay. But if you're building something, you need to build." The note's plain-English reading follows. It paraphrases Yarvin and links no transcript or episode.

**Entropy (the left)**

- The natural tendency toward disorder, decay and dissolution. In physics, things fall apart unless energy is constantly added.
- Deconstructs hierarchies, traditions, standards, borders and merit-based systems.
- More "equality" on paper, but societal breakdown, inefficiency and eventual collapse: "things can just decay."
- The note's examples are modern progressivism, democracy and bureaucratic drift, everything slowly sliding leftward and getting messier. It ties this to his famous line "Cthulhu always swims left."

**Extropy (the right)**

- The active increase of order, complexity, intelligence, vitality and capability. The note calls extropy the made-up opposite of entropy, popularized in futurism and transhumanism.
- Life itself is extropic: it builds organized structures (cells → organisms → societies).
- Strong hierarchies and sovereign decision-making, meaning one person or a small group who can actually act.
- Innovation that creates new capability and complexity.
- Patchwork-style governance or corporate-sovereign models that get things done instead of decaying.
- Not passive conservatism ("just slow down the left a bit") but energetic construction.

### Why the frame beats left versus right

- Traditional "conservative vs. liberal" or "Republican vs. Democrat" misses the point. Real right-wing energy is not slow-motion leftism but creative and order-building (extropic). Most "conservatives" are just slower versions of the entropic trend; the note says they conserve yesterday's leftism.
- Politics becomes a cosmic battle between decay and deliberate creation, not policy details. It cuts through the debate about specific policies to the underlying worldview difference: do you want to let systems rot, or actively build stronger structures?
- In short, per the note: "Left = lets the system rot (entropy wins). Right = fights rot by actively building stronger, smarter structures (extropy wins)."
- The note's account of his view: interesting, high-openness people used to flock left but are now starting to shift right as the entropy becomes obvious.

### Reception

A poster called it "the best line... that no one is mentioning." The note adds that the sharp thermodynamic metaphor cuts through political noise and reframes everything in one line.
