# Stanley Druckenmiller

Stanley Druckenmiller is a veteran macro investor. He is in this wiki because his style is the template for the daily brief in [[Markets - Brief]], titled "Daily Market Overview (Stanley Druckenmiller Style)".

## The style

The brief asks for a "direct, high-conviction, PM-ready" overview (from [[Markets - Brief]]):

- No fluff.
- Signal over narrative.
- Facts, interpretation and positioning implications kept separate.
- Concise, but complete enough to make portfolio decisions.

It runs in seven parts: an executive snapshot, a regime call with a confidence level, a cross-asset read, positioning and flow, regime shifts to watch with confirmation signals, a risk radar with trigger levels, and a Polymarket check. [[Markets - Brief]] holds the full template.

## Principles it encodes

Neither note quotes him directly; these are what the brief's structure puts first.

- The market regime comes first: growth, inflation, liquidity and volatility.
- Cross-asset moves show what a single market hides. See [[Macro Trading]].
- Crowded positioning creates squeeze risk. See [[Positioning and Sentiment]].
- Observable confirmation signals beat predictions.
- Risks need specific triggers, not vague worries.
- Perception moves markets more than truth: "Truth doesn't necessarily move markets. Perception & Uncertainty/Certainty transition does." (from [[Markets - Trading]])
