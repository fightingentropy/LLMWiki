# LomahCrypto

@LomahCrypto is a crypto trader on X, quoted in [[CT]] for the "leaving the casino" discipline: money withdrawn from exchanges never goes back. [[Trading Psychology]] cites the rule directly as the "withdraw and don't redeposit" rule, and one of the account's posts is linked from [[Business & Finance]].

## Key ideas

### Leaving the casino

LomahCrypto says the rule is borrowed from SalsaTekila (from [[CT]]):

> I have this rule that I stole from SalsaTekila, where any money that I withdraw from exchanges, I don't deposit back.
>
> I was annoyed with myself a bit for taking out a bulk of my portfolio in Feb. but hindsight being 20/20, it's probably the only reason I didn't give it all back.

And the mindset behind it (from [[CT]]):

> leaving the casino is a crazy mindset, ever since the first time cat withdrew from exchange, cat never deposited ever again

The rule pairs with [[Pentosh1]]'s "the only money that is yours is what you take out into the real world". Same discipline, two traders.

### Longing into Bitcoin ATHs

A line in the Business & Finance note reads "You're scared of longing into Bitcoin ATHs, I understand." and links a [LomahCrypto post](https://twitter.com/LomahCrypto/status/1764783649004621961) on "understand". The next line in the note is "Never a great idea to long into a high timeframe resistance but.." (from [[Business & Finance]])
