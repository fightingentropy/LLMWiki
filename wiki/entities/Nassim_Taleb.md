# Nassim Taleb

Nassim Nicholas Taleb is the author of *Antifragile* and *The Black Swan*. He is in the wiki for ideas on risk, optionality, dignity and reading statistics, which feed [[Trading Philosophy]] and [[Philosophy & Meaning]].

## Key ideas

- **Dignity has a price.** His father refused a militiaman's disrespect at a road check in the Lebanese war and was shot in the back as he drove away; the bullet stayed in his chest. Taleb's lesson: "This set the bar very high for me: dignity is worth nothing unless you earn it, unless you are willing to pay a price for it." (from [[Stories]])
- **Collect free options.** Lucky breaks are rare, so take them when they come: "Collect as many free nonlottery tickets (those with open-ended payoffs) as you can, and, once they start paying off, do not discard them." Big cities help because "you gain exposure to the envelope of serendipity." (from [[Thoughts & Observations]])
- **Antifragile.** Fragile systems break under shocks, robust ones survive them, and antifragile ones "*need* shocks to improve". If you gain from an input, seek variability, not comfort.
- **Optionality of people.** A line in [[Quotes]] applies option pricing to Nietzsche's view of human worth: a baby is an option with a big premium, an old man one whose premium has decayed.
- **Skin in the game.** Javier Milei's "Anybody can be a prostitute with other people's asses" is the same idea (see [[People Quoted]]); a video titled "Soul in the game" is saved in [[Youtube]].
- **Statistics and character.** Saved posts in [[Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas]] argue that a population average does not fix one person's outcome, that role models should be chosen for character over celebrity, and that politics should run from communal at family scale to liberty at federal scale.

## Lines

- "Academia is to knowledge what prostitution is to love."
- "Decline starts with the replacement of dreams with memories and ends with the replacement of memories with other memories."
