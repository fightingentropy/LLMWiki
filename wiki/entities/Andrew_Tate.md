# Andrew Tate

Former kickboxer turned online personality and controversial figure in the manosphere. Polarizing: treated as either the patron saint of reclaimed masculinity or a caricature of its worst failure modes, depending on audience. He is in this wiki for two saved clips in [[Top Pods]] and one clip in [[Women]] where he talks to a panel of western women.

## Key ideas

### Saved clips on fame and empire

Two clips are saved in [[Top Pods]], labelled "Tate about Fame" and "Tate about Fall of Empire (Second Half)". The note gives only the titles. This wiki reads them as follows:

- **Fame**: fame as a trap rather than a reward; the cost structure of being watched.
- **Fall of Empire (Second Half)**: civilizational-decline framing, a late-stage-Rome style analogy applied to the contemporary West.

None of his fitness or grindset material is saved. The interest is specifically his commentary on fame and the civilizational arc.

### The panel clip in Women

[[Women]] saves one further clip, linked as "Brutally reality-checks western women", in which Tate responds to a panel of western women. The lines under the link in the note read:

> She wants to be a doctor. Imagine Miss "Do Whatever The Fuck I Want" performing a triple bypass surgery on you. "Absolutely insulting to your entire bloodline" that right there hit my soul.

The next lines in the note are a first-time viewer's reaction ("You know I've actually never listened to Andrew Tate, this is my first opening to him"), and then a line from a father of a 5-year-old son:

> I can say that although I was reluctant to have a child at first, I have never loved a human being so much in my life and never will.

> Every child deserves parents but not every parent deserves children

### Cultural role

Lightning rod for the [[Masculinity_and_Men|Masculinity & Men]] discourse. The [[Andrew_Huberman|Andrew Huberman]] / [[Tim_Ferriss|Tim Ferriss]] / [[Lex_Fridman|Lex Fridman]] axis stands for "sterile optimization". Tate's positioning is the opposite pole: overt wealth display, aggression, and anti-therapeutic rhetoric. Both poles share algorithm-rewarded performance; they differ on the performance's surface.

### Paradox

- **Signal:** articulates a diagnosis of male anomie that mainstream discourse refuses to name.
- **Noise:** the messenger, the lifestyle, and the legal history complicate every claim he makes.
