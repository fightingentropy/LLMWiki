# BS 7671

BS 7671 is the technical requirements reference for electrical installations. [[GN3 Guidance Note 3]] supports inspection and testing; [[Building Regulations]] explains the separate statutory context.

## Edition checked 22 September 2026

The [IET edition checker](https://electrical.theiet.org/bs-7671-18th-edition-wiring-regulations/ensure-you-are-up-to-date-with-bs-7671/) lists BS 7671:2018+A4:2026. The Brown Book with Amendment 3 remains valid until **15 October 2026**, and either may be used during the transition. Record the edition used for each piece of work; check the IET's errata as well.

## How to use it in this wiki

A topic page is an explanation or study aid, not the complete standard. Check the actual text for conditions, exceptions and definitions before using a numerical rule. Historical page numbers are not portable between editions. Older installation dates do not, by themselves, establish that an installation is unsafe.

[[Electrical Evidence Review - September 2026]] records the scope of this edition check. [[Electrical Study Map]] links the study sequence; [[EIC and MEIWC]] and [[EICR]] cover distinct forms of documentation.
