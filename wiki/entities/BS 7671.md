# BS 7671

BS 7671 is the technical requirements reference for electrical installations. [[GN3 Guidance Note 3]] supports inspection and testing against it; [[Building Regulations]] explains the separate statutory context. This page holds the edition status and the limits on how the wiki's study notes should be used against it.

## Current edition and transition

As checked on 22 September 2026, the IET edition checker lists BS 7671:2018+A4:2026.

- The Brown Book with Amendment 3 remains valid until **15 October 2026**, and either edition may be used during the transition.
- Record the edition used for each piece of work. Check the IET's errata as well.
- The IET lists Guidance Note 3: Inspection & Testing as tenth edition and the On-Site Guide as ninth edition, both aligned with A4. See [[GN3 Guidance Note 3]]. (from [[Electrical Evidence Review - September 2026]])

## How to use it in this wiki

- A topic page is an explanation or study aid, not the complete standard.
- Check the actual text for conditions, exceptions and definitions before using a numerical rule.
- Historical page numbers are not portable between editions.
- Older installation dates do not, by themselves, establish that an installation is unsafe.
- [[Electrical Study Map]] links the study sequence; [[EIC and MEIWC]] and [[EICR]] cover distinct forms of documentation.

## Clause numbers in the notes

The Resources note cites Reg 522.6.202, Reg 522.6.203 and Reg 522.5.204 on 30 mA RCD protection for concealed cables. The September 2026 evidence review flags 522.5.204 as a source error rather than a verified current clause. It says nothing about the other two. The three claims, and the corrections to them, are on [[RCD Protection]]. (from [[Electrics - Resources]])
