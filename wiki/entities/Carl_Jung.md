# Carl Jung

Swiss psychiatrist and analytical psychologist (1875–1961). He appears in [[Quotes]], Erlin's quote collection, for lines on addiction, the shadow, and why most people judge instead of think.

## Key ideas

### Addiction

- **On addiction:** "All addictions are a low level search for God." In the note it follows an unattributed passage that begins "The soul is oriented towards obsession: it wants to be possessed by something greater".
- **Elsewhere in the same note** (unattributed): "Addiction is a progressive narrowing of the things that bring you pleasure. A good life is a progressive expansion of the things that bring you pleasure."

### Repressed passions and the shadow

- **On repressed passions:** "A man who has not passed through the inferno of his passions has never overcome them. They then dwell in the house next door, and at any moment a flame may dart out and set fire to his own house. Whenever we give up, leave behind, and forget too much, there is always the danger that the things we have neglected will return with added force."

### Thinking versus judging

- **On thinking vs judging** (in Greek): "Είναι δύσκολο να σκέφτεσαι γιαυτό οι περισσότεροι άνθρωποι κρίνουν." ("It is difficult to think, which is why most people judge.")
