# Quicker

Quicker is a unified-messaging product lead. The [retained post](https://x.com/tomaldertweets/status/2102005817507479969) advertises conversations from LinkedIn, Slack, WhatsApp, X, iMessage, and other services, with search, tags, reminders, suggested replies, and local storage. [Product entry point](https://quicker.chat/). (from [[Bookmarks - Business & Startups]])

## Open questions

“Local storage” alone does not describe authentication, synchronization, backups, model inference, or telemetry. The bookmark does not verify which services are officially supported, what message data leaves the device, how accounts are revoked, or how durable exports work.

The product pattern connects to [[AI Agents]] and [[Business_Strategy]]; account and message access belongs in [[Digital Security]]. Treat the integration list as advertising until checked against working product behaviour.
