# Quicker

Quicker is a unified-messaging product lead. A post by @tomaldertweets advertises it as one inbox for conversations from LinkedIn, Slack, WhatsApp, X, iMessage and other services (from [[Bookmarks - Business & Startups]]).

## What it claims

- Combines conversations from LinkedIn, Slack, WhatsApp, X, iMessage and other services.
- Local storage.
- Search, tags and reminders.
- Suggested replies and categorization.

## Open questions

"Local storage" alone does not describe authentication, synchronization, backups, model inference or telemetry. The post does not say:

- Which services are officially supported.
- What message data leaves the device.
- How accounts are revoked.
- How durable exports work.

The integration list is advertising until checked against working product behaviour. Before connecting private messages, check platform authorization, encryption, local-storage boundaries, model access, retention and account risk. Account and message access belongs in [[Digital Security]].
