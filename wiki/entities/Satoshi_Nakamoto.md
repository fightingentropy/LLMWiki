# Satoshi Nakamoto

Pseudonymous creator of Bitcoin. The wiki's main reference to him is a 2010 passage on production cost and price discovery, preserved in [[CT|Crypto Takes]], Erlin's archive of crypto-twitter posts.

## Key ideas

### Production cost pulls price toward it

The passage describes a feedback loop. Below cost, production slows. Above cost, profit pulls in more production, which raises difficulty and pushes the cost of generating back toward the price.

> "The price of any commodity tends to gravitate toward the production cost. If the price is below cost, then production slows down. If the price is above cost, profit can be made by generating and selling more. At the same time, the increased production would increase the difficulty, pushing the cost of generating towards the price." — Satoshi Nakamoto (2010) (from [[CT]])

### How the archive uses it

In [[CT]] the quote sits under [[0xaporia]]'s handle, followed by [[thiccythot]]'s take: "its interesting how price discovery moves from blunt proxies to more precise expressions with each wave." His oil example: in the first wave traders buy any oil, so WTI outperforms Brent as the default proxy; in the second, a better read on the supply shock makes Brent the cleaner expression. He adds that crypto shows the same thing, with each cycle more selective on asset quality. The two passages together are the wiki's reference for how price-discovery mechanics evolve across asset-class cycles.

### A passing mention

A passage in [[CT]] that calls crypto a "microcosm of endless opportunity" includes the line: "I guess it's the first time I've actually been thankful for whoever satoshi was and everyone who helped me along the way."
