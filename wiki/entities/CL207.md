# CL207

@CL207 is a crypto trader on Twitter, quoted in [[CT|Crypto Takes]]. The account is best known in this wiki for one framing: narratives are hallucinated *ex post* from price, not the other way around.

## Key ideas

### Narratives follow price

> "'whats the next narrative' — my trader in christ anytime price moves 10% we will literally hallucinate a story up for it" (from [[CT]])

The line answers the "what's the next narrative" question by saying that whenever price moves 10%, traders make up a story to explain it. [[Sentiment]], [[Trading_Psychology]] and [[Behavioral Finance]] all cite it as the example of narratives being backfit to price.

### The opposing view: @lightcrypto

@lightcrypto treats schelling-point narratives as pre-conditions for rallies, which contradicts @CL207's ex-post reading:

> "The market now lacks a viable schelling point narrative. Yet its desire for one is palpable. It wants to make back what it lost. We will suspend disbelief in an effort to do so once a vehicle emerges to carry this latent desire. In the wreckage are nascent roots." (@lightcrypto, from [[CT]])

Both are partially true. [[CT]] flags the clash under Contradictions rather than resolving it.

### Reply threads with DegenSpartan

[[CT]] also quotes a reply thread under a @CL207 post, led by [[DegenSpartan]]:

- @DegenSpartan, replying to @CL207: "imagine if all the protocols sell each others tokens (and their own tokens) and in the end its just retail bagholding + funds with a position too large that they can never exit"
- @Awawat_Trades, replying to @DegenSpartan and @CL207: "I liked this post because it's nice to pretend it isn't exactly what is happening"
