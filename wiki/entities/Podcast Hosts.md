# Podcast Hosts

Seven long-form hosts and online personalities who come up in the notes, mostly through saved episodes and one sharp critique. Each gets who they are and what is saved; the episode links themselves live in [[Youtube]]. Writers and thinkers are on [[People Quoted]].

## The "secular priesthood" critique

One saved [post by @xirtman](https://x.com/xirtman/status/1902893343014592631) groups Tim Ferriss, Andrew Huberman and Lex Fridman as men who preach self-optimization without building families. Its claims about their private lives are the post's own. It is the main reason Ferriss and Fridman are in the wiki at all, and [[Gender Dynamics]] and [[Self-Improvement]] build on it.

> "Tim Ferris: the productivity hacker turned self-experimenting monk. Andrew Huberman: the neuroscience demigod whose dopamine maps rarely intersect with intimacy or fatherhood. Lex Friedman: the emotionless black-clad pod-priest who flirts with sentimentality but recoils at generativity. These aren't men with families — they're cult leaders of sterile optimization. Their mantra: hack your life — not live your life." (from [[X Takes]])

> "Where are the podcasts about building families, holding down difficult relationships, sacrificing for children, standing up for tradition? The algorithm doesn't reward those. It rewards slick, postmodern celibacy dressed as enlightenment."

> "Do you want to follow men who optimize their testosterone — or men who use it to build a future?"

[[DrDynamis]] makes the same point from the other side: the bodybuilder with "25 supplements" who "Gets 0 pussy."

## The optimizers

- **Andrew Huberman** _(@hubermanlab)_, neuroscientist and podcaster known for sleep, dopamine, cold-exposure and supplement protocols. His magnesium case (threonate or bisglycinate, 30–60 minutes before bed) is on [[Body Optimization]], his sleep work on [[Circadian Rhythm]]. Saved: the video [How to reclaim your brain in 2026](https://youtu.be/YvWU4Zd-IMc). Two posts in [[X Takes]] joke that dating five women at once at 48 proves his methods work: "If that's not a ringing endorsement of his methods, I don't what is."
- **Tim Ferriss**, author of *The 4-Hour Workweek* and the face of life hacking and self-experimentation. In the wiki only as a target of the critique above.
- **Lex Fridman**, podcast host and AI researcher known for long conversations with scientists and public figures. Also only a target of the critique; none of his own words are saved.

## The long-form rooms

- **Joe Rogan**, comedian and host of The Joe Rogan Experience, where guests sit for two to three hours unscripted. Saved: [JRE with Matt Damon & Ben Affleck](https://youtu.be/AVEZBy1uAk8), a lead in [[Humor]] ("Salvador Dali biggest simp ever. JRE 1851 about 3 hour mark (5 min in)"), and a [recommendation](https://x.com/AkilahObviously/status/2098872734096724112) for an Elephant Graveyard video on Rogan and Peter Thiel.
- **Chris Williamson**, host of Modern Wisdom, long-form talks on psychology, performance, relationships and business. His episode with Alex Hormozi is the most-bookmarked podcast in the notes, with four saved moments: [imperfect action](https://youtu.be/Gk8EGWoGnEQ?t=3590) (59:50), [do epic shit](https://youtu.be/Gk8EGWoGnEQ?t=8918) (2:28:38), [views on happiness](https://youtu.be/Gk8EGWoGnEQ?t=9084) (2:31:24) and [the only way is through](https://youtu.be/Gk8EGWoGnEQ?t=9136) (2:32:16). His own line: "Alcohol is the only drug where if you don't do it, people assume you have a problem."
- **Alex Hormozi**, entrepreneur and author of *$100M Offers* (Acquisition.com). [[Go Do]] flags his "Imperfect action" point on Williamson's show to watch and analyse: execution beats planning, and polished plans are often procrastination. Two lines of his are kept:
  - "My Goal in life is not necessarily to be happy. My goal in life is to do epic shit."
  - "Rich people buy time. Poor people buy stuff. Ambitious people buy skills. Lazy people buy distraction." _(@AlexHormozi)_

## The provocateur

- **Andrew Tate**, former kickboxer turned manosphere personality, the opposite pole to the optimizers: wealth display and aggression instead of protocols. Saved: two clips, [Tate about Fame](https://youtu.be/YjZaLqC9_z8) and [Tate about Fall of Empire (Second Half)](https://youtu.be/-CaV3ePx-LE), and in [[Women]] a [clip](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nlDY0D7CscM) of him answering a panel of western women. The reaction saved with it: "Imagine Miss "Do Whatever The Fuck I Want" performing a triple bypass surgery on you."
