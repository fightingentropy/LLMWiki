# HighStakesCap

Crypto trader on X (@HighStakesCap). Erlin's crypto-takes note ([[CT|Crypto Takes]]) quotes the handle on execution, and the trading note ([[Markets - Trading]]) lists the handle among its "Good traders" to watch. Most of the saved material is about discipline in the trade itself: don't watch PnL mid-trade, know your levels, have a plan, don't get emotional.

## Key ideas

### Don't watch PnL mid-war

> "Do not watch your PnL or NW when you go to war. Just need to know levels where you need to act / add / reduce. Have a plan, don't get emotional" (from [[CT|Crypto Takes]])

- The plan is a set of levels: where to act, where to add, where to reduce.
- Watching profit and loss, or net worth (NW), during the trade is what the line says to avoid.
- In the note it sits among posts about accepting trading losses and staying resilient; see [[Trading Psychology]] for the wider theme.

### A non-trading line

The same note carries a second post under this handle, a reply to @smileycapital, @CanteringClark and two others, about men and women:

> "There is chaos and destruction in men too, it just materializes differently. In men, it's mostly predatory instinct In women, it's mostly emotional disorder" (from [[CT|Crypto Takes]])
