# Robinhood HYPE

The "Robinhood HYPE wallet" is a wallet on [[Hyperliquid]] EVM, tracked as Robinhood's cryptocurrency trading and operations wallet. It is the only entry under "Wallets" in Erlin's trading note ([[Markets - Trading]]). The Robinhood attribution is the note's label.

## Address

- Hyperliquid EVM: `0x841ed663f2636863d40be4ee76243377dff13a34`

## Why it is tracked

The idea is to follow a major institutional participant's positions in crypto derivatives. The same trading note lists "Top traders positioning" and "liquidations" among the things to look for. Watching this wallet is meant to show:

- Institutional flow and positioning
- Market-maker activity
- Potential hedging flows
- Retail interest proxy, through Robinhood's customer base
