# Compositor

Compositor is Robbie Tilton's open-source Mac image editor, focused on compositing and post-processing. Its official page lists layers, masks, selections, adjustment layers, filters, and retouching tools, with an editable Xcode project. [Official product page](https://robbietilton.com/compositor), checked 2026-09-22.

## Retained releases

- [Launch bookmark](https://x.com/robbietilton/status/2100946395972976843) — small, focused Photoshop-like editor.
- [v1.1 bookmark](https://x.com/robbietilton/status/2101714382665519192) — text tools and layer effects.

These are two source posts for the same product. The latest installed release, file compatibility, colour handling, and performance have not been tested in this wiki. (from [[Bookmarks - Design & Frontend]])

## Why retained

A native, focused editor is relevant to [[Design_and_Aesthetics]] and the graphics tools in [[Developer_Resources]]. The useful comparison is whether ordinary compositing tasks remain familiar and reliable; marketing resemblance to Photoshop does not establish full compatibility.
