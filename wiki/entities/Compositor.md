# Compositor

Compositor is Robbie Tilton's open-source Mac image editor, focused on compositing and post-processing. It is in this wiki as a small, native, Photoshop-like editor, relevant to [[Design_and_Aesthetics|Design & Aesthetics]] and to the graphics tools in [[Developer_Resources|Developer Resources]]. (from [[Bookmarks - Design & Frontend]])

## Key ideas

- **Features.** The official page lists layers, masks, selections, adjustment layers, filters and retouching tools, with an editable Xcode project.
- **How it is presented.** A free, open-source, 12 MB editor focused on essential compositing tools.
- **v1.1.** Adds a text tool, layer effects and smaller improvements.
- **What to compare.** Whether ordinary compositing tasks stay familiar and reliable. Marketing resemblance to Photoshop does not establish full compatibility.
- **Before adopting.** Inspect the repository, platform support, file compatibility and licence, and try the latest release for colour handling and performance on real files.
