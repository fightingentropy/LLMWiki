# David Goggins

Former US Navy SEAL, ultramarathon runner, and author of *Can't Hurt Me* and *Never Finished*. In this wiki he is the canonical voice of discipline, suffering as fuel, and refusing comfort.

## Key ideas

### Comfort as the failure mode

> "You are in danger of living a life so comfortable and soft, that you will die without even realizing your true potential." (from [[Quotes]], cited in [[Self_Improvement|Self-Improvement]] under discomfort and growth)

Goggins sits alongside Musashi, Napoleon and [[Peter_Thiel|Peter Thiel]] in the Action, Agency, Courage cluster of [[Quotes]]. Comfort is the failure mode that swallows potential.

### Haters

> "If you could walk on water, the haters will say it's because you can't swim." (from [[Quotes]])

### The courage cluster around him

The other lines in that cluster of [[Quotes]]:

- "To win any battle, you must fight as if you are already dead." — Musashi
- "Courage cannot be counterfeited. It is one virtue that escapes hypocrisy." — Napoleon Bonaparte
- "Brilliant thinking is rare, but courage is in even shorter supply than genius." — Peter Thiel (video linked below)
