# PhoneLLM Alpha 1

Daily/Pipecat open-weights fine-tune of NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Nano 30B-A3B, aimed at concise multi-turn dialogue and tool use with thinking disabled. It is the text-language component of a voice pipeline, not an end-to-end speech model; speech recognition and synthesis remain separate.

The model card and PhoneBench evaluation are useful deployment references. Cost, latency, and capability comparisons are vendor benchmarks under specific serving conditions and need reproduction on the target hardware and workload. (from [[X-Bookmarks]])
