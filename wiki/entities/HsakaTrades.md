# HsakaTrades

Twitter: @HsakaTrades. Crypto trader known for punchy one-liners. [[Markets - Trading]] lists him under "Good traders" to watch, alongside [[cobie]], [[fejau_inc]], [[TheFlowHorse]] and [[HighStakesCap]].

## Key ideas

His saved lines in [[CT|Crypto Takes]] borrow the voice of a famous figure and turn it into a trading rule. Quotes are verbatim.

### Orderflow vs newsflow

> "The orderflow frontruns the newsflow"
>
> \- Marcus Aurelius

Price moves on positioning and flow before the story arrives to explain it. See [[Flow Analysis]].

### Archimedes on leverage

> "Give me a lever long enough and a fulcrum on which to place it, and I shall move the curve"

A riff on Archimedes' line about moving the world. See [[Leverage]].

### Sell walls as filter

The note introduces this one with "Hsaka writes", and the quote is signed "Karl Marx":

> "The sell walls are there for a reason.
>
> The sell walls are not there to suppress price. The sell walls are there to give us a chance to show how badly we want higher prices.
>
> Because the sell walls are there to stop the people who don't want it badly enough."
>
> \- Karl Marx
