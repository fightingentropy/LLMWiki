# Heartiste

Pseudonymous game/red-pill writer, also published as **Roissy**. He is the most-quoted external voice in the [[Women]] note: the anchor of its attraction framework and the author of the **Sixteen Commandments** the note carries in full. This page collects what the note takes from him. The note credits him by name on one one-liner and on the "Heartiste on Game" passage; most of the other lines sit in a run of the note beside the [@CHeartiste](https://twitter.com/CHeartiste) handle.

## Key ideas

### Voice

- Terse, aphoristic, polemical.
- Pro-"game" as a learnable craft; anti-feminist framing as a blind spot.
- Writes as if describing an observed biological order rather than prescribing one.

### The Sixteen Commandments (Roissy)

The [[Women]] note carries each commandment in full, under the heading "The Sixteen Commandments, by Roissy". Headline and key line of each:

1. **Never say "I love you" first** — don't rob her of the satisfaction of earning your love.
2. **Make her jealous** — jealousy excites; no girl wants a man no other woman wants.
3. **Your mission, not your woman, is your priority** — women want to subordinate themselves to a worthy purpose, not to be a man's everything.
4. **Don't play by her rules** — polarity is the core of a healthy relationship.
5. **Adhere to the golden ratio** — give her 2/3 of everything she gives you.
6. **Keep her guessing** — she doesn't want passion security; reward intermittently and unpredictably.
7. **Always keep two in the kitty** — options build confidence and boldness.
8. **Say you're sorry only when absolutely necessary** — apologies increase the demand for more. Two freebies for the life of the relationship.
9. **Connect with her emotions** — frolic in the surf of emotions rather than the arid desert of logic.
10. **Ignore her beauty** — train yourself out of awe; never compliment looks.
11. **Be irrationally self-confident** — irrational self-confidence out-pulls rational defeatism.
12. **Maximize your strengths, minimize your weaknesses** — there's a groupie for every male endeavor (except World of Warcraft).
13. **Err on the side of too much boldness** — inappropriate touch on date one beats no touch at all.
14. **Fuck her good** — own her body, own her gratitude, own her love.
15. **Maintain your state control** — "You are an oak tree. You will not be manipulated."
16. **Never be afraid to lose her** — fear is the love-killer.

See [[Dating_and_Relationships|Dating & Relationships]] for how these interact with other frameworks in the wiki; see [[Gender_Dynamics|Gender Dynamics]] for the background assumptions they encode.

### Watch what she does, then listen for what turns her on

The passage the note labels "Heartiste on Game" in [[Women]]:

> "There is a maxim among the pick-up community that if you want to know what women want it's better to watch what they do than listen to what they say. Very true. However, if you are going to listen to what a woman says for clues about her innermost desires, or read what she writes, you would do well to pay attention to what a woman says TURNS HER ON. Not what she says she wants in a hypothetical husband or boyfriend but what she specifically describes that got her horny and hungry for loving penetration."

One version in the note adds a closing line: "Any editorial commentary about the ideal man ca[n] be safely ignored."

### Status in this wiki

Heartiste is quoted in [[Women]] more than any other named voice and is the spine of that note's attraction framework. Erlin's notes quote him rather than argue with him: the entries are kept as received wisdom, not interrogated.

[[DrDynamis]] is the other dominant voice in [[Women]], the Mediterranean-stoic moralist cluster. Heartiste's pure-game framing coexists with it but does not fully overlap: DrDynamis is about how a man should live, Heartiste about how attraction works. Both feed [[Masculinity_and_Men|Masculinity & Men]].
