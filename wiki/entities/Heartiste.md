# Heartiste

Pseudonymous game and red-pill writer, also published as Roissy and on X as @CHeartiste. He is the most-quoted voice in [[Women]] and the spine of its attraction framework. His voice is terse and aphoristic, and he writes as if describing a biological order rather than prescribing one. He covers how attraction works; [[DrDynamis]] covers how a man should live.

## The Sixteen Commandments

Never say "I love you" first. Make her jealous. Make your mission, not your woman, your priority. Don't play by her rules. Adhere to the golden ratio: give her 2/3 of everything she gives you. Keep her guessing. Always keep two in the kitty. Say you're sorry only when absolutely necessary. Connect with her emotions. Ignore her beauty. Be irrationally self-confident. Maximize your strengths, minimize your weaknesses. Err on the side of too much boldness. Fuck her good. Maintain your state control: "You are an oak tree." Never be afraid to lose her: "Fear is the love-killer."

## Key ideas

- **Watch what turns her on.** Watch what women do rather than what they say; if you do listen, "pay attention to what a woman says TURNS HER ON. Not what she says she wants in a hypothetical husband or boyfriend". (from [[Women]])
- **Sexual economics.** "Eggs are expensive, sperm is cheap. Every psychological dynamic you see playing out in mass societies liberated from artificial constraints on the sexual market flows from this premise"
- **Persistence from want.** "Never listen to man-haters aka feminists who claim that women don't like persistent men. They do. Women love persistent men who are persistent from a position of want, not need"
- **Men aim too low.** "Most men can get cuter girls than they think. False psychological projection of their own sexual attraction mechanism onto women blinds them to this reality"
- **Attitude is dominance.** "Chicks dig dominant jerks, and dominance is primarily a function of attitude"
- **The bleak one.** "Success with women is more disillusioning than failure."
