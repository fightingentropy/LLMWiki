# GN3 Guidance Note 3

Guidance Note 3: Inspection & Testing is an IET guidance publication supporting [[BS 7671]]. It is not an HSE publication. In this wiki it is the reference behind the inspection and testing study pages. Check the edition in hand before relying on a chapter or page number.

## Key ideas

- **Edition.** The IET lists the **tenth edition**, aligned with A4:2026. The same IET listing gives the On-Site Guide as ninth edition, also aligned with A4. (from [[Electrical Evidence Review - September 2026]])
- **Correction, 22 September 2026:** the earlier wiki described GN3 as fifth edition, which mixed it up with fifth-edition in-service equipment material. Its chapter map, PAT forms, exam format and page references were therefore unreliable, and they have been withdrawn from this summary.
- **Source gap.** The Electrics Webinar 4 summaries on inspection and testing demonstration and on PAT testing cannot currently be found. They are not evidence for current GN3 page numbers.

## A useful study workflow

Start with the contents and index of the edition actually supplied for the course. For each test, identify its purpose, required safety state, equipment, applicable limits and recording requirements.

Link questions to [[Inspection and Testing Fundamentals]], [[Continuity Testing]], [[Safe Isolation]] and [[Test Equipment Standards]]. Check any detailed instructions against the actual book and supervised training.

## Appliance maintenance is a separate subject

For appliance maintenance, follow [[In-Service Testing]] and [[PAT Testing]] as a separate subject from GN3.
