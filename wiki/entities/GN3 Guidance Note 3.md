# GN3 Guidance Note 3

Guidance Note 3: Inspection & Testing is an **IET** guidance publication supporting [[BS 7671]]. It is not an HSE publication.

## Edition correction

The [IET publications list](https://electrical.theiet.org/guidance-and-codes-of-practice/publications-by-category/amendment-42026-to-bs-76712018-iet-wiring-regulations/bs-7671-and-guidance/) lists the **tenth edition**, aligned with A4:2026. The earlier wiki mixed GN3 with fifth-edition in-service equipment material. Its chapter map, PAT forms, exam format and page references were consequently unreliable and have been withdrawn from this summary.

## A useful study workflow

Start with the contents and index of the edition actually supplied for the course. For each test, identify its purpose, required safety state, equipment, applicable limits and recording requirements. Link questions to [[Inspection and Testing Fundamentals]], [[Continuity Testing]], [[Safe Isolation]] and [[Test Equipment Standards]], while checking any detailed instructions against the actual book and supervised training.

For appliance maintenance, follow [[In-Service Testing]] and [[PAT Testing]] as a separate subject. The missing webinar transcripts are recorded in the source gaps above; they are not evidence for current GN3 page numbers.
