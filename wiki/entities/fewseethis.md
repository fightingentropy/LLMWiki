# fewseethis

Crypto-twitter commentator (@fewseethis), quoted in [[CT|Crypto Takes]], Erlin's archive of crypto-twitter posts. The handle is the archive's source for the framing that cascades are a secondary effect of price moves in levered environments, not the primary cause.

## Key ideas

### Cascades are a result, not a cause

A cascade follows prices falling in a levered environment. Once the mechanical bounce is over, prices resume the direction they were already heading, up or down.

> "the simple truth is that cascades occur as a RESULT of prices going down within a levered environment. in other words a cascade is a secondary effect. then — outside of a mechanical bounce — prices can resume wherever they were going in the first place: up or down." (from [[CT]])

### A related view in the same note

[[CT]] also quotes Alex Krüger, who studied altcoin crashes of more than 25% in a day using ADA as the dataset:

> "Conclusion: crashes may come in the middle of a bull run, at the top, or at the bottom. That is right. There's no signal."
