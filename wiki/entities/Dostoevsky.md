# Fyodor Dostoevsky

Russian novelist (1821–1881). He is one of the most-quoted authors in [[Quotes]], Erlin's quote collection. The notes return to him for lines about self-betrayal, the cost of lying to oneself, and the pathology of overthinking.

## Key ideas

### Cowardice and self-betrayal

- **On cowardice:** "Man has it all in his hands, and it all slips through his fingers from sheer cowardice." The note also holds a shorter wording of the same line, unattributed: "Man has it all in his hands and it slips away out of sheer cowardice."
- **On self-betrayal:** "Your worst sin is that you betrayed yourself for nothing."

### Lying to oneself

- **On lying to oneself:**

  > A man who lies to himself, and believes his own lies becomes unable to recognise truth, either in himself or in anyone else, and he ends up losing respect for himself and for others. When he has no respect for anyone, he can no longer love, and, in order to divert himself, having no love in him, he yields to his impulses, indulges in the lowest forms of pleasure, and behaves in the end like an animal. And it all comes from lying - lying to others and to yourself.

### Overthinking

- **On overthinking:** "I swear to you that to think too much is a disease, a real, actual disease."

### Human naivety

- **On human naivety** (*The Brothers Karamazov*): "As a general rule, people, even the wicked, are much more naive and simple-hearted than we suppose. And we ourselves are, too."

### Last words

- **To his wife Anya:** "Remember, Anya, I have always loved you passionately and have never been unfaithful to you ever, even in my thoughts." The note gives these as his last words to her.

## Where the wiki uses him

- **Self-betrayal as worst sin:** a recurring theme across [[Quotes]], called out in the themes section at the bottom of that page. It ties into [[Self-Improvement]]'s line "Man's greatest burden is unfulfilled potential" and [[Mental Health]]'s shame-as-parasite framing.
- **Overthinking as disease:** a direct match for [[Self-Improvement]]'s "aim, fire, correct later" and the enthusiasm-gap observation that dumber-but-faster people lap overthinkers ("You are getting lapped by people 50% dumber than you because they don't overthink shit").
- **Cowardice as the default mode:** mirrors the [[Quotes]] cluster "Anxiety is what happens when your brains are bigger than your balls" and Napoleon's line "Courage cannot be counterfeited, It is one virtue that escapes hypocrisy."
