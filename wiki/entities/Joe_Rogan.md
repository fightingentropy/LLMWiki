# Joe Rogan

Comedian, commentator and host of The Joe Rogan Experience (JRE), the dominant long-form podcast in Western media. Guests span scientists, fighters, comedians, politicians and A-list actors. He is in this wiki through the saved JRE leads below and as a point of comparison for other podcasters.

## Key ideas

### Role in the media stack

- JRE is one of the few remaining rooms where mainstream celebrities sit for 2–3 hours unscripted.
- That length is load-bearing: it collapses the PR-scaffolded interview format the legacy press relies on.

### Place among podcasters

Rogan sits in the same constellation as [[Andrew Huberman]] (a frequent guest) and [[Lex Fridman]], but occupies a different cultural slot: generalist-curious rather than neuroscience-optimizer or AI-intellectual. Related discussion: [[Masculinity_and_Men|Masculinity & Men]] and [[Political Commentary]].

A line in [[Funny]] points at one JRE episode: "Salvador Dali biggest simp ever. JRE 1851 about 3 hour mark (5 min in)".
