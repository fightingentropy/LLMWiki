# Naval Ravikant

Entrepreneur and aphorist (AngelList co-founder, @naval on Twitter). Erlin's notes keep his one-liners on agency, freedom, and intention in [[Quotes]].

## Key ideas

- **On intelligence:** "The only real measure of intelligence is if you can get what you want from life." The note holds this line in two wordings. One is unattributed. The one credited to @naval reads "The only real measure of intelligence is if you get what you want out of life."
- **On intent:** "The people you most want to impress can read your intentions."
- **On a taste of freedom:** "Once you've truly controlled your own fate, for better or for worse, you'll never let anyone else tell you what to do. A taste of freedom can make you unemployable."
- **On projection:** "Ultimately, you attract the complement of what you [project](https://x.com/naval/status/1893500759947293126)." In the note, the word "project" links to a post by @naval.

## Why he matters to the wiki

- **Intelligence is outcome, not IQ.** Anchors the high-agency framing in [[Self_Improvement|Self-Improvement]]. It matches "Most successful people I've met are not that smart. They just move fast, take risks and work a lot" elsewhere in [[Quotes]].
- **Freedom as unemployability.** Fits the freedom-and-choice-architecture thread in [[Philosophy_and_Meaning|Philosophy & Meaning]] ("I've never come back from holiday feeling relaxed"). It also fits the [[Trading_Psychology|Trading Psychology]] observation that trading attracts people with "disdain for authority."
- **Intent is readable.** Sits against the [[Dating_and_Relationships|Dating & Relationships]] and [[Masculinity_and_Men|Masculinity & Men]] theme that internal state leaks out regardless of performance.
- **Projection becomes attraction.** The "complement of what you project" line maps onto self-labeling in [[Self_Improvement|Self-Improvement]] and onto the attractor-state observation in [[Dating_and_Relationships|Dating & Relationships]].
