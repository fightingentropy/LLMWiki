# Zhu Su

Twitter: @zhusu. Co-founder of Three Arrows Capital (3AC), the crypto hedge fund that collapsed in 2022 during the LUNA/Celsius cascade. Subsequently launched OPNX (a claims-trading exchange) with his 3AC co-founder Kyle Davies. High-profile and controversial figure in crypto; his takes remain widely read. This wiki holds one of his posts, on exit psychology.

## Key ideas

### Selling the top is more dangerous than selling on the way down

> "In my experience, I've found that it is often more dangerous to sell the top than to sell on the way down. This is because the euphoria associated with selling the top can lead to early rebidding as well as a sense of overconfidence." (from [[X-Bookmarks]])

- The same post adds: "I suspect Garrett having made 9 figs on 10/10, succumbed to a version of this hubris as well."
- The failure mode he names is the euphoric top-sell that leads to premature re-entry.
- The point pairs with [[Pentosh1]]’s "take life-changing money off the table" discipline. Both are about exit psychology, but Zhu frames the specific failure mode.
- [[Crypto Trading]] cites him directly on top-selling psychology.
