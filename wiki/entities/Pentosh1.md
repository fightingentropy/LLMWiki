# Pentosh1

Twitter: @Pentosh1. Long-tenure crypto trader. In one saved post he says he has "woken up and traded in this market nearly every day, for 2,500+ days straight." His recurring message is to take life-changing money off the table and to turn fearful in the back half of a bull run. His posts are saved in [[CT|Crypto Takes]].

## Key ideas

Quotes are verbatim from [[CT|Crypto Takes]].

### Take money off the table

From [a post he opens as "something useful"](https://x.com/Pentosh1/status/1740042952108388405), also cited in [[Trading_Psychology|Trading Psychology]]:

> "If you make life changing money. Take it out of the market. Many of you will make life changing money, but few will keep it … One day the music stops, and you won't have a chair."

- He grounds it in experience: "I have seen or felt every emotion, have seen the same narratives, the same charts play out 1000+ times, the same psychology over and over."
- The edge belongs to others: "There are people with more information than you have, better systems, better execution, better everything ... And over time, your money will eventually start ending up in those pockets until you are left with very little towards the end of the cycle."
- He says it is not a bear post: "It is a post on what to do when you hit life changing money. Secure it, then start over with just a bit less."

### "It was never yours"

Written [after seeing](https://twitter.com/Pentosh1/status/1779171728100339944) posts from people who "somehow lost everything on the first dip":

> "The only money that is yours is what you take out into the real world. Making money is easy. Keeping money isn't."

- "As long as you keep all your money on the table the odds of losing it eventually are around 100%. Like a gambling addict who always wants to play another hand."
- "One day the market just never bounces again and your wait to sell on a bounce they never comes or chase your previous ath value. Then you're down 70-90%"
- "When the time comes. Take the money from the casino, or someone else will take it from you. That's how the story ends for almost everyone. Greed will eventually cost you everything. Unless you learn to defeat it"

### Back half of the bull run means fear

From [a post on where the largest gains come](https://twitter.com/Pentosh1/status/1779586050597249472):

> "The largest gains come in the back half of the bull run as greed completely takes over. The only way to survive is fear at that point."

- "Be fearful, when others are greedy. Greed is the parabola you sell into little by little."

### Dilution kills bulls

The same post continues:

> "Once there are too many bulls, there's no-one left to buy. Then the market dies from dilution … The end simply comes when the dilution exceeds the amount of capital inflows."

- "Dilution is the bull market killer. Supply is infinite in this market as we dream up a million new coins a day"

### The market forces participation

> "The markets job is to force participation on your end. it moves up to force you to want to buy and down to force you to want to sell … it moves when people are offsides, forcing their participation"

- The post goes on: "there is a lot of game/liquidity theory / people are being forced to close shorts".

### Aligned views

Aligned with [[cobie]]'s "grow fast, protect slow" framing and [[CryptoCred]]'s bag-holder test. [[LomahCrypto]] saves a related rule, stolen from SalsaTekila: money withdrawn from exchanges is never deposited back.
