# Pentosh1

Pentosh1 (@Pentosh1) is a long-tenure crypto trader who says he has "woken up and traded in this market nearly every day, for 2,500+ days straight." His recurring message: take life-changing money off the table, and turn fearful in the back half of a bull run. Quotes come from [[Crypto Takes]].

## Key ideas

- **Take it out.** "If you make life changing money. Take it out of the market. Many of you will make life changing money, but few will keep it." He says this is not a bear post: "Secure it, then start over with just a bit less."
- **It was never yours.** "The only money that is yours is what you take out into the real world. Making money is easy. Keeping money isn't." And: "Take the money from the casino, or someone else will take it from you."
- **Fear in the back half.** "The largest gains come in the back half of the bull run as greed completely takes over. The only way to survive is fear at that point." Then: "Greed is the parabola you sell into little by little."
- **Dilution ends the cycle.** "Once there are too many bulls, there's no-one left to buy." Daily unlocks need ever larger inflows until supply wins: "Dilution is the bull market killer. Supply is infinite in this market as we dream up a million new coins a day".
- **The market forces you in.** "The markets job is to force participation on your end", moving up to make you buy and down to make you sell.

The same exit discipline appears in [[cobie]]'s "grow fast, protect slow", [[CryptoCred]]'s bag-holder test, and LomahCrypto's rule never to redeposit money withdrawn from exchanges ([[Traders Directory]]).
