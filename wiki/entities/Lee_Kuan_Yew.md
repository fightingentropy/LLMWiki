# Lee Kuan Yew

Founding father and first Prime Minister of Singapore, known for pragmatic governance and a focus on technological advancement. He is in this wiki for one quote, saved in [[Body]], on air conditioning as infrastructure for civilisation.

## Key ideas

- **Air conditioning changed civilisation.** The note introduces him as "the founding father of Singapore" and quotes him:

  > "Air conditioning was a most important invention for us, perhaps one of the signal inventions of history.
  >
  > It changed the nature of civilization by making development possible in the tropics.
  >
  > Without air conditioning you can work only in the cool early-morning hours or at dusk.
  >
  > The first thing I did upon becoming prime minister was to install air conditioners in buildings where the civil service worked. This was key to public efficiency."

- **Why it matters.** He saw that climate infrastructure directly determines cognitive capacity and economic output. In tropical regions, temperature management is foundational public health and economic policy. The insight came decades before modern neuroscience on temperature and cognition; the note cites no study for that link.
- **What sits next to it.** In [[Body]] the quote follows a post by @levelsio saved as "Temperature is DIRECTLY related to intelligence", a claim in a post rather than a finding.
