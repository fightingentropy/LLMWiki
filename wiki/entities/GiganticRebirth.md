# GiganticRebirth

@GiganticRebirth is a crypto trader on Twitter. Three of his posts sit in Erlin's [[CT|Crypto Takes]] note: sacrificing weekends in a favourable regime, "never deliver" as a narrative mechanic, and imitation as the threat to any successful project.

## Key ideas

### Work the weekends while the market is easy

> "You should be sacrificing your weekends to give everything you have to harvesting all possible yield out of this market
>
> Oh, you're 22 and want to have fun with your friends?
>
> You will never have such easy opportunities for generational wealth even if you live another 80 years." (from [[CT|Crypto Takes]])

This contradicts Lord Ashdrake's post in the same note, to which [[cobie]] replied "took me like 8 years to learn this toh":

> What Traders should be like: "Sit on my ass, then a few times a year make massive bank then sit on my ass again"

[[CT|Crypto Takes]] lists the pair as an unresolved contradiction: it depends on life stage and regime.

### The Hoskinson (never deliver)

> "Ironically, most bullish path for $DOGE would be for twitter to never actually integrate it into anything. Would be for Musk to tease, hint, meme, riddle, joke, that there was a master plan for years; allow people's imaginations to go wild. We call it the Hoskinson; never deliver" (from [[CT|Crypto Takes]])

The point is to let people's imaginations run instead of delivering anything.

### Imitation kills success

A reply to @knowerofmarkets (from [[CT|Crypto Takes]]):

> "the greatest threat to any successful project is its own success. success breeds imitation, and this is a mercenary industry that will ruthlessly move to derivatives of original chasing higher upside [from VCs to traders to ultimately retail]"
