# cobie

cobie (@cobie) is a crypto trader and essayist who has hosted the *UpOnly* podcast. He is on the good-traders list in [[Markets - Trading]], and [[Crypto Takes]] keeps many of his posts. Quotes come from there.

## Key ideas

- **Grow fast, protect slow.** "you act too quickly when you are trying to grow capital, and act too slowly when you are trying to protect capital". He credits a tweet he half-remembers and says "it rly changed my life tbh".
- **Sit on your hands.** To Lord Ashdrake's "Sit on my ass, then a few times a year make massive bank then sit on my ass again", cobie replied: "took me like 8 years to learn this toh".
- **The supercycle as a siren.** "A decade long supercycle bubble of specualtive cryptocoins as a siren for the market top of the multi-decade Everything Bubble." [[GCRClassic]]'s "inverse supercycle" is the flip side.
- **Late money bids new projects.** "Low conviction money enters extremely late and offsets their lack of belief by bidding only "new projects" so they can be "early"". Their first crypto bet is "an outerspace metaverse hotel in 2022". "They're fomo-capitulating into bidding things they don't understand."
- **Crypto against the old elite.** "The billionaires multiplied wealth selling sugar to children, selling addiction to teenagers, selling adverts to people that can't afford to consume." Then: "We got money too now. Are you scared?"
- **No clean bank failures.** Answering @twobitidiot's call to let the banks die: "letting them die is also letting normal people die."
- **Gratitude.** Watching old co-workers weigh every expense: "And then I feel grateful and lucky, all of a sudden money has meaning again."
- **The machines worked.** On crypto's largest crash: "Everyone died, but the machines did their jobs perfectly. A beautiful world."
