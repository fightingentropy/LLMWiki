# Rewkang

Rewkang (@Rewkang) is a crypto trader and essayist. Two of his posts are quoted in [[Crypto Takes]], and his X essay "The exponential Horizon" is listed in [[Markets - Articles]].

## Key ideas

- **The best money game.** "Crypto will continue to expand because it is vastly better than both casinos and other trading markets in terms of: 1. volatility 2. variety 3. complexity 4. social component 5. higher odds of developing an edge. It is simply the most engaging multi player money game"
- **PvP versus PvE.** "There are certain heuristics that work in PvP market conditions that don't apply as much in PvE market conditions". When traders mostly trade each other (PvP), fading and rotating work. When new money pours in (PvE), they fail.
- **Funding can outlast you.** Fading high funding and up-only price action is a PvP habit. "In bull markets funding can stay higher than shorts can remain solvent". See [[Leverage and Liquidations]].
- **New money doesn't rotate.** "new money does not schizo trade like crypto traders. They pick their favorite meme coins and just keep sending every paycheck in". Shorting a coin because it is "being rotated out of" gets you blown out, as fading AXS, ADA and LUNA did last cycle.
- **The rule.** "To outperform like retail you need to think like retail"
