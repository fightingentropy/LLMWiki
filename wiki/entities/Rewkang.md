# Rewkang

Twitter: @Rewkang. Crypto trader and essayist. Two of his posts are quoted in [[CT|Crypto Takes]]: crypto as "the most engaging multi player money game", and a split between PvP and PvE market conditions. His X post "The exponential Horizon" is saved in [[Markets - Articles]].

## Key ideas

### Crypto as the most engaging money game

> "Crypto will continue to expand because it is vastly better than both casinos and other trading markets in terms of: 1. volatility 2. variety 3. complexity 4. social component 5. higher odds of developing an edge. It is simply the most engaging multi player money game" (from [[CT]], [post](https://twitter.com/Rewkang/status/1780351547181789353))

### PvP vs PvE heuristics

> "There are certain heuristics that work in PvP market conditions that don’t apply as much in PvE market conditions
>
> - The most obvious is fading high funding/up only price action. In bull markets funding can stay higher than shorts can remain solvent
>
> - The second is the need to rotate. Understand that new money does not schizo trade like crypto traders. They pick their favorite meme coins and just keep sending every paycheck in to continue buying every day/week. Traders that short memecoins because they are “being rotated out of” eventually will get blown out just as those that faded AXS, ADA, LUNA last cycle
>
> To outperform like retail you need to think like retail" (from [[CT]])

- First heuristic: funding. Fading high funding and up-only price action is a PvP habit; in a bull market funding can outlast the shorts. See [[Leverage]].
- Second heuristic: rotation. New money picks its favorite meme coins and keeps adding every paycheck, so shorting a memecoin because it is "being rotated out of" gets blown out, as fading AXS, ADA and LUNA did last cycle. See [[Market Cycles]] for the regime framing.
