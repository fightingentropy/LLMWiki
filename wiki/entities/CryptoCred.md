# CryptoCred

Cred (@CryptoCred) is a crypto trader and educator on X. He is here for trading psychology and dry jokes about Crypto Twitter. Quotes come from [[Crypto Takes]].

## Key ideas

- **The bag-holder test.** "If I didn't have a position, would I want the exposure I have now?" If yes, you're fine; if no, act. "Active positions cost mental capital, which is a scarce resource."
- **First-cycle traders.** "First cycle participants make money because they're open-minded, optimistic, and think "What if I'm right?" instead of "What if I'm wrong?"" They lose their gains for the same reasons. "Navigating cycles requires switching between childlike idealism and sagely cynicism".
- **Bear-market habits.** Trade less when markets are bad, because "Bear market PTSD is real. Fading breakouts, rotating too quickly and being unable to hold a position, unbridled cynicism etc. are all hard to unlearn".
- **Every system pays a cost.** Trend-following pays in false starts, momentum in late entries, mean reversion in outsized moves. The expected cost is not proof the system broke. See [[Markets - Trading]] and [[Trading Evidence and Review Framework]].
- **Reset after a big move.** After missing or fighting one, skip revenge shorts and late oversized entries; rebuild the trade from scratch.
- **Retail edge.** Small accounts have low market impact, a flexible horizon, freedom to concentrate and the option to sit in cash. None of it fixes bad selection or leverage.
- **Speculation moved to AI.** "AI is the frontier for speculation, and has many more tangible use cases and impact than crypto in a much shorter time frame."
