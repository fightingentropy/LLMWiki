# GCRClassic

Twitter: @GCRClassic (also @GCRVentures). Crypto macro trader described as legendary, known for long-form philosophical takes, high-conviction positioning calls and a consistent "long degeneracy" thesis on retail speculation. [[CT|Crypto Takes]] saves many of his posts. A post from @confessions in the same note: "I have literally stopped mid-sex multiple times to open a GCR tweet notification."

## Key ideas

Quotes below are verbatim from [[CT|Crypto Takes]].

### Long degeneracy ("Generation Moonshot")

GCR treats crypto as a bet on a secular trend of desperate, lonely, jobless young men seeking lottery-ticket payouts. It is not a bet on technology. It is a bet on society decaying into gambling.

> "i'm long humans being desperate, greedy, degenerate, lonely, and trapped in the metaverse"

- The line closes a reply on why "it's too expensive for the average man to fly to Macau or Vegas", so a "decentralized casino and/or decentralized ponzi is always fastest horse when we have easy money and macro is risk on". The note places the question "And do you think there's another cycle?" just above it, and his answer opens with "always".
- As a trading instruction: "Want to do well trading over the next decade? Tap into the heart and soul of the increasingly desperate, degenerate, jobless, lonely, addicted-generation moonshot. Front run the lottery tickets, if you have the savvy to get inside of the lost souls trapped inside the metaverse."
- Under his handle the note also saves a "Generation moonshot" passage: "Young investors are less likely to approach speculative financial products as investments with underlying value. Rather, they are inclined to treat them like lottery tickets — probably worthless, but still worth the gamble on a life-changing payout."
- [[DegenSpartan]] states the same thesis as a contrast: "you think crypto is a bet on human ingenuity, financial self sovereignty and the natural progression of civilization / i think crypto is a bet on degradation of society, failure of trust and trad economies, forced speculation, desperation and human greed we are not the same".

### Inverse supercycle

The flipside of [[cobie]]'s supercycle. Crypto is the addictive financial vice that replaces Vegas and Macau during macro risk-on periods, and it also compounds social decay. The post answers @DeFifrog1's jab that "This time it's different" is the same as saying supercycle.

> been making this point in chats inverse supercycle is crypto going to 0
> - gambling one of largest human use cases
> - gen z prefers memes to growth stocks
> - crypto is most addictive of financial vices; addictions parabolic [obesity, drug use, etc]
> - more UBI, pandemics, joblessness

@avocado_toast2 adds in reply: "as income inequality is only to increase, desperation for getting rich quickly/naively will be alive and well. That's why gambling and lottery tickets don't suffer in downturns".

### Determinism

Periodic takes frame trading losses as caused by antecedent conditions, with a "find comfort" ending.

> "Our volition or agency is an illusion of our mind. Any movement of any body is entirely explicable in terms of antecedent physical conditions. The deeds of the mind are mechanically caused by preceding conditions of body+brain. From moment of conception, your history was written."
>
> "So, from moment of creation (of the universe), everything was already destined? Creator must be the ultimate forecaster, then." "Yes." "Of this, I am certain. And therefor, whatever trading losses any of you have incurred, it never could have been any of other way. Find comfort in this."

### Liquidations as wealth transfer

> "Liquidations are a forced transfer of wealth from impoverished traders who need leverage - to wealthy spot buyers."

Also cited on [[Liquidations]].

### Regulation cynicism and free speech

> "Most laws and regulations that are initially written under the pretense of 'protecting retail', are in time, coopted by powerful interests to entrench their financial hegemony. Mostly psyops; rarely accomplishes initial objectives."

The same instinct shows in his Kanye take:

> "Censoring Kanye after his initial interviews would have allowed him to remain a martyr. Free speech enabled him to reveal just how shallow his beliefs were; for those who thought he had found some enlightened truth. Allow people to speak; the market of ideas will allocate."

### New-account mental algo

Posting as @GCRVentures, he trains a skeptical pattern-match for anon handles that spawn a following on a single project narrative.

> "Here's where you need to train your mental algo. If someone arrives on the scene, not known where they were in prior cycles; spawns up ~50k followers on back of some new project. Ox + [some waifu/animal/character]. Likely a 46 year old man living in his 5th country and 6th ponzi"

### Reading markets and blowups

- **Laggard effect.** "Traders will often reach out to me, inquiring as to why crypto is showing relative strength or weakness relative to equities. I will usually tell them to wait before drawing conclusions; more often than not, we are simply witnessing a laggard effect." The saved line above it is [always have to keep in mind this cuts both ways](https://twitter.com/GCRClassic/status/1563540982305501186).
- **Macro unwind.** "celsius, terraform, etc are just inevitable blowups of a macro unwinding, long prophesied by a handful of augurs." He adds the boomer meme about the tide going out and seeing who is swimming naked: "many such cases you will survive". Insilico replied: "I always swim naked."
- **SBF.** Replying to @tier10k: "At his core, SBF couldn't handle being less powerful than CZ, after years of blood feuding. Fundamentally narcissistic, a decade of abusing stimulants sapped any remaining empathy he might have otherwise had. Entire thing was a video game to him, so his only pivot was bet more."
- **1996.** "Throwback to 1996; Mad Cow disease threatening to infect all of Britain, Greenspan questioning if stocks were overvalued, Bill Clinton began having sexual relations with his intern, and Taiwan's 1st presidential election led to the Taiwan Strait Crisis. Time is a flat circle…"
- **Street-corner anecdote.** "saw a disheveled bearded old man howling on the street corner late at night; went over to give him some money, assuming he was homeless and schizophrenic. he told me he had already made hundreds of millions in the dotcom bubble, but that insulin sensitivity was the tree of life."
