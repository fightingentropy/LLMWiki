# GCRClassic

GCR (@GCRClassic, also @GCRVentures) is a crypto macro trader known for high-conviction calls and long philosophical posts. His core thesis is that crypto is a bet on retail desperation, not on technology. One fan in [[Crypto Takes]]: "I have literally stopped mid-sex multiple times to open a GCR tweet notification." Quotes come from there.

## Key ideas

- **Long degeneracy.** "i'm long humans being desperate, greedy, degenerate, lonely, and trapped in the metaverse". As a trading instruction: "Front run the lottery tickets, if you have the savvy to get inside of the lost souls trapped inside the metaverse".
- **Inverse supercycle.** The flip side of [[cobie]]'s supercycle: crypto as the most addictive financial vice.

  > been making this point in chats inverse supercycle is crypto going to O
  > - gambling one of largest human use cases
  > - gen z prefers memes to growth stocks
  > - crypto is most addictive of financial vices; addictions parabolic [obesity, drug use, etc]

- **Liquidations.** "Liquidations are a forced transfer of wealth from impoverished traders who need leverage - to wealthy spot buyers." See [[Leverage and Liquidations]].
- **Laggard effect.** When crypto shows strength or weakness against equities, wait: "more often than not, we are simply witnessing a laggard effect".
- **Determinism as comfort.** "Our volition or agency is an illusion of our mind". So "whatever trading losses any of you have incurred, it never could have been any of other way. Find comfort in this".
- **Regulation cynicism.** Rules written to protect retail "are in time, coopted by powerful interests to entrench their financial hegemony". On free speech: "Allow people to speak; the market of ideas will allocate".
- **New-account filter.** An unknown account that "spawns up ~50k followers on back of some new project" is "Likely a 46 year old man living in his 5th country and 6th ponzi".
- **Blowups.** "celsius, terraform, etc are just inevitable blowups of a macro unwinding, long prophesied by a handful of augurs".
