# David Cifaldi

David Cifaldi is an emergency-room nurse. On 20 July 2026 he slipped near the summit of Granite Peak in Montana and fell onto a trekking pole that passed through his torso. His account sits in [[Stories]] as an example of calm and endurance under extreme pressure.

## What happened

- He slipped near the summit and fell onto a trekking pole. It passed through his torso and narrowly missed vital organs.
- He assessed himself first: he could breathe, had full feeling, and was not bleeding heavily.
- He and his two companions alerted authorities by satellite communicator.
- They then descended roughly ten miles in about six and a half hours, under their own power.
- One friend steadied him on the way down. The other warned approaching families about the injury.
- Surgeons later removed the pole. The group intends to attempt the summit again after he recovers.

## How to read it

The note puts it this way: "This is a remarkable account of calm and endurance, not a model for handling impalement or deciding against emergency extraction." (from [[Stories]])
