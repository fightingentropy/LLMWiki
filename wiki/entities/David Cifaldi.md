# David Cifaldi

Emergency-room nurse whose 20 July 2026 Granite Peak accident is retained in [[Stories]]. After a trekking pole passed through his torso, he assessed his condition, used a satellite communicator with his companions, and descended roughly ten miles over six and a half hours before surgery removed the pole.

The story is evidence of calm and endurance under extreme pressure, not a model for handling an impalement or declining emergency extraction. (from [[Stories]])
