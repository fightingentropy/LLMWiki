# OxHamz

Crypto commentator on X (@OxHamz, also written 0xhamz), quoted in [[CT|Crypto Takes]], Erlin's archive of crypto-twitter posts. He is the archive's voice for a flow-based framework on crypto-equity correlation: equities can be bid by rebalancing inflows while crypto relies on new entrants, which produces apparent decorrelation with no forward-looking information in it.

## Key ideas

### Correlation needs a flow framework

> "Correlation discussion needs a flow framework. Equities can get bid from rebalancing inflows from assets like bonds (eg month end pension rebalance). Crypto relies on new entrants. Therefore equities can go up while crypto is flat/down." (from [[CT]])

The next line in the note carries no handle of its own and may belong to the same post: "It's not the de-correlation you wanted".

### Disagreement with RJ Killmex

[[RJ_Killmex]] reads the same decorrelation as crypto pricing information ahead of equities and rates. OxHamz's answer is that flows explain it without any signal. Both views can be true in different regimes. [[CT]] flags the pair under Contradictions.
