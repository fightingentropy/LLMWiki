# Autism Capital

@AutismCapital is an X account covering crypto, culture and society. Erlin's notes use it in three places: [[CT]] quotes it for the "dissociation timeline" thesis, the Thoughts note links one of its posts on the ideology gap between women and men, and [[X-Bookmarks]] holds a few of its celebrity and scandal posts.

## Key ideas

### The dissociation timeline

The thesis (from [[CT]]): people will have progressively lower self-esteem and will want to outsource living their lives to others. The account's name for it among friends is "investing in cuckification". Its examples are watching porn instead of having sex, watching streamers instead of playing a game, and watching people eat food instead of eating yourself. It ends on "The future is in human streaming, being able to delegate your life to others."

Filed in the crypto-takes note, it reads as a bet that crypto rides this wave; the passage itself does not mention crypto.

> We believe in what we call the dissociation timeline. The crude term we use amongst friends is "investing in cuckification"
>
> The thesis is that people
>
> will continue having progressively lower self esteem and will want to outsource living their lives to others.
>
> Examples of this are watching porn vs having sex. Watching streamers Vs playing a game. Watching people eat food vs eating yourself. The future is in human streaming, being able to delegate your life to others. We don't know how this idea fully manifests yet, but this will happen

### The ideology gap between women and men

The Thoughts note asks: "The delta in ideology gap between women and men has gone insane. What do you think the main cause is?" It links the word "cause" to an AutismCapital post. The next line in the note is attributed to @naval: "The state can substitute for what women want from men, but it can't substitute for what men want from women." (from [[Thoughts & Observations]])
