# TheFlowHorse

@TheFlowHorse is a crypto trader focused on market flow, structure and on-chain dynamics. His X display name is Ryan Scott (Horse). He is on the "Good traders" list in [[Markets - Trading]], and several of his posts are saved in [[X-Bookmarks]] and the bookmark categories.

## Key ideas

### Trading routine

- **Get dressed.** He says he has traded independently from home for over a decade and went through a phase of living in sweats: "Lululemon every day, Nike jumpsuit, whatever. Thought I was winning." His conclusion (4 March 2026): "That ain't it. Something about putting real clothes on in the morning signals your brain that the day is serious. I can't fully explain the neuroscience, but I don't need to." He remembers a study on how wearing a suit sharpens you and assumes it works along the same lines. (from [[X-Bookmarks]])
- **Info grabs, not browsing.** "The best decision I have made in a long time is to do info grabs on X rather than browse. I give myself a few minutes to scroll, I bookmark what I want to get back to, and then I gtfo." (3 February 2026) (from [[X-Bookmarks]], [[Bookmarks - Personal & Life]])

### Trading as a business

- A post of his that only shares a link to a long-form piece on managing costs, profits and position additions as a professional process. @paxtrader777 quote-posted it: "This is a phenomenal piece. As long as there is an open, a close, a high and a low-I can make money. So can you. Treat trading like a business. Manage your expenses, take profit along the way and learn to add to positions." (from [[X-Bookmarks]], [[Bookmarks - Markets & Crypto]])

### Market structure

- **Open interest beyond OI 101.** He opens a thread on the details that crypto "open interest 101" tweets leave out: "It's all so tiresome. Pull up a chair.. Time to cook them." Only the opening post is in the note. (from [[X-Bookmarks]])
- **Trend filters.** He argues that moving averages can be useful. The 50-day Bitcoin trend filter and the cross-sectional momentum examples in the post come from Ethan Kho quoting Leigh Drogen. Read them as bias checks for avoiding mature downtrends and finding leaders, not as proof of lasting alpha. (from [[Bookmarks - Markets & Crypto]])

### Silver

- **Thesis update (4 February 2026).** The long he flagged earlier on stream and in his group is playing out, but the metals trend trade is "likely cooked for now" and he expects range-building. He looks for silver to grind toward yearly VWAP and top out somewhere below 100. He calls the 87–95 zone "an air pocket, dead space with little structural support." Momentum buyers had been doing more of the heavy lifting than the fundamental geo/industrial bid, and after the historic run the buyer base is thinner: "A lot of longs got carried out on stretchers." He says he covers this daily in @TradingStable. (from [[X-Bookmarks]])
