# DegenSpartan

Twitter: @DegenSpartan. Long-running bearish/cynical voice on crypto. Frames crypto as a bet on societal degradation rather than technological progress. Three of his posts are quoted in [[CT|Crypto Takes]].

## Key ideas

### Two framings of crypto

> "you think crypto is a bet on human ingenuity, financial self sovereignty and the natural progression of civilization. i think crypto is a bet on degradation of society, failure of trust and trad economies, forced speculation, desperation and human greed — we are not the same" (from [[CT]])

He contrasts the "ingenuity/self-sovereignty" framing with his own "degradation/greed" framing. Same asset class, opposite theses.

### Tokens selling tokens

> "imagine if all the protocols sell each others tokens (and their own tokens) and in the end its just retail bagholding + funds with a position too large that they can never exit" (from [[CT]])

- A reply to [[CL207]].
- @Awawat_Trades replied: "I liked this post because it's nice to pretend it isn't exactly what is happening".

### Blandification of the world

> "not crypto related, but still a very interesting trend to observe can you feel it yourself? the world slowly creeps to become more cold, neutral and banal" (from [[CT]])

The post carries a link to a tweet by @culturaltutor (below).

## Links

- [@culturaltutor post linked by DegenSpartan](https://twitter.com/culturaltutor/status/1551976051860963333) — The link under DegenSpartan’s "cold, neutral and banal" line. Two replies to @culturaltutor follow it in the note: one puts the trend down to "doing away with distinction", the other to colour being scarce and expensive for most of history until the post-WW2 "colour explosion".
