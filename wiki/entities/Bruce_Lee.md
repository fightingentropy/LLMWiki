# Bruce Lee

Martial artist, actor, philosopher (1940–1973). Appears in [[Quotes]] for two distinct registers: self-talk as spellcasting, and the "be like water" adaptation framework.

## Key ideas

### Lines in [[Quotes]]

- **On self-talk / spelling:** "Don't speak negatively about yourself, even as a joke. Your body doesn't know the difference. Words are energy and cast spells, that's why it's called spelling. Change the way you speak about yourself and you can change your life. What you're not changing, you're also choosing." The note credits it to Bruce Lee.
- **On the mind:** "Your mind is like a water my friend when it is agitated it becomes difficult to see but if you allow it to settle the answer becomes clear." The note gives this as "Another comment" under the entry "On Bruce Lee's 'Be Like Water'".
- **Core "Be Like Water":** "no matter what form water takes, it remains true to itself. It remains water." This is the first comment under the same entry, where the commenter says Lee failed to mention it and that it makes the quote even more powerful. It is a commenter's addition, not a Lee quote.

### Why he's load-bearing in the wiki

- **Self-talk rewires neurology**: direct fit for [[Self_Improvement|Self-Improvement]]'s "Self-Labeling & Reality" section ("words are like spells; be careful labeling yourself 'traumatized' or 'depressed'").
- **Adaptability without losing identity**: connects to [[Japanese_Philosophy|Japanese Philosophy & Cultural Concepts]]'s kaizen (continuous small corrections while remaining oneself) and [[Philosophy_and_Meaning|Philosophy & Meaning]]'s distinctiveness-as-active-work, the same argument [[Jeff Bezos]] makes about staying distinctive.
- **Agitated-mind metaphor**: aligns with [[Mental_Health|Mental Health]] on overthinking paralysis; settling the mind as a prerequisite for clear action.
