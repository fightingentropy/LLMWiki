# OpenShip

Open-source application deployment and operations platform for infrastructure under the user's control. It is a watchlist item, not a verified production dependency.

Before adoption, test restoration from backup, rollback under failure, secrets and access controls, monitoring and alert delivery, and outbound-email reputation from a clean server. (from [[Setup]])
