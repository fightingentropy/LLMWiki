# OpenShip

OpenShip is an open-source application deployment and operations platform for infrastructure you control. It sits on the self-hosted platform watchlist in Erlin's setup note, as a product to evaluate rather than a verified production dependency. (from [[Setup]])

## Key ideas

The note lists five things to test before adopting it:

- Restore from backup.
- Rollback under failure.
- Secrets and access controls (see [[Digital Security]]).
- Monitoring and alert delivery.
- Outbound-email reputation and deliverability from a clean server.

[[Tools|Tools & Software]] and [[Developer_Resources|Developer Resources]] list it as a self-hosted deployment reference.
