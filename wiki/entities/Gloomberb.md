# Gloomberb

Open-source, keyboard-driven finance terminal with desktop and terminal interfaces. It is saved as a tool for watchlists, filings, macro data, alerts, and market research. (from [[Markets - Tools]])

## Key ideas

- **What it covers:** watchlists, filings, macro data, alerts, and market research, in a desktop app or a terminal.
- **How it is used:** it is a tooling lead, not a trusted trading-data source. Verify its data providers, timestamps, entitlements, adjustment methodology, outage behavior, and licensing before relying on its output for decisions. (from [[Markets - Tools]])
