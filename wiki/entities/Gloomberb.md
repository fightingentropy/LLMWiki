# Gloomberb

Open-source, keyboard-driven finance terminal with desktop and terminal interfaces for watchlists, filings, macro data, alerts, and market research.

It is a tooling lead rather than a trusted trading-data source. Verify providers, timestamps, entitlements, adjustment methodology, outage behavior, and licensing before relying on its output for decisions. (from [[Markets - Tools]])
