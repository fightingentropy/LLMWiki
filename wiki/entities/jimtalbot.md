# jimtalbot

@jimtalbot is a crypto trader on Twitter. His posts in Erlin's [[CT|Crypto Takes]] note split between mechanical microstructure and esoteric, philosophical takes. The [[Traders Directory]] lists him for range-expansion mechanics and Straussian references.

## Key ideas

### Range, open interest and expansion

- **Range builds the fuel for expansion.** Open interest piles up inside a range. When price leaves the range it hits the stop losses of that built-up OI and forces selling. Longer ranges give bigger expansions. (from [[CT|Crypto Takes]])

  > "you build range to generate enough open interest to cause expansion out of range when it hits the stop losses of the OI that has been built up causing forced selling. The longer the range the bigger the expansion"

  In the note the line runs on to "so yes 20k is on the table" and is followed by a "- ImperatOr" tag, so the closing clause may be ImperatOr's rather than jim's.

### Range versus trend

- **Where to trade relative to average price flips between regimes.** The post opens "you bait me and piss me off." (from [[CT|Crypto Takes]])

  > "sideways, you interact as far away from average price as possible, in trend you interact with as close to average price as possible"

### Esotericism and the herd

- **Straussian communication.** (from [[CT|Crypto Takes]])

  > "The Straussian distinction between exoteric and esoteric communication is a profound culture-neutral phenomenon. Wisdom can in fact never be gained while in the herd; it can only be found by deciphering the soft footprints of the mystics"

- **CryptoCred's reply.** [[CryptoCred]] replied to jim and @Pentosh1 with a Hegelian-dialectics line: "Hegelian dialectics are the epistemological antidote to perfunctory verbiage, specifically the type contained within your blasé repartee to my otherwise incisive commentary. Confront your self and your ontological angst before you confront the market".
