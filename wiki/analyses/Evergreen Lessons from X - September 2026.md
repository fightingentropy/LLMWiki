## Culture & Ideas

- **Persuasiveness is not evidence.** A fluent argument can support incompatible positions. Ask what observation would distinguish them, what would falsify the claim, and whether contrary evidence changes the conclusion. A reasoning heuristic, not a measured claim about personality or intelligence. (from [[Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas]])

## Markets & Crypto

- **Name the source of your trading edge.** Use four prompts: informational advantage, better analysis, behavioural discipline, and quantitative or execution advantage. For each trade, name the proposed edge and the result that would disprove it. This is a journal heuristic; categories overlap and do not establish positive returns. (from [[Bookmarks - Markets & Crypto]])
- **Do not manufacture trades to relieve boredom.** Judge a trade against the setup and process, rather than the need for stimulation. If only your mood changed, record that before adding risk. This is an attributed process principle from a post quoting jimtalbot, not evidence that a particular strategy is profitable. (from [[Bookmarks - Markets & Crypto]])
- **Keep the trading hot path simple and measure bottlenecks.** The pictured March 2023 messages attributed to Jeff favour an in-memory order book, simple feed-to-strategy handling, and choosing Rust or C++ when measured performance requires it. Separate network delay from computation; introduce shared memory or concurrency for a demonstrated need. Historical engineering advice, with attribution and venue-specific performance unverified. (from [[Bookmarks - Markets & Crypto]])
- **Mortgage prepayment is an option with conditions.** The contractual ability to repay early can let a borrower refinance after rates fall, while creating prepayment risk for the lender. Its value depends on fees, penalties and the ability to qualify for a new loan. Federal Reserve staff research explains the option in US fixed-rate conforming mortgages; CFPB confirms some loans charge prepayment penalties. Keep the concept, without the post's spread figures or a universal buy-now rule. (from [[Bookmarks - Markets & Crypto]])

## Developer Tools

- **Review production software through concrete failure cases.** Ask how the system detects or survives deadlocks, memory leaks, N+1 queries, concurrent updates, stale reads, dependency failures and failed releases. For each relevant failure, identify an owner, an observable symptom, and recovery or rollback. A reusable review checklist distilled from the post. (from [[Bookmarks - Developer Tools]])
- **Use a travel device as a frontend to home compute.** Run demanding jobs on an available home workstation and use the portable device for the interface. Private-network reachability and SSH or remote-desktop authentication are separate controls; test recovery when the connection drops. The source is a workflow anecdote, not proof of a universal hardware setup. Tailscale documentation confirms that ordinary SSH authentication remains separate from network access. (from [[Bookmarks - Developer Tools]])
- **Treat privacy as several separate layers.** A VPN exit IP does not remove every browser, account or locale signal. WebRTC candidate addresses can expose network information depending on application and browser configuration; MDN describes relay-only ICE as one application control. DNS-over-HTTPS or TLS encrypts DNS exchanges, not all traffic or identity signals. Test the actual device and browser instead of assuming WebRTC always bypasses a VPN or encrypted DNS hides every visited site. (from [[Bookmarks - Developer Tools]])

Original post links and supporting primary references are preserved in the category source register.

## AI & Agents

- **Preserve data provenance when using generated training data.** The 2024 Nature paper studies degradation under recursive training on model-generated data. The useful practice is to preserve provenance and independent real-data evaluation, including rare cases, rather than treating successive generated outputs as fresh ground truth. The experiment does not establish that all synthetic-data use fails or that a current named model is collapsing; those claims in the social post are not retained. (from [[Bookmarks - AI & Agents]])
- **Voice works well for bounded administrative triage.** Use spoken interaction for tasks such as reviewing email, discussing priorities and organizing calendar decisions while away from a screen. Capture the resulting decisions in durable text and review consequential actions before execution. Retained as a workflow idea from first-person experience; predictions that screens or apps will disappear are omitted. (from [[Bookmarks - AI & Agents]])

## Reference & Utilities

- **Recognize carmine and cochineal on ingredient labels.** Carmine and cochineal extract are colourants derived from cochineal insects. US rules require their specific names on food ingredient lists; FDA explains this exception partly through possible allergic reactions. Useful label literacy for dietary choices, without the post's insect-count, lobbying-history or blanket health claims. (from [[Bookmarks - Reference & Utilities]])
- **Molasses is central to brown sugar's character.** Molasses content distinguishes brown sugar's flavour and colour. Manufacturing processes vary, so do not assume every brown sugar is simply white sugar recoloured in the same way. Keep this as cooking and ingredient knowledge; the post's fixed percentages, calorie comparison and health framing are omitted. (from [[Bookmarks - Reference & Utilities]])
