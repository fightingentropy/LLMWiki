# Wiki Overview

Erlin's notes and saved links, kept short. The [[Wiki Index]] lists every page.

## How it is organised

- **Saved links** live in ten bookmark categories (see [[X-Bookmarks]]) and a few link collections such as [[Dev Resources]] and [[Markets - Articles]]. Each link is described once, in one line, on its home page.
- **Topics** summarise what the notes say about a subject: the ideas worth keeping and a few quotes, with pointers to where the links live.
- **People** are covered in [[People Quoted]], [[Podcast Hosts]] and the [[Traders Directory]], with a few traders and writers on their own pages.
- **Note digests** condense Erlin's original notes, such as [[Quotes]], [[Crypto Takes]] and [[Humor]], and link to the full original text.

## Subjects

- **Markets.** Start at [[Markets]] for a reading order: macro, rates, FX, commodities, crypto market structure, trading philosophy and psychology.
- **Dev, design and AI.** [[AI Agents]], [[Developer Resources]], [[Design & Aesthetics]] and [[Digital Security]], backed by the four developer bookmark categories.
- **Health.** [[Health Evidence]] explains how to weigh a health claim and lists the corrections to older notes. [[ADHD]], [[Fitness]] and [[Body Optimization]] cover the rest.
- **Electrics.** UK electrical study notes, in the order given by the [[Electrical Study Map]].
- **Psychology and culture.** [[Decision-Making]], [[Mental Health]], [[Dating & Relationships]], [[Philosophy & Meaning]] and [[Political Commentary]].

## Gaps

- Many health claims in the original notes are unproven and are labelled as such.
- Two electrical webinar transcripts are lost, so some procedure pages rest on summaries.
- Most saved developer tools have not been tried; [[Developer Resources]] has a short way to evaluate one.
