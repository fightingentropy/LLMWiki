# Wiki Overview

A personal knowledge wiki spanning self-improvement, markets, electrics, and culture. Bootstrapped from an existing Obsidian vault on 2026-04-12.

## Domains

### Personal & Psychology
The most thematically rich domain. Centres on a recurring tension between **optimization culture** (biohacking, productivity, self-improvement) and **meaning-making** (philosophy, legacy, lived experience). Key figures: [[Andrew_Huberman]], [[Nassim_Taleb]], [[Curtis_Yarvin]], [[Jeff_Bezos]]. Core themes include [[ADHD]] as an attention allocation difference rather than deficit, [[Philosophy_and_Meaning]] (entropy vs extropy, compounding small decisions), and [[Self_Improvement]] with a critical eye toward sterile optimization disconnected from real life.

### Health & Body
Practical optimization notes. [[Supplements]] (creatine, magnesium, zinc, vitamin D), [[Body_Optimization]] protocols, and [[Fitness]] approaches now sit beside [[Health Evidence]], which separates clips and anecdotes from ex-vivo work, pilots, randomized trials, and indication-specific conclusions. [[Water Filtration]] is a purchasing framework rather than a selected product.

### Markets & Trading
The most structured domain. Built around a [[Trading Philosophy]] that “truth doesn't move markets — perception does.” Includes [[Macro Trading]], detailed [[Commodities]], [[Crypto Trading]] mechanics, and a [[Traders Directory]]. The latest batch adds backtest-overfitting discipline, skeptical funding-rate results, leverage as a compressed deadline, Hyperliquid/[[TradeXYZ]] diligence, and [[AI Infrastructure Economics]] across compute demand, capex, power, and value capture.

### Electrics
Technical study notes for electrical certification. Comprehensive coverage of [[Electrical Safety]], [[Building Regulations]] (Part P), [[Safe Isolation]] procedures, [[Continuity Testing]], [[In-Service Testing]] (PAT), and the [[EIC and MEIWC]] certification framework. All formulas and regulation numbers preserved. References [[BS 7671]] and [[GN3 Guidance Note 3]].

### Culture & Politics
Observations on society, dating dynamics, aesthetics, and political commentary. [[Political_Commentary]] now distinguishes official COVID-origin positions from unresolved evidence, while [[Online Extremism and Evidence]] records how real events, studies, screenshots, and anecdotes are inflated into racial, religious, gender, or conspiratorial claims. [[Dating_and_Relationships]] and [[Gender_Dynamics]] preserve contradictions rather than treating hostile rhetoric as group fact.

### Dev & Design
The current [[X-Bookmarks]] category files contain 624 structured entries plus a few bare design leads. [[AI Agents]] organizes personal operations, voice models, browser runtimes, orchestration, and approval boundaries; [[Digital Security]] covers accounts, devices, phishing, connected brokerage tools, and cross-device control. [[Developer_Resources]] and [[Design_and_Aesthetics]] retain tools as evaluation leads rather than production endorsements.

## Gaps and Opportunities

- **Older health claims still lack sources.** The new evidence-aware batch improves the standard, but many earlier supplement and optimization assertions remain weakly sourced.
- **Bare design URLs need annotation.** Several current source links have no context, licence check, accessibility review, or durable reason for retention.
- **No analyses yet.** The `wiki/analyses/` directory is empty — no comparisons, timelines, or synthesized arguments have been filed. This will grow as queries get answered and filed.
- **Tool maturity remains mostly untested.** Many dev and agent entries are watchlist items; installation, permissions, rollback, real-device behavior, and production use are not verified.
- **Cross-domain connections are sparse.** Trading psychology connects to personal psychology; health optimization connects to trading performance. These links could be made more explicit.
