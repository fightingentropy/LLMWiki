# Technology Critique

A scattered but consistent suspicion of tech-optimization culture runs through Erlin's notes. It is not Luddite: the wiki is full of tools. It is alert to the ways tools reshape the wielder. The material comes from the "Technology Critique & Women" thread in [[Culture|Culture & Observations]], the notes on podcasts, spatial awareness, work theatre and AI in [[Thoughts|Thoughts & Observations]], and later bookmarks on AI adoption, chosen friction and agent permissions.

## Tools shape the wielder

A thread in [[Culture|Culture & Observations]] starts from a question: "why do so many young women just hate technology for no reason where does this impulse come from". The lines saved under it give three answers.

- **The tools change the user.** "People can mend and shape the world around them with tools, but so can tools mend and shape the wielder. In my own anecdotal experience, women are somewhat likelier to investigate this dynamic than men. There's a wisdom in that question that we shouldn't overlook. It hasn't been accounted for in the agreed consensus yet." The resistance is read as a reasonable question about tech optimization culture, not as irrational. See [[Gender Dynamics]].
- **The product pitch invites it.** "If you tell people that you are building a technology to replace them and that also progressively makes the internet worse, many people with cultural cache will not like it." Another line: "nearly all of my cool friends have either slightly negative or largely negative views on AI."
- **The hatred-of-capitalism chain.** "hatred of capitalism -> hatred of billionaires -> hatred of tech billionaires -> hatred of tech". And: "male dominated tech bro culture is also a contributing factor" (from [[Culture]]).

## Feeds, attention and a worse internet

- **Three tipping points.** Another line in the thread dates the decline of the internet to "the introduction of the feed by Zuck in 2011, the next was the tiktokfication of everything (google, meta, Tiktok) then the newest wave is AI slop making the entire internet much less enjoyable" (from [[Culture]]).
- **Doomscrolling as intermittent reinforcement.** The [[Quotes]] archive keeps a line from [graveair](https://x.com/graveair/status/2081453437728735561): "Doomscrolling is gambling for revelation, pulling the lever of an infinite slot machine, persuaded that after a billion worthless posts, the next one will finally justify all the others." It explains why an infinite feed survives a very low hit rate.
- **Podcasts have the lowest information density.** "Podcasts probably have the lowest information density of any medium ever created, but listening to them is a totally passive activity. Unlike reading, it requires no energy expenditure. That's partly why they're horrible for learning." (from [[Thoughts]]). A post in [[X Takes]] asks where the podcasts about building families and sacrificing for children are: "The algorithm doesn't reward those." See [[Media Critique]].
- **Spatial awareness decline.** A post filed next to @GathererSkull: "Noticing a steady increase in people who are seemingly unaware of their existence in three dimensional space" and "Just listlessly loitering in doorway and in front of things, shocked and startled when someone says 'excuse me'". A follow-up adds: "Not in a rude uncaring way either" and "They're genuinely and noticeably confused at their predicament when it happens" (from [[Thoughts]]; also in [[X Takes]]). The post names no cause; reading it as technology rewiring spatial attention is an interpretation.
- **Phones out of the room.** A [@signulll post](https://x.com/signulll/status/1926453874753175612) about an Austin cocktail bar that confiscates phones at the door: "first you feel phantom limb syndrome but then you remember what it's like when conversations actually finish instead of reaching for your phone anytime you have a gap or get a notification. i want more spaces like this." (from [[Culture]]).
- **Detachment as status.** A [provocation from @RoguesPhilo](https://x.com/RoguesPhilo/status/1922373194666623317) calls listening to music "peasant coded" and says: "Land ownership is reserved for the aristocratic gentlefolk strong enough to have a strong detachment from technology." (from [[Culture]]).
- **Distraction as slow collapse.** A long passage in [[Culture]] on a slow, unspectacular decline includes: "People will engross themselves in technological distractions and fantasy while never truly experiencing the real world."

## Work theatre and sterile optimization

[@signulll](https://x.com/signulll) supplies the core critique of the productivity-tool economy (from [[Thoughts]]):

> we've built an entire shadow economy around the simulation of work rather than work itself. the productivity-industrial complex feeds on the desire to feel like you're making progress rather than actually making it.

- The more you consume guides, planners and frameworks, "the more you train yourself to exist in a preparatory state instead of an executory one."
- "it's a kind of paralysis disguised as momentum."
- "maybe the human mind was always wired for this kind of recursive loop, & modern technology just exposed it. but it does feel like an intelligence trap—spending more time honing the system than actually using it."

[[Self_Improvement_Culture_Critique|Self-Improvement Culture Critique]] extends this to the [[Andrew Huberman]], [[Tim Ferriss]] and [[Lex Fridman]] axis: tools for performance, cut off from generativity. A post in [[X Takes]] calls them "cult leaders of sterile optimisation. Their mantra: hack your life — not live your life."

## AI and human exceptionalism

- **AI punctures exceptionalism.** "The illusion of human exceptionalism crumbles fast when you see how much of our intelligence is just pattern recognition & predictive modelling. ai exposes how much of what we call 'creativity' is remixing, how much 'insight' is regurgitation, & how much 'expertise' is just memorised [heuristics](https://x.com/signulll/status/1891192852711190634)." (from [[Thoughts]]).
- **Anthropomorphism is a weak control model.** [roon argues](https://x.com/tszzl/status/2085450979894497434) that persona framing was more useful when training mainly shaped conversational style, and gets less reliable as models are optimized for unfamiliar objectives. Treat models as optimized systems rather than inferring human motives from how they talk. This is a conceptual caution, not proof of "superintelligent minds" (from [[Bookmarks - AI & Agents]]).
- **Adoption is not activation.** [Vas argues](https://x.com/i/article/2085540776512192512) that enterprise AI rollouts form a barbell: a few power users, a larger group of shallow users, and many non-users. Nominal adoption can rise without matching output, so measure skill, workflow integration and verified output rather than licensed seats. The percentages are the author's own, commercially interested observations (from [[Bookmarks - AI & Agents]]).

## AI, work and media

- **Who is cooked.** A take in [[X Takes]]: "Non technical folk saying the SWEs are cooked — it's you guys who are cooked." The line saved with it [links a post by @Pavel_Asparagus](https://x.com/Pavel_Asparagus/status/1870202326650089804): "Ur gonna have ex swes competing with everything you're doing now, and they're gonna be AI turbocharged".
- **Knowledge workers and trades.** A [humorous reaction](https://x.com/ns123abc/status/2098088198404448537) to a claim that AI will displace knowledge work and push people into trades or sex work. Labour-market rhetoric, not a forecast (from [[Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas]]).
- **The gap between product framing and lived use.** An [AI actress interview clip](https://x.com/PiersUncensored/status/2100936178509717591) with Tilly Norwood and Tom Conti is framed as a "glitch" and may be entertainment rather than a technical demonstration. A [Hacker News "truth-bombs" screenshot](https://x.com/paraschopra/status/2100925357012738352) carries its argument in the image; find the original thread before quoting it as evidence (from [[Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas]]).

## Friction, agency and permissions

- **Chosen friction preserves agency.** [Aporia's reflection](https://x.com/aporia9n/status/2090604149595357399) on a [Sahil Bloom post](https://x.com/SahilBloom/status/2090478737573711950): outsourcing every inconvenience can also remove daily practice in navigating, cooking, improvising, asking for help and tolerating disrupted plans. Remove waste while keeping valuable, chosen difficulty. Hardship is not automatically meaningful (from [[Bookmarks - Personal & Life]]).
- **Authentication fatigue.** A [satirical inventory](https://x.com/allgarbled/status/2098092173321244946) of passkeys, magic links, codes, MFA, SSO, biometrics and sessions that still expire (from [[Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas]]).
- **Tool power widens the permission problem.** Personal-operation bots, relationship graphs, brokerage MCPs and cross-device control planes make [[Digital Security]] part of product design rather than an afterthought. Each one asks for broad access to accounts, messages, money or machines; give it a narrow role and require approval for consequential actions. Examples are under Resources and in [[AI Agents]].
