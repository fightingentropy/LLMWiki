# Market Performance Measurement

A leaderboard is an observation about accounts under a particular calculation, not automatically a ranking of comparable trading skill. The September Hyperliquid bookmark asks what the largest displayed accounts actually represent. (from [[Bookmarks - Markets & Crypto]])

## Questions raised by the source

- Is the account a protocol contract, market maker, token deployer, long-term holder, or directional trader?
- What period, starting capital, deposits, withdrawals, and transfers enter the calculation?
- Are gains realised, marked to market, or inferred from incomplete cost bases?
- Do hedges, inventory, financing, fees, or positions on another venue change the result?
- Are account labels and ownership established, or inferred from wallet activity?
- What drawdowns and liquidation exposure accompanied the displayed gain?

The retained post is a methodology lead; it does not verify named account identities or returns.

## The corrected funding-trade example

The Alex Hunter source preserves both a HYPE spot/perpetual funding setup and the author's later mathematical correction. Keep the correction attached to the original thesis. Official [portfolio-margin documentation](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/portfolio-margin), checked 2026-09-22, also describes borrowing, account-level maintenance requirements, and carry-trade exposure to funding, interest, and spot/perpetual price drift.

That is a reason to evaluate [[Risk Analysis]] alongside returns. It is not a tested strategy or a source of current account-specific borrowing terms. [[Trading_Psychology]] connects this to the distinction between a convincing public narrative and measured results.
