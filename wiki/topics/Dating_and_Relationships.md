# Dating & Relationships

The saved dating material is mostly X takes and pick-up advice. The loudest voice is red-pill: nonchalance, state control, hypergamy. Quieter lines on agency, warmth and intimacy run against it. The full quotes and links are in [[Women]] and [[X Takes]]; [[Heartiste]] holds the Sixteen Commandments, and [[Gender Dynamics]] the wider debate.

## Attraction versus retention

Looks, fitness and money attract; leadership, humour, certainty and ambition retain. "Looks get the woman’s attention. Game keeps her around." _(@LatinaCasanova)_

- "Women are attracted to men who are threatening to other men. Height is threatening." So are strength and quick wit.
- "Sometimes she's not turned on, or attracted, until you make a move."

## Nonchalance

Need repels; want from abundance attracts.

- "Banks give loans to people who can prove they don't need them. Girls sleep with men who don't need them."
- "Express sexual desire towards her, but have a level of indifference towards the outcome. Be interested, but not needy." _(@PUA_DATING_TIPS)_
- Heartiste's golden ratio: give her two-thirds of the attention she gives you.
- Tucker Max went out with no sexual goal, only to meet women and have fun, and says it tripled his results. It is the same move as detaching from outcomes in [[Trading Psychology]].

## On the date

- "The most seductive thing you can do when on a date with a woman is listen." Let her talk, build on what she says, and never state your own value.
- **Kino** is escalating touch. And: "A woman's greatest weakness is when you kiss her on her forehead".
- "Eye contact is an art. Too little, you're hiding; too much, you're hunting."

## What love selects for

- "You fall in love with the people who make you love the person you are when you're around them"
- High agency matters most in a partner. In [[Stories]], a woman on a second date hears him say he is cold, walks into a hotel, claims a lost scarf, and hands him the best one from lost and found.
- "yeah sex is cool but has anyone ever hugged you so tight that you feel genuinely loved. The greater form of intimacy is never sexual."

## Proximity and limerence

> ur coworker is not Hot they’re just within 10 ft of u 40 hrs/week

_(@Nia_mp4)_ calls this **proximity infatuation**: feelings that seem to belong to a person may belong to a schedule and a shared room. Remove the proximity and see if the attraction survives.

Limerence is intense, involuntary fixation on someone, not a diagnosis. The test is whether you know a real person or are feeding an imagined one through checking and rumination. See [[Mental Health]].

## Breakups

> "The reason men tend to take longer getting over breakups is that women spend half the relationship getting over it."

Warning signs: "when a girl is done with you she talks to you like she in HR or something"; she remakes her persona for each partner until "I did that for you" comes up in a fight; "Girls will forget the man they once loved. But never forget the man they respected."

## Marriage and readiness

- "DO NOT DATE WHILE: You're broke, In terrible shape, Your life is chaotic."
- Before marrying, talk through money, parenting, religion, family, sex, health history and politics. "Love alone is not enough."
- A reading of 1 Corinthians 7:27-28: "Do not search for someone to fill the role of “wife” or “husband”. That is not a position to be filled but a path that naturally emerges if you are activated by a specific person."

## The market

- The red-pill frame: "Hypergamy (She only dates up), Solipsism (Her feelings are her reality), Briffault’s Law (No value, no relationship)."
- "Dating is ruthless capitalism. Your market value is whatever you and your takers agree it is" _(@divya_venn)_
- @TMTLongShort argues apps spread access unevenly: a few men get most matches and the rest withdraw. The counter: "Smoking a cig outside a bar will yield you more dates than every dating app combined."

## Contradictions

- **Presentism versus front-loading.** "Every day is like a brand new day with them. There’s no past, nor future. Only now." That cannot hold alongside women spending "half the relationship getting over it".
- **The rational woman.** Slightly male-brained women are called the most attractive, yet acting rational around you means she has stopped loving you.
- **Warm or immovable.** "If you're a physically warm guy then no woman will ever breakup with you" against Heartiste's oak tree, who cannot be moved.
- **Drama or detachment.** The same notes want "Latin Novella" romance, getting back together a third time, and "never be afraid to lose her".
