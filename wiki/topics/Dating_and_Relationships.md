# Dating & Relationships

Erlin's notes on dating are mostly X takes and pick-up material, with a thinner layer of first-person observations, a few bookmarked film and video moments, and a handful of posts about the dating market itself. The loudest voice is red-pill: nonchalance, state control, hypergamy. Running against it are quieter lines about agency, warmth, intimacy and what love actually selects for. This page sorts all of it by theme, keeps the quotes as they were written, and flags where the takes contradict each other instead of smoothing them over. [[Women]] holds the raw material in bulk; [[Gender_Dynamics|Gender Dynamics]] and [[Masculinity_and_Men|Masculinity & Men]] carry the wider polarity and men's-discourse context.

## Dating culture and when to date

- **Back to Latin Novella energy.** "I have faith that Dating Culture is leaving its over-therapized, sterile, everything-is-toxic, fearful era and going full Latin Novella... Paragraph texts, ring the doorbell, get back together a third time, drama on the balcony. What else is life for" ([the post](https://x.com/sighswoon/status/1923147106014871588); from [[Culture & Observations]]). The shift it hopes for: from risk-averse therapy culture back to accepting emotional intensity as part of life.
- **Get your life in order first.** "DO NOT DATE WHILE: You're broke, In terrible shape, Your life is chaotic. First, get your life in order man." The same note gives the method: "The fastest way to change your life is to make the easy choice impossible and the right choice inevitable. Burn the boats." (from [[Body]])
- **Gen Z pessimism.** "I've dated minimally in my life and hopefully never again and I would fully expect Gen Z to be absolutely celibate based on how young people treat each other like a gender version of KGB vs CIA in dating and only in an emotionally psychotic terrorism way that is not at all hot" (from [[X Takes]]).

## Proximity, presentism and limerence

**Proximity infatuation.** "ur coworker is not Hot they're just within 10 ft of u 40 hrs/week. Called 'proximity infatuation'." ([@Nia_mp4](https://x.com/Nia_mp4/status/1869907388557340785); from [[Women]], also in [[X Takes]]). Context creates perceived attraction; remove the proximity and the attraction often evaporates. Recognise the illusion and don't build a future on it. The idea has its own page: [[Proximity Infatuation]].

**Presentism.** Several takes say women run on the present moment (from [[Women]]):

- "I've learned after many disappointing situations that you can't build equity with women. Every day is like a brand new day with them. There's no past, nor future. Only now."
- "Women are basically memoryless creatures who run entirely on how you're making them feel in the moment." The [post](https://x.com/TheDimitriT/status/1928159719148912781) continues into a gaslighting joke.
- "You can have the most profoundly intricate connection for six months straight, disappear for two weeks, and she's already met a guy whose made her feel everything she felt with you in the span of a day. Women are very fickle, very in the moment. Proximity is their kryptonite.."

These are takes, not findings, and they collide with the breakup material further down (see Contradictions).

**Limerence.** A line in the Women note catches itself: "I guess when you're young, you believe that you will meet many people with whom you'll connect with. But later in life, you realize it only happens a few times. I just realized this quote is a limerence trigger. Don't go there, Anon." The quote it reacts to is also saved with its [source post](https://x.com/dejavucoder/status/1908527202536505555). Limerence is intense, involuntary fixation on another person; it is not an official diagnosis. [Cleveland Clinic's overview](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/limerence) discusses insecure attachment and a possible vulnerability related to ADHD hyperfocus, not an inevitable outcome of ADHD or a universal biochemical explanation; the [post that prompted the bookmark](https://x.com/apaji_x/status/2091560421555614093) overstates it as a severe dopamine addiction. The useful distinction: reciprocal knowledge of a real person versus an imagined relationship maintained through checking and rumination. Persistent distress is a reason to seek support. See [[Mental_Health|Mental Health]] and [[ADHD]]. (from [[Bookmarks - Health & Psychology]])

## What love selects for: agency, warmth, intimacy

- **Love as a selection mechanism.** "You fall in love with the people who make you love the person you are when you're around them." Recorded in [[Quotes]] and, in [[Psychology|Psychology & Behavior]], as a psychology professor's words. Love as recognising your best self reflected back.
- **High agency.** "The older i get, the more I'm convinced the single most important trait that matters in your friends, coworkers, life partner, and kids is high agency." (from [[X Takes]]). The story that shows it, from [[Stories]]: "i learned something about agency when, on my second date with my now-girlfriend, i mentioned feeling cold and she about-faced into the nearest hotel, said she left a scarf in a room last week, and handed me the nicest one out of the hotel's lost & found drawer". High-agency people create solutions on the fly; low-agency people stay inside victim narratives. Agency is foundational to relationship health.
- **Physical warmth.** "If you're a physically warm guy then no woman will ever breakup with you no matter what." (from [[X Takes]]). Read literally (body temperature) or as emotional warmth; the line does not say which. Either way it is offered as an attachment mechanism.
- **Intimacy.** "yeah sex is cool but has anyone ever hugged you so tight that you feel genuinely loved. The greater form of intimacy is never sexual." (from [[X Takes]]). A [Youtube](https://youtu.be/eLj9HrKfcYE?t=525) moment is bookmarked under the label "True intimacy" (from [[Youtube]]); the note gives only the label, so it stays a prompt rather than a definition. The distinction worth holding: everyday mimicry of closeness (cohabitation, routine, shared calendars) can substitute for the thing itself.
- **Abandonment and closure.** "My therapist once said 'People don't abandon the people they love, they abandon the people they're using' and that was all the closure I needed" (from [[Quotes]], also in [[Psychology|Psychology & Behavior]]). Relationships end when one party becomes a consumer rather than a partner. Alongside it: "Real closure is being okay with unfinished endings, messy conclusions, and words left [unsaid](https://x.com/onlystresstoday/status/1891428063193292829)."

## Attraction versus retention

Two different games (from [[Women]]):

- **Attract:** being handsome, in shape, rich.
- **Retain:** being a leader, dominance, humor, charisma, wit, certainty, ambition, intelligence, decisiveness.

> "Looks get the woman's attention. Game keeps her around." _(@LatinaCasanova)_

The height reframe: "[Women aren't necessarily attracted to height. Women are attracted to men who are threatening to other men. Height is threatening. Body mass is threatening. Strength is threatening. Intelligence and quick wit are threatening.](https://twitter.com/TheEcho13/status/1765150044825821246)" Height is one entry in a class. (from [[Women]])

Other attraction claims saved in the same note:

- "Most men can get cuter girls than they think. False psychological projection of their own sexual attraction mechanism onto women blinds them to this reality"
- "Women are attracted to dominance. It's ingrained in them." And: "As a man, If you can't attract women without showing off your wallet, you most likely will never find a woman who loves you genuinely."
- Social proof: "[Most girls are not attracted to you, Until they see you with a beautiful girl, Social proof.](https://twitter.com/ShewillbeWet/status/1757674138913874347)" and "You immediately become more attractive when you have a pretty woman with you."
- Make the move: "the older i get the more i notice women just sort of wait for me to do something whenever i talk to [them](https://x.com/cxgonzalez/status/1920086375916794267)" and "Sometimes she's not turned on, or attracted, until you make a move." _(@SeduceHerToday)_
- "the [most](https://x.com/cookiecarver/status/1923462068033708356) attractive women are slightly male-brained and the most attractive men are slightly female-brained. it's yin and yang. a flicker of hardness in the feathers, a trace of softness in the steel"
- Hormones: "The hormones do change your attraction to potential partners." The note says multiple studies show women on the pill prefer more effeminate men and, off it, more masculine men, and links one study reported by The Atlantic: [Women on Birth Control Pills Prefer Less Masculine Men](https://www.theatlantic.com/health/archive/2013/03/study-women-on-birth-control-pills-prefer-less-masculine-men/274464/).
- An open question the note asks itself: does intellectually stimulating conversation destroy attraction when dating? The saved answer is a compartmentalisation claim ("Have girls you associate with for attraction and women you only speak to for intellectual conversation or work"), marked "(Is this true)" in the note.

## The nonchalance doctrine

A cluster of repeated aphorisms (from [[Women]]):

- "They like em nonchalant motherfuckers."
- "Banks give loans to people who can prove they don't need them. Girls sleep with men who don't need them. There's a big lesson in there."
- "Women get more attracted to you when you don't do what they want." _(@DatingTaxi)_
- "Order, don't ask, a girl to do something with you. Telling her she's coming with you to Bar A or Event X is, you will find hard not to notice, far more invigorating to her libido than asking her the same"
- "Lunch dates = GOAT" ([[DrDynamis]]): "Dinner n drink 1st dates aren't ideal. Lunch dates = GOAT. When u got an hr n a half while being all suited up meeting a chick while she can't even look her best or sexiest. Giving off the vibe of a dude who got shit to do n is just 'evaluating' her. leaving mid-warmup". The joke version, from [[X Takes]]: "Taking a girl out to dinner on the first dates was only possible under the zero interest rate policy. Too many dollars chasing too many bad dinners. Now that the funny money's gone, be prepared for 3-5 years of coffee dates."
- "Be the first one to reject her. Find a way to somehow indicate that she cannot be in a relationship with you. This will bruise her ego as most girls are not used to being rejected. Plus the fact that women like men they can't have. She'll eventually start to chase just to prove a point."
- "Express sexual desire towards her, but have a level of indifference towards the outcome. Be interested, but not needy." _(@PUA_DATING_TIPS)_
- "Don't send texts that say, 'let me know.'"
- "[Train yourself to take nothing personal from women](https://twitter.com/SeduceCleopatra/status/1757701978879090983)"
- "[How to make a woman interested: Have a more arousing lifestyle than her](https://twitter.com/guideforlovers/status/1757724868735373732)"
- "[Your girl is not your therapist. If you keep telling her your problems...She is going to lose respect for you.](https://twitter.com/woman_think/status/1757705153648423039)"

The general principle: need repels; want-from-abundance attracts. Match her enthusiasm with two-thirds of it returned (the [[Heartiste]] "golden ratio"). Never chase butterflies: "Don't waste your time chasing butterflies. Take care of your garden and they will come."

**Tucker Max's "love your work" model.** From *What Women Want*, excerpted in [[Women]]: do not enter a social situation with a sexual goal; tell yourself your only goal is to meet new women and have as much fun with them as possible. Removing the sexual-pressure frame improves outcomes. Tucker reports that at twenty-four, by not thinking about short-term hookups at all when he talked to women, he tripled the number of women he was sleeping with while putting in 50 percent less time per woman. A behavioural analogue to "detach from outcome" in trading (see [[Trading Psychology]]).

## On the date: kino, the forehead kiss, boldness

**The forehead kiss.** The note returns to this one (from [[Women]]):

> "A woman's greatest weakness is when you kiss her on her forehead. It gives her so many different good feelings that it literally fries her brain. She feels protected, loved, cherished, not worshiped (very important) all at the same time. Kiss on the forehead, when done right, is one of the most powerful weapons in your Game."

**Kino.** The physical layer of game, under the pick-up name *Kino* (from kinesthetic): "Subtle, escalated physical touch is without a doubt the most powerful way to spark attraction." The note points to a Substack article, [How to seduce any woman](https://open.substack.com/pub/jackedguy/p/how-to-seduce-any-woman?r=31tj3q&utm_campaign=post&utm_medium=web), and a [YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IWBNvUkcS9Q) saved beside it without a note. The specific moves the note lists:

- Ponytail: "If she has long hair, tell her you want to see what she looks like with a ponytail. Grab all her hair together at the back base of her skull. While doing that, give it a subtle pull."
- Neck: "whenever you kiss her romantically, don't neglect her neck."
- Eye contact: "[If you don't have good eye contact, she thinks you're creepy.](https://twitter.com/SeduceHerToday/status/1772836191282618813)" and "Eye contact is an art. Too little, you're hiding; too much, you're hunting."
- Touch early and often; Heartiste's thirteenth commandment puts it bluntly: "Touching a woman inappropriately on the first date will get you further with her than not touching her at all."
- "The blessing of loud environments is you can get inside a girls personal space easier because you have to be close to talk to her."

**Declare intentions.** "The best way to always stand out of the friend zone is to declare your intentions explicitly and not hide behind the bushes." On talking: "You can tell how confident a man is with his sexuality by how smoothly he can slip into dirty talk without freezing up. Most men are so conditioned to hide their desire they legitimately can't do it." ([@BonesawMD](https://x.com/BonesawMD/status/1927805029987098784)). A YouTube short is saved as "[Important thing to ask girl on date](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/YGi5Q60El1Y)"; elsewhere in the note the same short carries the question itself: "For you, what is that one thing in life that makes it worth living?"

**Conversation.** "The most seductive thing you can do when on a date with a woman is listen. Listening is an overpowered seductive technique." The note's list: let her do the majority of the talking; don't directly reveal information about yourself; be outwardly oriented; make her laugh; show you are listening with your body; ask questions that build off what she said, not rapid-fire ones "like it's a job interview"; drop breadcrumbs for her to follow and never explicitly state your value; stay present. "When in doubt, let the woman do the majority of the talking and shut up... always flirt and be physically escalate." A separate fourteen-point list, [Solid dating advice for men](https://x.com/WhySanaaWhy/status/1870047106917921003), adds: smell ("Smell hits an impression more than looks. Invest in a good cologne that's woody & earthy"), boundaries ("Women find men with boundaries super attractive than a sleazy man that's down bad for anything"), no empty silences or viva-style Q&A, and a reminder that reserved men win too.

## Heartiste's Sixteen Commandments

The spine of the Women note, by Roissy. [[Heartiste]] has the full list with one-line commentary. The ones that do the most work in practice:

- (3) Make your mission, not your woman, your priority.
- (5) Golden ratio: "Give your woman 2/3 of everything she gives you. For every three calls or texts, give her two back."
- (6) Keep her guessing: "A woman may want financial and family security, but she does not want passion security."
- (7) Always keep two in the kitty: "A man with options is a man without need."
- (10) Ignore her beauty; (11) be irrationally self-confident; (13) err on the side of too much boldness.
- (15) Oak-tree state control: "You are an oak tree. You will not be manipulated by crying, yelling, lying, head games, sexual withdrawal, jealousy ploys, pity plays, shit tests, hot/cold/hot/cold, disappearing acts, or guilt trips." Do not be dragged into her weather.
- (16) Never be afraid to lose her: "She who can destroy you, controls you. Don't give her that power over yourself. Love yourself before you love her."

Heartiste, as the note quotes him: "Success with women is more disillusioning than failure." Unattributed lines in the same vein: a "maxim among the pick-up community" that "if you want to know what women want it's better to watch what they do than listen to what they say." And if you do listen, "pay attention to what a woman says TURNS HER ON. Not what she says she wants in a hypothetical husband or boyfriend." On shit tests: "Inexperienced guys: 'What a bitch, why do all women suck now?' Experienced guys: 'There's my hook, I'm in'". "Alpha males are breathtakingly sexist, but they aren't spiteful about it, nor do they allow their cynicism to ruin a good time. They love women as *women*, not as substitute men".

## Hypergamy, solipsism, Briffault's Law (the stated frame)

> "Every single girl is motivated by: Hypergamy (She only dates up), Solipsism (Her feelings are her reality), Briffault's Law (No value, no relationship). Once you understand these concepts, it's laughably easy to predict their behaviour."

(from [[Women]]). The market version: "[There](https://x.com/divya_venn/status/1926428846066135108) is no 'dating out of your league'. Dating is ruthless capitalism. Your market value is whatever you and your takers agree it is". See [[Gender_Dynamics|Gender Dynamics]] for the broader polarity context and the contradictions this frame leaves out.

## Breakups and the signs before them

> "The reason men tend to take longer getting over breakups is that women spend half the relationship getting over it. The divergence between her ideal of him and the reality of him grows. She'll eventually be forced to admit the whole thing was imaginary and [move](https://x.com/RealDianeYap/status/1935357240611061877) on."

The same idea in prose (from [[Women]]): "After a breakup, women often seem to move on quicker than men, almost as if they had a backup plan. Women detach emotionally before they leave. By the time she walks away, she's already processed the breakup in her mind. Men, on the other hand, realize what they lost after it's gone. Lesson? Pay attention before she stops caring, because once she does, there's no coming back." Women front-load detachment; men back-load it.

Early-warning signs the note collects:

- "[Once a woman starts acting rational & like a grownup around you, That's when you know she's not in love with you anymore.](https://twitter.com/guideforlovers/status/1765696898369741267)" The note's gloss: "When a woman truly loves you, she shows it by letting her guard down and embracing her vulnerable, childlike side."
- "When a girl is done with you she talks to you like she's in HR or something."
- "The moment she starts acting cold is when you should know her attraction for you is dying and you have to do something very fast. If you ask me what to do, I'd say remove all attention and act like she doesn't exist."
- "Women are shape [shifters](https://x.com/BasedBlondex/status/1893673152682533278), they will literally change their whole identity and persona based off the guy they're into or dating.. Suddenly vegetarian, suddenly loves Football, suddenly prefers Country music. You know the relationship is over when 'I did that for you' comes up in a random disagreement"
- "Girls will forget the man they once loved. But never forget the man they respected."
- The gym version, from [[X Takes]]: a guy fails to join a 200 kg "deadlift party": "He doesn't know it yet, but I saw the look on his girlfriend's face — I know it well. That relationship is over."

A Nick Fuentes clip, "[Your girl is going](https://x.com/cocagroyp/status/2074746892521447432)", claims that status, attractiveness, athleticism and power cannot prevent a partner from eventually leaving. A cynical rhetorical position about relationship insecurity, not a prediction. (from [[X Takes]])

## Marriage and commitment

**The "before you get married" checklist** (from [[Women]]):

> "Before you get married... discuss bills, parenting styles, credit, debt religion, how to deal with family, what beliefs will be instilled in your children, childhood traumas, sexual expectations, partner expectations, financial expectations, family health history, mental health history, bucket list, dream home, careers and education, political views and whatever else comes to mind. Love alone is not enough."

**Pre-commitment scripture.** 1 Corinthians 7:27-28 (New Century Version): "If you are not married, do not try to find a wife. But if you decide to marry, you have not sinned. And if a girl who has never married decides to marry, she has not sinned. But those who marry will have trouble in this life, and I want you to be free from trouble." The note's interpretation, from [[Women]]:

> "Do not marry out of obligation. Do not search for someone to fill the role of 'wife' or 'husband'. That is not a position to be filled but a path that naturally emerges if you are activated by a specific person. The default path is being single, anything else is putting the cart before the horse. You must make peace with the uncertainly of not knowing if or when."

Two smaller lines from the same note: "Sex happens because a woman wants it. Relationships happen because a man wants it." And a marriage rule: "Tease your woman all day every day." _(@Men_Sex_Health)_

## The dating market: apps, group chats, OnlyFans, repeated games

- **Dating apps as scaled distribution.** [The thesis](https://x.com/TMTLongShort/status/2084038397685899578): apps expanded access unevenly; a smaller group of highly desired men can obtain more casual matches, some women may read that access as relationship availability, and less successful men may disengage or turn resentful. A platform-effects argument about matching and expectations, not established sex differences; its "league" and market-value language is reductive and the causal claims need behavioural data. (from [[Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas]])
- Two counterpoints from the notes: "[Smoking a cig outside a bar will yield you more dates than every dating app combined.](https://x.com/bumbadum14/status/1922760048687608045)" (from [[Women]], also in [[Quotes]]) and "dating apps dont work for me because i need at least two years of friendship charged with weird sexual tension to even consider falling in love" (from [[X Takes]]).
- **Peer-group feedback.** "[Anyone want to take bets on how many relationships have been unnecessarily ruined by 'girl group chats'?](https://x.com/cubsjaw/status/1927757347864125942)" A hypothesis about peer-group reinforcement and co-rumination, not evidence that women's group chats generally damage relationships. Group chats can reinforce selective retellings, but they can also surface genuine risk: judge the evidence and incentive structure of the advice, not the sex of the group. The joke version is a "[snapshot of the girl's group chat when one of them is dating a guy](https://x.com/FrenchOG3/status/1867306743593660455)". (from [[Women]])
- **Repeated-game boundaries.** Begin cooperatively, respond proportionally to exploitation, and restore cooperation after correction rather than keeping a permanent revenge ledger. The [source essay](https://x.com/alexeixbt/status/2083512257808417082) dramatises Robert Axelrod's tournaments and overstates Tit for Tat as universally dominant; its [follow-up](https://x.com/alexeixbt/status/2085402227489800240) adds the Prisoner's Dilemma framing. A boundary-setting analogy, not a law. See [[Repeated Games]]. (from [[Thoughts & Observations]])
- **[[OnlyFans]].** [One post](https://x.com/HakureiRyan/status/2084714667033706766) argues the platform was turned into a pornography platform by women acting voluntarily, and so disproves victimhood accounts. The note's answer: creator agency and platform incentives can coexist; neither a clean victimhood story nor a clean voluntarist story captures every case. (from [[Women]])

This is stuff that would otherwise take decades of travel and experience to break through modern conditioning, and we share in it freely, every day. A numinous feast." (from [[X Takes]]). The claim is that appearance signals health, genetics and character, and matters more than meritocratic framing admits. A claim, not a finding. An anecdote beside it: "my physiognomy has [changed](https://x.com/BlondB00/status/1882121878757847356) with the decisions I make in my life, when I started to make more aggressive masculine decisions, my features got sharper, my frame got bigger" (from [[Thoughts & Observations]]).

## Modern celibacy and the optimisation priesthood

A critique of hyper-optimised masculinity, saved in full ([the post](https://x.com/xirtman/status/1902893343014592631); from [[X Takes]]):

> "What we have here is a secular priesthood preaching gospel to a generation of men hungry for guidance, yet incapable of fulfilling the most basic biological or civilisational imperative: the creation of a family. Tim Ferris: the productivity hacker turned self-experimenting monk. Andrew Huberman: the neuroscience demigod whose dopamine maps rarely intersect with intimacy or fatherhood. Lex Friedman: the emotionless black-clad pod-priest who flirts with sentimentality but recoils at generativity. These aren't men with families — they're cult leaders of sterile optimisation."

What they sell, in the post's words, is "Surrogate masculinity. Masculinity unmoored from duty, generational continuity, or even real-world consequences... no sons, no daughters, no wives, no lineage." "Where are the podcasts about building families, holding down difficult relationships, sacrificing for children, standing up for tradition? The algorithm doesn't reward those. It rewards slick, postmodern celibacy dressed as enlightenment." The closing question: "Do you want to follow men who optimise their testosterone — or men who use it to build a future?"

The joke rebuttal, saved in the same note: "Andrew Huberman has been 'outed' for having relationships with 5 different women at the same time. He's a 48 years old man with the energy to date 5 different women. If that's not a ringing endorsement of his methods, I don't [what](https://twitter.com/francisjfoster/status/1772300104193482788) is."

See [[Masculinity_and_Men|Masculinity & Men]] for the fuller map of this discourse, including the "performative male epidemic" and "war on men" framings bookmarked in [[Top-Pods|Top Pods]], and the entity pages for [[Tim Ferriss]], [[Andrew Huberman]] and [[Lex Fridman]]. [[Self-Improvement Culture Critique]] covers the same optimisation loop outside dating.

## Dialogue as relationship characterisation

Two bookmarked scenes where one exchange defines the relationship (from [[X-Bookmarks]]; listed in [[Bookmarks - Media & Inspiration]]):

- **_Off Campus_: consent as intimacy.** In season 1, episode 4, [Garrett turns away while Hannah changes](https://x.com/onfilmdiaries/status/2065829523128467634) because seeing her is not the same as being invited to look. Restraint carries the romantic characterisation without a speech about virtue. _(@onfilmdiaries)_
- **_Titanic_: rejecting possession.** [Rose converts Cal's attempted sexual insult](https://x.com/QUEENP0P/status/2065472136748363795) into a rejection of status, possession and his control. The reversal defines both characters through one exchange. _(@QUEENP0P)_

## Contradictions across the wiki

Flagged rather than resolved (see also [[Gender_Dynamics|Gender Dynamics]]):

- **Presentism vs front-loading.** "Women are memoryless, only now" vs "women spend half the relationship getting over it" before leaving. Both cannot be generally true.
- **"Rational woman" coded both attractive and terminal.** Slightly male-brained women are the most attractive, *but* once a woman becomes rational around you she is no longer in love.
- **"Women do things for themselves"** (the AJA_Cortes corrective in [[Women]]) vs the default red-pill reading that all female behaviour is strategy aimed at men.
- **Physical and emotional warmth** (above) vs **the oak-tree doctrine** of Heartiste: "be warm" vs "be immovable". Which applies depends on context; the notes do not resolve it.
- **Latin Novella drama** ("get back together a third time") vs **"never be afraid to lose her"** and "remove all attention and act like she doesn't exist." The notes want both.
