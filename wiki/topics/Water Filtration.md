# Water Filtration

The current notes preserve two shopping leads rather than a product selection: an Oasis Health fluoride-removal comparison and a choice between under-sink reverse osmosis and a Ninja HydraSense countertop filter.

## Decision framework

- Start with the contaminants and concentrations relevant to the local water supply.
- Match those needs to current independent certification or model-specific laboratory data; a category label or unexplained score is not enough.
- Compare influent concentration, flow rate, filter age, rated capacity, and exact model—not brand-level marketing.
- Include installation, counter space, replacement filters, cleaning, wastewater, power, noise, and failure modes in total cost.
- Treat countertop filtration and reverse osmosis as different treatment approaches even when purchase prices overlap.

The Oasis ranking lacks an accessible common methodology in the retained source. Ninja's launch material describes HydraSense certification to NSF/ANSI 42, 53, and 401, but that does not make it equivalent to reverse osmosis. No product is selected or purchased here. (from [[X-Bookmarks]])
