# Markets

The markets pages grew from the trading notes, the daily brief template, a reference handbook, saved essays, an archive of crypto-trader takes and the market bookmarks. This page is the map. Read the framework first, then the asset classes, then crypto structure and the people.

## The framework

1. [[Trading Philosophy]] — the principles: perception over truth, regime first, risk before return, sizing and timing.
2. [[Macro Trading]] — the regime call, the cross-asset read, crypto-Twitter macro framings and Citrini Research.
3. [[Positioning and Sentiment]] — where the money sits, what the crowd believes, and how flows push price.
4. [[Leverage and Liquidations]] — funding, leverage and the forced selling that ends crowded trades.
5. [[Trading Psychology]] — why traders lose, variance, losing streaks and staying in the game.
6. [[Trading Evidence and Review Framework]] — how to record a claim and its outcome so a trading journal compounds.

- **Trade idea:** is it crowded ([[Positioning and Sentiment]])? What breaks it ([[Trading Philosophy]])? Where is the forced selling ([[Leverage and Liquidations]])? Is this the setup, or the itch to do something ([[Trading Psychology]])?
