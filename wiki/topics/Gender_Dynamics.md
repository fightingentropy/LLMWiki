# Gender Dynamics

How men and women differ, according to the posts saved in [[Women]]: red-pill takes and game writers, with a few correctives. They are takes, not findings. The tactics are on [[Dating & Relationships]].

## How men and women read actions

@minordissent's thread is the centrepiece:

> To men, especially autistic men, actions are disposable and inconsequential. [...] To women, it's basically the opposite though. EVERY action matters, EVERY action has a deeper story, a deeper moral weight to it.

To a woman, an action signals identity, which is hard to change; to a man, it is a mistake to correct and forget. He adds that men make the same error about systems: "The impulse that drives a guy to find some way to blame everything on the jews, is that the same one the drives a woman to get the ick and dump her man because he cried during a movie."

## Born valuable, must become valuable

"Women are born valuable. Men must become valuable". Women are valued without self-development but have less choice in the form of that value; men choose their path but are discarded if they fail. Hence, @minordissent argues, men's politics vary more. A later post by the same author recasts the bargains as "cherished property" and "disposable tools".

## Motherhood and fertility

One long answer in [[Women]] calls motherhood "an all-consuming and irreversible trajectory change": years of physical and financial dependence and a decade-long career gap. Now that women can choose, the calculus has changed, and some opt out; the same logic explains men avoiding marriage. Two other explanations for falling fertility are saved: an NBER paper estimating that early iPhone spread explains 33–52% of the US decline, and @allie__voss on careers as the main source of adult company. None of the sources tests them against each other.

## Masculinity now

Saved videos name the "performative male" and the "war on men": signals of strength and discipline cut loose from what they once meant (protection, provision, lineage), because the algorithm rewards the signal. Two archetypes win online: sterile optimisers and overt displays like Andrew Tate. @xirtman on the first:

> These aren’t men with families — they’re cult leaders of sterile optimisation.

"Do you want to follow men who optimise their testosterone — or men who use it to build a future?" The joke reply: Huberman dating five women at once is "a ringing endorsement of his methods". More on the hosts in [[Podcast Hosts]].

[[DrDynamis]] is a third voice: God, suits, lifting, Mediterranean taste, contempt for nerds and supplement-stackers.

- "Number 1 killer of men isn't "seed oils" But a fear of confrontation".
- Most men who DM him about feeling depressed are feeling "ENNUI": "A feeling of listlessness & dissatisfaction arising from a lack of occupation and excitement".
- "Respect > Adoration" for men.

## OnlyFans

@HakureiRyan argues women voluntarily turned OnlyFans from a general creator site into a porn platform, which disproves the victim story. Contradiction: histories describe it as mostly adult content from early on; its founder had adult-industry businesses and recruited adult performers. Platform recruitment and creator choice can both be true; neither settles coercion or exploitation.

## Contradictions

- **Present or planning.** Women are called "memoryless" and living only in the now, yet also said to detach long before leaving. See [[Dating & Relationships]].
- **Strategy or self-regard.** Most red-pill readings treat women's behaviour as strategy aimed at men; @AJA_Cortes says much of it is for themselves.
- **Malice.** "women are far more cunning than the average man gives them credit for, and men are far less malicious than the average woman assumes them to be".
