# Gender Dynamics

This page collects what Erlin's readings say men and women do differently, and how those differences shape the dating and relational game. It is the structural, biological and cognitive axis of the material. [[Dating_and_Relationships|Dating & Relationships]] holds the tactics, courtship notes and market observations; [[Masculinity_and_Men|Masculinity & Men]] holds the men-side landscape and the "performative male" discourse. Nearly all of it comes from the [[Women]] note, a run of X posts, game writers and red-pill one-liners. Read the claims as attributed takes, not findings. Where the sources contradict each other, the page says so.

## How men and women read actions

Central thesis, attributed in the note to @minordissent ([the thread](https://x.com/minordissent/status/2012609456329384220), [the "cried during a movie" post](https://x.com/minordissent/status/2012627222616830114)):

> To men, actions are disposable and inconsequential… To women, EVERY action has a deeper story, a deeper moral weight. "Only someone who believes X could possibly do Y."

- Men track **first-order effects** in person-land (he cried → he was sad) and apply **second-order thinking to systems** (politics, power, business).
- Women track **second-order effects in person-land** (he cried → he is weak, not in control of himself, "what does this signal about his identity") and often struggle to apply the same pattern to impersonal systems.
- Both genders hallucinate the same shape of second-order effect. Men point it at macro abstractions ("blame it on the jews"), women at micro personal signals ("got the ick"). (from [[Women]])

For a woman, a man's action signals **identity**, which is perceived as hard to change. For a man, another man's action is a data point in a policy about behaviour: correctable and dismissible. The post's own explanation is that men are "designed" to take risk and be measured by the quality of their results rather than the content of their actions, so a single action carries little moral weight between them.

## Born valuable vs must become valuable

> "Women are born valuable. Men must become valuable."

Double-edged on both sides (from [[Women]]):

- Women have the benefit of being valued without self-development, and the cost of no choice about the form that value takes (biology prescribes mothering).
- Men have the freedom to choose their vocation, and the cost of being expelled from the tribe if they fail to develop.

The same passage notes that each side romanticises the other's deal: "lazy, stupid, or otherwise defunct modern men" imagine women "live life on easy mode", while "disagreeable, unattractive, or otherwise defunct modern women" romanticise the male freedom to choose a vocation.

This drives the observation that **men's political and ideological variance is wider than women's**. Biology doesn't hand men a fixed role, so their concerns diversify. Women's political concerns cluster around the role biology handed them (child-bearing rights, family economics). The author is explicit that this is not a claim of male intellectual superiority: "we simply have more variance in what we need to care about."

**"Cherished property" / "disposable tool".** A later post by the same author (Max, [@minordissent](https://x.com/minordissent/status/1965159762024435791)) recasts traditional roles as opposite bargains: women are valued intrinsically but constrained as "cherished property", men have more agency but are valued only for their usefulness as "disposable tools". He argues that modern attempts to escape these roles produce new forms of dependence (women becoming economically disposable, men becoming consumers managed by large corporations) and proposes self-cultivation, provider status and traditional family formation as the way forward. It is an ideological compression, not a biological or sociological fact. (from [[Women]])

## Motherhood as an irreversible trajectory change

An extended answer in [[Women]] to the question "why does so much of women's politics revolve around the right to murder their own children?" frames modern fertility decline as a **rational response** to a changed calculus:

- Physically handicapped from the moment of pregnancy until a year after the birth, and the child can't bear to be apart from her for more than minutes until about two years old.
- Utterly dependent on a husband or other resource provider, and "completely enslaved to your children's needs", until the youngest is at least five.
- A decade-long gap in work experience that "permanently toasts" lifetime earnings and career growth, even with full commitment to staying home.
- Women used to have no choice in whether, and little choice in when, they took this on. Now they do, and "that has changed their calculus": even women who want children hesitate, and some opt out entirely.
- The author's thought experiment: even with a 90% chance of a 20× return, only about a third of respondents took the bet; with realistic divorce-era odds, effectively no one took it. The respondents were "autistic, intellectually dissident men" with little to lose, likely in the top decile of risk tolerance; the author asks what the average, risk-averse woman would answer.

Parallel framing: women's politics revolves around abortion rights because the risk-asymmetry of motherhood is the dominant variable in their lives; men's politics revolves around conscription, family courts and taxation because those are the dominant variables in theirs. The note adds that the same mechanism explains men opting out of marriage.

Other angles on the same calculus:

- [Careers can also solve social isolation](https://x.com/allie__voss/status/1904892220559282299) — @allie__voss argues paid work stays attractive partly because it supplies routine adult contact; where neighbourhood and family support have thinned, full-time caregiving can be isolating. A lens for designing work and family support, not evidence of what all women want. (from [[Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas]])
- [Is the iPhone Birth Control?](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35310) — a June 2026 NBER working paper uses AT&T's 2007–2011 iPhone exclusivity and coverage variation to estimate that early smartphone diffusion explains 33–52% of the decline in the US general fertility rate. A new identification claim, not settled consensus. (from [[Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas]])
- [Motherhood and changing views of boys' social support](https://x.com/AntiFeminismAU/status/2092853924021797337) — a mother discusses equal treatment, rhetoric about men, and her concern about the support available to boys. One person's account of parenting changing her perspective; the repost's claim that every woman abandons feminism after having a son is not demonstrated by one speaker. (from [[Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas]])

## Attraction asymmetry

### Currencies

- Men's currencies: **experience and masculinity** (mastery, confidence, dominance).
- Women's currencies: **innocence and youth** (purity, vulnerability).
- "Confidence and mastery in men are magnetic, while innocence and purity in women often captivate." (from [[Women]])

The premise [[Heartiste]] puts under all of this ([@CHeartiste](https://twitter.com/CHeartiste)): "Eggs are expensive, sperm is cheap. Every psychological dynamic you see playing out in mass societies liberated from artificial constraints on the sexual market flows from this premise." (from [[Women]])

An unattributed line in the same note, in the stretch dominated by [[DrDynamis]] posts, frames the asymmetry as directional: "Energy flows towards the more perfect creature. You see chicks tryina invade male hobbies & spaces never vice versa."

### Who initiates what

> "Sex happens because a woman wants it. Relationships happen because a man wants it."

(from [[Women]]) — flagged as load-bearing: the gating variable flips at each level of commitment. Two observations in the note sit alongside it. [@cxgonzalez](https://x.com/cxgonzalez/status/1920086375916794267): "the older i get the more i notice women just sort of wait for me to do something whenever i talk to them." And @keta_mean_: "the hetero dating space would be significantly less frustrating for all parties involved if more women felt comfortable making first moves."

### Threat-reading, not height per se

> "Women aren't necessarily attracted to height. Women are attracted to men who are threatening to other men. Height is threatening. Body mass is threatening. Strength is threatening. Intelligence and quick wit are threatening."

([@TheEcho13](https://twitter.com/TheEcho13/status/1765150044825821246), from [[Women]]) — reframes status indicators as instances of a more general "threat to other males" signal.

### Yin/yang mixing

> "The most attractive women are slightly male-brained and the most attractive men are slightly female-brained. A flicker of hardness in the feathers, a trace of softness in the steel."

([@cookiecarver](https://x.com/cookiecarver/status/1923462068033708356), from [[Women]]) — attraction is polarity plus a small counter-trace, not pure type-match. Roissy's Sixteen Commandments, also in the note, state the polarity half without the counter-trace: "Polarity is the core of a healthy loving relationship." (see [[Heartiste]])

### Humour as a signal

The note's answer to "why is humor an advantage for sexual reproduction": it de-escalates conflict with physically stronger counterparties, shows creativity and pattern-matching, signals intelligence and cognitive flexibility, and signals social power and tribe acceptance ("slick talking husband gets your tribe out of mass beheadings"). Lack of it reads as too serious, too awkward, trying too hard. Companion one-liner: "Men don't tell jokes they check temperature." (from [[Women]]; see [[Humor]])

## Breakup asymmetry

> "The reason men tend to take longer getting over breakups is that women spend half the relationship getting over it."

(from [[Women]], linked to [a post by @RealDianeYap](https://x.com/RealDianeYap/status/1935357240611061877)) — "The divergence between her ideal of him and the reality of him grows. She'll eventually be forced to admit the whole thing was imaginary and move on."

Women **front-load** detachment; men **back-load**. By the time she walks, she's already processed; he only now realises what he lost. Signal for the man: the moment she starts acting rational, talks to you like HR, or stops creating drama, attraction is already dying. (from [[Women]])

- [Once a woman starts acting rational and like a grownup around you, that's when you know she's not in love with you anymore](https://twitter.com/guideforlovers/status/1765696898369741267) — @guideforlovers. The note's gloss: "When a woman becomes rational, it might signal emotional detachment. It's not about her maturity but the shift in emotional dynamics." Companion line: "when a girl is done with you she talks to you like she in HR or something."
- [Possibly why women get over a breakup faster](https://youtu.be/kPpvzOhNbaw?t=153) — a different explanation saved from a video: women allow themselves to feel the full intensity of emotion instead of packing feelings down and bottling them up.

## Sexual-market logic: polygamy, monogamy and the price of sex

Observation preserved in [[Women]] (attributed to @amalieskram, replying in a thread on tall-woman/short-man relationships):

> "Soon they will conclude that it's better to share a hot guy than monopolize an average guy. Polygamy emerging as a feminine solution to their problem."

Frame: hypergamy + legal monogamy + modern dating-app distributions produce pressure toward de-facto polygamy. The "solution" is framed as feminine-originating, not male-imposed.

Pieces that back the market frame:

- [Dating apps as scaled distribution in partner markets](https://x.com/TMTLongShort/status/2084038397685899578) — @TMTLongShort argues apps expanded access unevenly: a smaller group of highly desired men get more casual matches, some women read that access as relationship availability, and less successful men disengage or turn resentful. A platform-effects thesis about matching and commitment, not established sex differences; its "league" and market-value language is reductive. (from [[Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas]])
- [There is no "dating out of your league"](https://x.com/divya_venn/status/1926428846066135108) — the note pairs it with: "Dating is ruthless capitalism. Your market value is whatever you and your takers agree it is." (from [[Women]])
- "It's not a 1 on 1 convo. The girl is speaking to a couple guys and will pick the best one. In a sense it's a bidding war and he lost every time." (@SOCIALMECHANICS, on a man who botched 587 app conversations.) And: "Most women have an abundance mindset. Most men have a scarcity mindset." (from [[Women]])

### Slut-shaming as price floor

> "Slut shaming is a game-theoretic enforcement mechanism to ensure that the price of sex doesn't descend lower than women want it to. Which is why women are the enforcers of slut shaming, not men."

(from [[Women]]) — female-female social policing as a cartel mechanism over sexual access.

## Loyalty asymmetry

> "The most racist man you know would still save a brown man from a burning building. The least racist woman you know would laugh at your funeral if you voted wrong."

(from [[Women]], the last line of the note's own collection) — polemical framing: men's principles are tied to act-in-the-moment behaviour, women's principles are tied to in-group affiliation. The source's own editorial flourish, not data.

## Female pleasure and self-regard

- "men think women wear sexy outfits to the gym for the male gaze but in reality this is only a fraction of the explanation, the primary being that female pleasure is inherently narcissistic and we like to watch ourselves looking hot" (@peachbIvd, from [[Women]])
- "Go to any pilates class where membership is entirely female and gay and notice how tiny the outfits and how many the mirrors." (a reply in the same thread)
- @AJA_Cortes corrective: "The replies to this are a good example of men being brotarded from the redpill. They cognitively cannot conceptualize that women do things for themselves and not everything is solely done to manipulate male behavior."

The note uses this as a general corrective to male-projection error: women's behaviours are frequently **self-regarding**, not manipulative toward men. [[Heartiste]] makes the mirror-image point about men: "Most men can get cuter girls than they think. False psychological projection of their own sexual attraction mechanism onto women blinds them to this reality." (from [[Women]])

The opposite reading sits in [[Culture|Culture & Observations]]: "Gyms in London are full of women in their 20's and 30's who are never going to have children… Very fit, but childless. They crave male attention. But they've forgotten why." That take assumes exactly the male-gaze motive @peachbIvd and @AJA_Cortes reject.

## The memoryless / present-tense frame

> "Women are basically memoryless creatures who run entirely on how you're making them feel in the moment."

> "I've learned after many disappointing situations that you can't build equity with women. Every day is like a brand new day with them. There's no past, nor future. Only now."

(from [[Women]]; the first is linked to [a post by @TheDimitriT](https://x.com/TheDimitriT/status/1928159719148912781))

Lines in the same register from the note: "Girls operate based on current psychological state. There is no continuity in their actions"; "ONLY thing chicks are better @ than men is being better grounded in the present"; and "You can have the most profoundly intricate connection for six months straight, disappear for two weeks, and she's already met a guy who's made her feel everything she felt with you in the span of a day… Proximity is their kryptonite." The proximity mechanism has its own page, [[Proximity Infatuation]] ("ur coworker is not Hot they're just within 10 ft of u 40 hrs/week", [@Nia_mp4](https://x.com/Nia_mp4/status/1869907388557340785)).

**Contradiction flagged:** the same file also says women *front-load* breakup processing (long forward-looking emotional planning) and that "a woman's love lasts until she finds a better suitor" (explicit forward comparison). Presentism is not total.

## Hypergamy / Solipsism / Briffault's Law (the stated frame)

> Every single girl is motivated by: Hypergamy (She only dates up), Solipsism (Her feelings are her reality), Briffault's Law (No value, no relationship).

(from [[Women]]) — the most compact "red pill" compression in the source, which adds: "Once you understand these concepts, it's laughably easy to predict their behaviour." The file uses this as a predictive frame; it does not critically examine its limits.

## Birth control as endocrine distortion

- [Study: Women on Birth Control Pills Prefer Less Masculine Men](https://www.theatlantic.com/health/archive/2013/03/study-women-on-birth-control-pills-prefer-less-masculine-men/274464/) — The Atlantic (2013), cited in the note for the claim that women on the pill prefer less-masculine men and prefer more-masculine men off it. The note says "multiple studies" show it; this is the one it links. (from [[Women]])
- Further claim (source unclear): "Birth control makes a woman's body think it's undergoing a miscarriage every month…perpetual grief and confusion…why most modern women are genuinely insane." Provocative framing, not medical data.

## Peer influence: girl group chats

> "Anyone want to take bets on how many relationships have been unnecessarily ruined by 'girl group chats'?"

([@cubsjaw](https://x.com/cubsjaw/status/1927757347864125942), from [[Women]]) — co-rumination, selective retelling and peer reinforcement are plausible mechanisms; the source supplies no evidence that women's group chats generally ruin relationships, or that outside feedback is harmful.

- [Snapshot of the girl's group chat when one of them is dating a guy](https://x.com/FrenchOG3/status/1867306743593660455) — saved with that caption only.
- @sentientist's anecdote: "Women on internet forums are constantly encouraging one another to escalate small or nonexistent misdeeds into existential relationship threats." The example given: a pregnant wife asks "do you think of me as an incubator?", the husband answers "well, you kinda are an incubator", she doesn't speak to him for two days, and the replies say "You go girl!" (from [[Women]])

## OnlyFans and agency

A post by [@HakureiRyan](https://x.com/HakureiRyan/status/2084714667033706766) argues that [[OnlyFans]] began as a general premium-content platform, was turned into a pornography platform by women acting voluntarily, and therefore disproves accounts of women as victims of the porn industry. The note's check: the platform did support general creator subscriptions, but contemporary histories describe it as primarily hosting pornography early on, its founder had prior adult-industry businesses, adult performers were deliberately recruited, and Patreon does not categorically ban all adult content. A platform can deliberately recruit adult creators while individual creators also exercise agency. Neither fact alone resolves coercion, incentives, exploitation or victimisation. (from [[Women]])

## Open questions and contradictions

- Presentism vs forward-looking detachment (above).
- Attractive-because-rational vs rational-means-over: female "rationality" as both draw and death-knell.
- "Women do things for themselves" (@AJA_Cortes) vs the broader red-pill assumption that female behaviour is legible strategy aimed at men.
- "Men are less malicious than women assume" vs "women control men through tears, sex, drama." The note's full line: "women are far more cunning than the average man gives them credit for, and men are far less malicious than the average woman assumes them to be." (from [[Women]])
- Is polygamy-as-feminine-solution a real trend or a rhetorical device?
- Fertility decline has three competing explanations on this page (the motherhood calculus, smartphone diffusion, careers as social contact); none of the sources tests them against each other.
