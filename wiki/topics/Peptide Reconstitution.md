# Peptide Reconstitution

Reconstitution means dissolving a prepared medicine in a specified vehicle. The right vehicle, concentration, storage conditions and use-by period depend on the actual formulation and its validated instructions.

## Correction to the earlier guide

The earlier page generalized a saved [[Retatrutide]] guide to injectable research peptides. It repeated exact potency-loss percentages, a blanket 28-day shelf life and a titration schedule without formulation-specific evidence. Those should not be treated as established facts. The original text remains in [[Peptides]] and its linked captured source.

## What the label supports

The [Bacteriostatic Water prescribing label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=ccadcf46-6a6f-436b-9bbc-17e2983a335f&type=display) directs users to the drug manufacturer's instructions and notes that compatibility varies. Benzyl alcohol is a preservative, not proof of stability or a universal solvent choice.

## Evidence required before trusting a protocol

Identify the regulated product and formulation; use its manufacturer or pharmacy instructions for the diluent, preparation, route, storage and expiry. Clinical-trial material has its own controlled protocol. A research seller's label and a numerical concentration calculation do not supply these missing assurances. (from [[Health Evidence Review - September 2026]])
