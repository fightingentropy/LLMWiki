# Philosophy & Meaning

Ideas about how to orient a life: what decays, what has to be built, and what keeps a person distinct. Most are other people's lines, gathered in [[Quotes]] and [[Thoughts & Observations]]; the authors are on [[People Quoted]].

## Entropy versus extropy

Curtis Yarvin (Mencius Moldbug) recast left and right as thermodynamics in his February 2026 interview on Peter McCormack's podcast:

> "Fundamentally it's entropy versus extropy. The left is entropic, so things can just decay. But if you're building something, you need to build."

- **Entropy, the left.** Systems drift to disorder unless energy is added. The left dissolves hierarchies, traditions, standards, borders and merit: equality on paper, decay in practice. "Cthulhu always swims left."
- **Extropy, the right.** The coined opposite: rising order and capability, the way life builds cells into societies. The "true right" builds hierarchies, sovereign decision-making by someone who can act, and new capability.
- **Not conservatism.** Most conservatives "conserve yesterday's leftism". Real right-wing energy is construction.

The explainer's summary: "Left = lets the system rot (entropy wins). Right = fights rot by actively building stronger, smarter structures (extropy wins)." It is a frame, not evidence: it names a taste for order and decisive authority.

## Distinctiveness takes energy

Jeff Bezos's last Amazon shareholder letter borrows Richard Dawkins: a body left to itself reverts to equilibrium with its surroundings. So do companies and lives.

> "The world wants you to be typical - in a thousand ways, it pulls at you. Don't let it happen."

> "Democracies are not normal. Tyranny is the historical norm."

> "Being yourself is worth it, but don't expect it to be easy or free. You'll have to put energy into it continuously."

This is extropy at personal scale.

## Place, taste and story

- **Place shapes the person.** "Every space you occupy is voting on who you become" (@tishray), so where you live may matter more than a job title, "because the place shapes the plan". The full thesis is on [[Social Commentary]].
- **Taste is a confession.** "Aesthetic taste is an unconscious confession." What you are drawn to shows which parts of you are awake. See [[Design & Aesthetics]].
- **Story is the interface.** "storytelling is the ultimate compression algorithm for human attention." Get the story right "& you bend steel with your bare hands."

## Regret and serendipity

- **Regret rests on a fantasy.** "the regret only festers when you falsely believe that the alternative would've been a perfect reality." See [[Decision-Making]].
- **Collect open-ended tickets.** [[Nassim Taleb]]: when a big publisher or banker offers a meeting, "cancel anything you have planned". Work hard "not in grunt work, but in chasing such opportunities"; big cities give "exposure to the envelope of serendipity."

## Useful beats true

- "What's useful is more important than what is true." A belief earns its keep by what it lets you do.
- "Sometimes delusion is a feature, not a bug." Youthful optimism "was a useful fiction that actually propelled us forward"; losing it breeds cynicism, and "depression is a progressive narrowing of the things that give you pleasure."
- Alex Hormozi on Chris Williamson's podcast: "My goal in life is not necessarily to be happy. My goal in life is to do epic shit." The timestamped clips are on [[Youtube]].

## Freedom and time

- After a holiday: "I come back with the taste of freedom, still fresh in my mouth, a renewed hatred for work" _(@MazeDaMouth)_
- Naval Ravikant: "A taste of freedom can make you unemployable."
- The other face: "when you can do what ever you want. What do you call it, freedom or loneliness?"
- Steve Jobs: "Remembering that you are going to die is the best way I know to avoid the trap of thinking you have something to lose. You are already naked."
- Jack Kornfield: "The trouble is that you think you have time." Sam Altman: "life is half over by 23 or 24. Don't waste time."
- Carl Jung: "All addictions are a low level search for God."

## Japan gets it

Japan is one of three long trips on the [[Go Do]] list: ikigai (a reason for being), kaizen ("continuous improvement" in small steps), wabi-sabi (beauty in the imperfect), kintsugi (mending pottery with gold so the crack shows), omotenashi ("hospitality and selflessness"), forest bathing, onsen, tea ceremony, wagyu. The appeal is the package: craft, small gains, impermanence, presence.
