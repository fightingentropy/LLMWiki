# Fitness

Fitness here is mostly bodyweight training: push-ups, pull-ups and squats, plus saved clips on core, posture and lower-leg mobility. The NHS baseline and a few trials anchor it; the rest comes from the body note (see [[Body Optimization]]) and the health bookmarks. Fit any plan to your own health, and get pain or injury assessed.

## Baseline

[NHS guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/physical-activity-guidelines-for-adults-aged-19-to-64/) for adults aged 19–64: 150 minutes of moderate or 75 minutes of vigorous activity a week, plus strength work for the major muscles on at least two days.

## Push-ups and heart disease

Saved under @robkhenderson:

> Men who can do 40 push-ups or more have a 96 percent reduced risk of heart disease compared with men who could do less than 10 push-ups.
>
> Push-ups are a better predictor of whether a man will get heart disease than an aerobic treadmill test.

Correction (22 September 2026): the [study](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2724778) followed 1,104 active male firefighters; more push-ups at baseline went with fewer cardiovascular events. The 96% compares groups in an observational cohort, the top group did more than 40, and push-ups are not a general heart screening test.

## The bodyweight plan

The plan "to cover most of body":

| Area | Exercises |
|---|---|
| Most of the body | Push-ups, pull-ups, squats |
| Abdominals | Leg raises |
| Lower back | Supermans and plank |
| Hamstrings | Side squats/lunges |
| Calves | Calf jumps/calf string skipping |
| Shoulders (and abs) | Pull up bar loops, dips, L sits |
| Neck | Neck bridges and neck raises |

- **Legs without bulk.** "The key to strengthening the legs without bulking up is to keep the reps high (at least 15 reps per set). Perform three rounds of each exercise with minimal rest between each movement."
- Correction (16 October 2026): in a [meta-analysis of 21 trials](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28834797/), light and heavy loads taken to failure built similar muscle, and heavy loads gave more maximal strength. High reps alone do not prevent growth.
- A [YouTube Short](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/UNAlhoqVKLQ) saved as "Core exercises", and a [thread](https://twitter.com/TheJackBly/status/1564953697465016322): "If you want to build more muscle than 93% of men, avoid these 13 mistakes."
- "Lifting weights but never looking at the pump as it is impermanent."

## Hormones and physique

- "Testosterone is secreted when you sleep, you train legs, and spend time in the sun."
- Correction (16 October 2026): most daily release happens in sleep, and a week of five-hour nights cut young men's levels 10–15% ([study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21632481/)). A heavy leg session raised testosterone briefly but added no arm muscle or strength over 15 weeks ([study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19910330/)). In people, the sun link is observational ([study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34433056/)).
- "One of the best parts of losing weight is getting your face structure [back](https://twitter.com/hxdleyo/status/1764968381898260587)."
- [Keep a small waist](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/yF7LXLeK-js), a YouTube Short.
- A [post](https://x.com/NTFabiano/status/1926295974428766537) saved as "Exercise rewires the brain", and a reaction to a claim, with no study attached, that imagined muscle contractions built strength.
