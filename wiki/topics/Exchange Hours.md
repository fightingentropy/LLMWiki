# Exchange Hours

Opening hours for the five stock exchanges in Erlin's handbook note, in local time and UK time (from [[Markets - Handbook]]). The hours set when each regional session trades, when sessions overlap, and when news can only show up as an opening gap.

## Opening hours

| Exchange | Local time | UK time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NYSE | 9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET | 2:30 PM – 9:00 PM |
| NASDAQ | 9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET | 2:30 PM – 9:00 PM |
| Shanghai Stock Exchange | 9:30 AM – 3:00 PM | 1:30 AM – 7:00 AM |
| Tokyo Stock Exchange | 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM | Midnight – 6:00 AM |
| Euronext | 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM CET | 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM |

Correction, 15 October 2026: the Tokyo Stock Exchange extended its afternoon session to 3:30 PM local from 5 November 2024, so it now closes at 6:30 AM UK (GMT). The 3:00 PM close above is the earlier schedule.

## Clock changes and breaks

- **Asia in UK summer**: the Shanghai and Tokyo UK times hold for UK winter (GMT). China and Japan do not use daylight saving, so during British Summer Time both trade one hour later in UK time: Tokyo opens at 1:00 AM, Shanghai trades 2:30 AM – 8:00 AM.
- **US and UK mismatch**: the US and UK change clocks on different dates. For two to three weeks in March and about a week around the turn of October and November, NYSE and NASDAQ open at 1:30 PM UK.
- **Europe**: Euronext and the UK change clocks on the same dates, so the one-hour gap holds all year.
- **Lunch breaks**: Shanghai pauses from 11:30 AM to 1:00 PM local; Tokyo pauses from 11:30 AM to 12:30 PM local.

## Trading sessions

- **Asian session**: Shanghai, Tokyo (10 PM – 7 AM UK). The two listed exchanges themselves trade from midnight to 7 AM UK in winter.
- **European session**: Euronext (8 AM – 4:30 PM UK).
- **US session**: NYSE and NASDAQ (2:30 PM – 9 PM UK).

The European and US sessions overlap from 2:30 PM to 4:30 PM UK. None of the listed exchanges is open between 9 PM and midnight UK.

## How the sessions hand over

- **Asian close → European morning**: drives morning volatility in Europe.
- **European close → US morning**: US earnings and data reactions.
- **US close → Asian morning**: overnight positioning.

This handover is key for understanding opening gaps and overnight positioning flows. See [[Exchange Microstructure]] for how venues work, [[Positioning]] for overnight positioning, and [[Cross-Asset Analysis]] for how moves carry across markets.
