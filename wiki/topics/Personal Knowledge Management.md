# Personal Knowledge Management

The Brain bookmarks and this wiki serve related jobs: the Brain preserves selected references, while wiki pages make their claims, tools, contradictions, and connections easier to retrieve.

## Retention is separate from endorsement

On **30 September 2026**, the human instructed: keep only evergreen things worth knowing, then clear all live X bookmarks. For this batch, distil reusable knowledge and omit release news, hype, disposable posts and unsupported factual conclusions. The earlier September keep-all instruction is historical and does not govern this batch. Existing archives remain intact; preserving a source still differs from endorsing its claims. See [[Evergreen Lessons from X - September 2026]] and [[X-Bookmarks]].

## From source to reusable knowledge

- Keep source material intact and attach a capture date.
- Separate the date a bookmark was saved from the date its claims were verified.
- Keep corrections beside the original claim rather than silently erasing the trail.
- Give durable tools and concepts a page when there is enough context; leave unidentified screenshots as unresolved sources.
- Link reusable insights into existing topics, then update the index and operation log.
- Record counts precisely: note entries, unique URLs, and X post IDs are different units.

## Depth and freshness

The saved Seneca reading reference favours sustained engagement over endlessly switching sources. A separate bookmark observes that knowledge loses currency. Together they suggest both revisiting useful notes and checking claims that depend on current products, prices, research, or events. (from [[Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas]])

Account links, product shortlists, and reading queues have different lifetimes. A saved account portal does not establish that setup or activation is complete, and a watch-later link does not establish that its contents have been reviewed. (from [[Bookmarks - Personal & Life]] and [[Bookmarks - Reference & Utilities]])
