# Personal Knowledge Management

Erlin keeps two related stores. The Brain bookmarks hold selected references, sorted into ten topical notes (AI & Agents, Design & Frontend, Developer Tools, Markets & Crypto, Business & Startups, Health & Psychology, Culture & Ideas, Media & Inspiration, Reference & Utilities, Personal & Life). This wiki turns those references into pages where claims, tools, contradictions and connections are easy to find again. This page covers the rules for keeping, distilling and pruning that material, and the saved ideas about reading and attention behind them.

## Keeping is not endorsing

- **Current rule: evergreen only.** On 30 September 2026 Erlin set the rule for new X bookmarks: keep only evergreen things worth knowing, then clear all live X bookmarks. Distil reusable knowledge; leave out release news, hype, disposable posts and unsupported factual conclusions. See [[Evergreen Lessons from X - September 2026]] and [[X-Bookmarks]].
- **Earlier rule: keep everything.** Earlier in September 2026 the rule was to keep every live bookmark, including entertainment, disputed claims and lower-confidence leads, labelled as claims or ephemera rather than fact. That rule is historical; it does not govern new material.
- **Entry-by-entry review.** The March 2026 X export (review completed July 2026) and the September 2026 Chrome bookmark review were handled entry by entry. Durable material went into the topical notes; existing Brain notes stay authoritative where they already cover the same material.
- **Old archives stay intact.** Changing the rule does not delete what earlier rules kept. Keeping a source is still different from endorsing its claims.
- **Clearing is a separate record.** The first evergreen-only pass cleared all 70 live X bookmarks, confirmed by an empty bookmark list. [[X Bookmark Cleanup - 30 September 2026]] records that operation apart from the knowledge kept.

## From source to reusable knowledge

- Keep the source material intact and note when it was saved.
- Separate the date a bookmark was saved from the date its claims were checked.
- Keep corrections beside the original claim instead of silently erasing the trail.
- Give a durable tool or concept its own page once there is enough context. Leave unidentified screenshots and bare links as unresolved until the destination is known (for example the [unidentified "beautiful essay"](https://x.com/tope_docx/status/2096974652325142977) and the [unidentified free tool](https://x.com/SwamiMalode/status/2096651240075825292)).
- Link reusable insights into existing topics, then update the index and the operation log.
- Count precisely: note entries, unique URLs and X post IDs are different units.

## Depth and freshness

The saved Seneca reading reference favours sustained engagement over endlessly switching sources: constant movement among many books and authors can stop ideas taking root ([[Seneca]], Letter 2 to Lucilius). A separate bookmark observes that knowledge loses currency. Together they suggest revisiting useful notes, and re-checking claims that depend on current products, prices, research or events. (from [[Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas]])

A related bookmark argues that sustained reading may become a visible marker of agency and advantage as algorithmic media erodes concentration. It is a provocative thesis, not a measured finding. (from [[Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas]])

## Collecting is not using

A saved take in [[Thoughts]] warns about the productivity-industrial complex: "the more you consume these meta-tools, the more you train yourself to exist in a preparatory state instead of an executory one." It calls this "a kind of paralysis disguised as momentum" and "an intelligence trap—spending more time honing the system than actually using it." _([@signulll](https://x.com/signulll))_

Two lines on passive input point the same way:

- "Podcasts probably have the lowest information density of any medium ever created, but listening to them is a totally passive activity. Unlike reading, it requires no energy expenditure. That's partly why they're horrible for learning." (from [[Thoughts]])
- "There's almost zero alpha in listening to podcasts. You're not getting smarter doing this, it just feels like you are." _(@growing_daniel, from [[CT]])_

## How long a link should live

Account links, product shortlists and reading queues have different lifetimes. A saved account portal does not show that setup or activation is complete, and a watch-later link does not show that its contents have been reviewed. (from [[Bookmarks - Personal & Life]] and [[Bookmarks - Reference & Utilities]])

| Kind of link | Keep it until | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account portal | The account or agreement closes | Argos Pay, V12 Finance |
| Setup or activation link | Installation and activation are complete | Hyperoptic Getting Connected |
| Product shortlist | A purchase decision is made | UniFi Express 7, Hommix Sparo |
| Reading or watch queue | It has been read or watched and any durable point distilled | FT article, Elephant Graveyard video |
| One-off implementation reference | Its conclusion has been acted on | Stremio on AltStore |
| Bounded idea collection | It turns into another endless queue instead of a bounded source | Billion Dollar Tweets |
| Durable principle | Indefinitely, with periodic re-reading | Seneca on scattered reading |

Consoles and utilities follow the same logic: keep the durable entry point and drop brittle deep links. The [Twilio Console](https://console.twilio.com/) entry replaced two regional API-key deep links, and the [SearXNG instance directory](https://searx.space/) replaced a single-instance bookmark. Developer tools themselves live in [[Developer_Resources]].
