# Body Optimization

The body note is a scrapbook of training, food, sleep, stress, supplements and looks, mostly pasted posts. Its useful thread is consistency: small choices, repeated. Its supplement claims need a stricter standard, and several carry corrections; doses and interactions are a question for a pharmacist or doctor. Training is on [[Fitness]], sleep on [[Circadian Rhythm]].

## Stress, breathing and cold

- "Acute stress is good. It makes you better, stronger, more adaptive. Chronic stress is bad." The fix: "Eliminate chronic stressors & replace with acute stressors." _(@NoahRyanCo)_ See [[Mental Health]].
- Boxed breathing, and @sporadicalia's "adrenaline on command": rapid nasal inhales timed to high-tempo music, eyes opened wide on each long exhale.
- @olivesagan tried cold showers after reading that cold "facilitates fairly high levels of dopamine release". A reply: "it almost feels like taking a pill or something."
- Correction (16 October 2026): the 250% dopamine rise came from an hour in 14 °C water and was measured in blood ([study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10751106/)). Immersion trials found lower stress and better sleep but no mood effect ([meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39879231/)); the largest cold-shower trial, 3,018 adults, cut sickness absence but not illness days ([trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27631616/)).

## The supplement stack

The core, from a post signed @hubermanlab:

> Vitamin D3 supplementation: largely accepted nowadays. Creatine: starting to break through. Next: Magnesium (Threonate or Bisglycinate). Why? Well for starters, Mg can protect against hearing loss, solid data on reducing migraines & it can improve sleep.

- **Vitamin D.** The [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/vitamins-and-minerals/vitamin-d/) advises adults to consider 10 micrograms daily in autumn and winter, and some groups all year.
- **Creatine.** Correction (16 October 2026): past "starting to break through"; creatine monohydrate is in [AIS Group A](https://www.ausport.gov.au/ais/nutrition/supplements/group_a), with consistent high-intensity performance gains.
- **Magnesium.** Correction (16 October 2026): nothing here is "solid data". Neurologists rate it probably effective for preventing migraine ([guideline](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22529203/)); sleep evidence is low to very low certainty ([review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42661485/)); hearing rests on a few trials such as one in 300 [military recruits](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8135325/).
- **Tongkat ali.** "Pomegranate juice to offset oestrogen block from tongkat Ali." Correction (16 October 2026): five trials found raised testosterone, mostly in men with low levels ([meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36013514/)); nothing supports the pomegranate fix.
- **Blueberries.** "Near-instant cure for brain fog: Eat 500g+ of blueberries in one sitting." Correction (16 October 2026): 14 trials found better blood-vessel function but no effect on executive function or mood ([meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41974068/)).
- **Vitamin C.** From [[DrDynamis]]: "People with marginal vitamin C status burned 25% less fat during exercise. After vitamin C supplementation? Fat burning increased 4-fold." Correction (16 October 2026): both figures come from a tiny [preliminary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16945143/) of treadmill fat burning, not fat loss.
- **L-theanine with caffeine.** An r/Nootropics thread says it lets coffee work "without letting it raise your blood pressure or induce anxiety & jitterness". Too absolute.
- **Peptides.** @goth600: "Wired: using peptides & micro dosing psilocybin to reinvigorate neural growth". Retatrutide is a drug, not a supplement; see [[Peptide Reconstitution]].
