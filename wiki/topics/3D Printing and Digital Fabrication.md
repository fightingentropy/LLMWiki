# 3D Printing and Digital Fabrication

The September bookmarks connect custom CAD generation, printer hardware, and modular workshop storage. The shared idea is turning a particular physical need into a reproducible object.

## Source examples

- **Image-to-CAD doorstop:** a photo with a measuring tape was reportedly used to model and print a fitted doorstop. The fit is the author's report, not a tested result here. (from [[Bookmarks - AI & Agents]])
- **OpenTools Studio:** a printer/product lead pointing to [the project site](https://www.opentools.studio/), with build difficulty, components, software, documentation, and independent results unresolved. (from [[Bookmarks - Developer Tools]])
- **VAULT/Gridfinity:** an expandable workshop-storage reference whose exact model repository, dimensions, licence, and compatibility still need identification. (from [[Bookmarks - Personal & Life]])

## Questions to resolve

- What dimensions and tolerances define success, and how were they measured?
- Which source model, revision, licence, printer, and material were used?
- Does a small prototype fit before committing to a whole storage system?
- What are the print time, material use, strength requirements, and failure modes?
- Can the editable model be retained alongside the exported mesh and physical result?

These questions connect the product references to [[Design_and_Aesthetics]] and [[Developer_Resources]]. No printer or storage system has been selected.
