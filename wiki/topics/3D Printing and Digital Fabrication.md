# 3D Printing and Digital Fabrication

Digital fabrication turns a particular physical need into a reproducible object: measure the space, model the part, print it, check the fit. The saved bookmarks cover three steps of that chain: generating a custom CAD model from a photo, the printer hardware itself, and a modular storage system to print for a workshop, plus one printable robot project. No printer or storage system has been chosen yet.

## From a photo to a fitted part

- **Image-to-CAD doorstop.** A post describes photographing a space with a measuring tape, generating a custom model from the photo, and 3D-printing a fitted doorstop. The fit is the author's own report, not a tested result. (from [[Bookmarks - AI & Agents]])

It is filed with the AI bookmarks because the model is generated from the photo; see [[AI Agents]]. The measuring tape gives the model its scale; the printed part is the check.

## Printer hardware

- **OpenTools Studio.** An enthusiastic product lead pointing to the OpenTools Studio project site. The printer design, bill of materials, build difficulty, software, safety, documentation and independent results are unresolved; check them before buying or building. (from [[Bookmarks - Developer Tools]])

## Workshop storage

- **VAULT / Gridfinity.** An expandable, 3D-printable storage system presented for woodworking and maker spaces and described as Gridfinity-based. The exact model repository, dimensions, licence, print time, material use and compatibility still need to be identified. (from [[Bookmarks - Personal & Life]])

## Printable projects

- **Kame.** A compact, open-source, 3D-printable quadruped robot with animal-like movement. (from [[Bookmarks - Developer Tools]])

## Before printing: questions to answer

- What dimensions and tolerances define success, and how were they measured?
- Which source model, revision, licence, printer and material were used?
- Does a small prototype fit before committing to a whole storage system?
- What are the print time, material use, strength requirements and failure modes?
- Can the editable model be kept alongside the exported mesh and the physical result?

These questions tie the product references to the craft notes in [[Design_and_Aesthetics|Design & Aesthetics]] and the tool lists in [[Developer_Resources|Developer Resources]].
