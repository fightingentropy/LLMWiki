# Traders Directory

The crypto traders and accounts worth following, each with the idea they are known for. Quotes come from [[Crypto Takes]] unless marked, and the views are the traders' own. The "good traders" list comes from [[Markets - Trading]].

## How to use them

Watching traders is a positioning and sentiment input, not a source of trades. The Trading note opens: "Truth doesn't necessarily move markets. Perception & Uncertainty/Certainty transition does." Its checklist includes "CT Sentiment" and "Top traders positioning" (from [[Markets - Trading]]).

- **Consensus:** when most of them agree, the trade is crowded.
- **Dissent:** when they split, consensus is breaking, which is inflection risk.
- **Conviction:** position size and the heat of their language show how hard they lean.
- **Retail proxy:** popular accounts move with retail positioning.

See [[Positioning and Sentiment]] for the wider framework.

## Macro and what crypto is

- **@fejau_inc** — On the good-traders list. Known for the M2 overlay: "It's crazy how you can just overlay M2 on any chart and if it goes up you long and can outperform the smartest pod shop hedge funds filled with phd traders". [[Macro Trading]] treats M2 as a regime prior, not a trigger.
- **@DegenSpartan** — The cynic's thesis: "i think crypto is a bet on degradation of society, failure of trust and trad economies, forced speculation, desperation and human greed we are not the same". On token recycling: "imagine if all the protocols sell each others tokens (and their own tokens) and in the end its just retail bagholding".
- **@RJ_Killmex** — Channelling Hayes: "Crypto is the least regulated/ controlled market and gives the most accurate reflection of what's really going on". So decorrelation from equities and rates may be crypto pricing something early.
- **@OxHamz** — The counter-view: "Correlation discussion needs a flow framework". Equities get rebalancing inflows while crypto relies on new entrants, so equities can rise while crypto stalls. The two readings can each hold in different regimes.
- **@thiccythot** — "its interesting how price discovery moves from blunt proxies to more precise expressions with each wave". The first oil wave buys WTI as the default proxy; the second moves to Brent. Each crypto cycle likewise grows more selective on asset quality.
- **Zhu Su** (@zhusu) — Co-founder of Three Arrows Capital, which collapsed in 2022. Saved for one post on exits: "it is often more dangerous to sell the top than to sell on the way down", because the euphoria "can lead to early rebidding as well as a sense of overconfidence" (from [[X-Bookmarks]]).

## Flow, leverage and structure

- **@HsakaTrades** — On the good-traders list. Trading rules in a famous voice: "The orderflow frontruns the newsflow" (signed "Marcus Aurelius"), and "Give me a lever long enough and a fulcrum on which to place it, and I shall move the curve".
- **@jimtalbot** — Range mechanics: "you build range to generate enough open interest to cause expansion out of range when it hits the stop losses" of that open interest. "The longer the range the bigger the expansion". The passage ends "- ImperatOr", so the attribution is mixed. On regimes: "sideways, you interact as far away from average price as possible, in trend you interact with as close to average price as possible".
- **@fewseethis** — Cascades follow price: "a cascade is a secondary effect". After the mechanical bounce, "prices can resume wherever they were going in the first place: up or down." See [[Leverage and Liquidations]].
- **@CL207** — Narratives come after price: "anytime price moves 10% we will literally hallucinate a story up for it". @lightcrypto argues the market first needs "a viable schelling point narrative"; the clash stays open.
- **@TheFlowHorse** (Ryan Scott) — On the good-traders list. Writes on flow, open interest, trend filters and trading routine, including "info grabs" on X instead of browsing. Saved posts:
