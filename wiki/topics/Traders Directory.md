# Traders Directory

The crypto traders and accounts Erlin's notes follow, and how to use them. Watching them is a positioning and sentiment input: when many of them agree, the trade is crowded; when they split, consensus is breaking. The featured traders below have their own pages and saved quotes in [[CT]]; the full directory lists every other handle the notes keep.

The Trading note opens with the reason this matters: "Truth doesn't necessarily move [markets](https://youtu.be/rZ9rUh9WhwU?t=1876). Perception & Uncertainty/Certainty transition does." Its checklist of things to look for includes "CT Sentiment" and "Top traders positioning", next to macro, liquidations, catalysts, stablecoin dominance and yields, [[Funding Rates]] and options positioning (from [[Markets - Trading]]).

## Monitoring framework

### What to watch

- **Position updates:** which way are they trading?
- **Conviction changes:** when do they shift views?
- **Regime calls:** do they see regime shifts coming?
- **Flow commentary:** stablecoin flows, liquidations, options positioning.
- **Volume and support:** how many followers? Are they influencing retail flow?

### Uses

1. **Consensus identification:** when many traders hold the same view, the trade is crowded.
2. **Dissent identification:** when traders disagree, there is no consensus, which is inflection risk.
3. **Conviction measurement:** traders' position sizing and the intensity of their language.
4. **Retail flow proxy:** popular traders' views correlate with retail positioning.
