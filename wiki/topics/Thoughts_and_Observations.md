# Thoughts and Observations

Erlin's Thoughts note is a loose running file of short takes and pasted posts on aesthetics, attention, intelligence, gender, status and the shape of modern life. This page draws out its main threads with the key quotes. Every quote and link from the note is in the source page, [[Thoughts|Thoughts & Observations]]. Specific threads are developed in [[Philosophy_and_Meaning|Philosophy & Meaning]], [[Social_Commentary|Social Commentary]], [[Design_and_Aesthetics|Design & Aesthetics]] and [[Masculinity_and_Men|Masculinity & Men]]. Read the note as takes, not findings.

## Taste as confession

> Your aesthetic taste is a map of your inner world projected outward. What you're drawn to reveals which parts of your spirit are awake. Aesthetic taste is an unconscious confession.

Taste reveals character. The note applies the idea to Elon Musk: "Geniuses have blind spots, one of Elon's is beauty. He has bad taste and doesn't understand the power or political utility of image. It's why the women are mid, the logos are cringe, the jokes are bad, etc." See [[Design_and_Aesthetics|Design & Aesthetics]].

## Attention, effort and learning

- **One optional thing a day.** From a [post](https://x.com/skooookum/status/1932184434784010379) _(@skooookum)_: compared with the poster's 20s, there is more peace with the fact that, after the non-optional tasks (dog, relationships, work, chores), there is capacity to consistently do one optional thing really well each day. "I've got one thing, that's okay, and it's better to just do one thing each day than burn myself out indefinitely trying to do 3 or 4 things." Accepting that prevents revenge procrastination and burnout.
- **Information density.** "Podcasts probably have the lowest information density of any medium ever created, but listening to them is a totally passive activity. Unlike reading, it requires no energy expenditure. That's partly why they're horrible for learning." Reading carries more information and demands active energy; passive consumption is not deep understanding.
- **Work theatre.** From [@signulll](https://x.com/signulll): "we’ve built an entire shadow economy around the simulation of work rather than work itself." Consuming guides, planners and frameworks trains you "to exist in a preparatory state instead of an executory one" — "a kind of paralysis disguised as momentum." See [[Technology_Critique|Technology Critique]] and [[Self-Improvement Culture Critique]].

## Story as the interface

> storytelling is the ultimate compression algorithm for human attention. everything else, data, logic, tech feeds into it, but if you can't wrap it in a compelling narrative, nobody will give a shit. people think they make decisions based on facts, but they're mostly responding to the shape of a story, whether it's a personal arc, a company vision, or a product pitch.
>
> if you want to accomplish anything meaningful, the story is the interface between it & the world. get that wrong & nobody will give af. get it right & you bend steel with your bare hands.

Get the narrative wrong and the idea dies. See [[Philosophy_and_Meaning|Philosophy & Meaning]].

## Intelligence, autism and AI

- **Autism and integrity.** "the correlation between autism and traits like honor and integrity is astronomically high relative to the general population." The note pairs it with "the best thing to do in life is to find autistic people and surround yourself with them". The reading: autists don't do social theatre; they do truth. See [[Neurodiversity]].
- **AI exposes human exceptionalism as pattern-matching.** From [@signulll](https://x.com/signulll/status/1891192852711190634): "ai exposes how much of what we call “creativity” is remixing, how much “insight” is regurgitation, & how much “expertise” is just memorised heuristics."
- **High IQ and basic human situations.** A passage on Charlton (2009a, 2009b) argues that people with high IQ often fail at basic human situations because they apply abstract thinking where instinctive strategies are more adaptive. A one-liner elsewhere in the note: "Why do intelligent people struggle with love? Because it’s not logical." ([post](https://x.com/lokymann/status/1886166317381669170) _(@lokymann)_)

## Behaviour shapes the body

A [post](https://x.com/BlondB00/status/1882121878757847356) _(@BlondB00)_ claims behaviour reshapes morphology: "when I started to make more aggressive masculine decisions, my features got sharper, my frame got bigger and I put on muscle easier as I was in more precarious environments". It is one person's account; the poster says the SAID principle "doesn't really uncover the mechanism behind it" and asks for a scientific explanation.
