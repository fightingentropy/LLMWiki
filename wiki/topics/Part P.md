# Part P

This page concerns **England**. Part P is a statutory electrical-safety requirement; **Approved Document P** is guidance on satisfying it. The earlier page incorrectly described Part P itself as non-statutory and treated its scope as uniform across the UK.

## Scope and notification

[GOV.UK's Approved Document P](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/electrical-safety-approved-document-p), sections 2–3, covers dwellings and specified associated installations, including some business premises sharing a dwelling's meter. Notification includes new circuits, consumer-unit replacement and additions or alterations within defined special locations. Being in a kitchen alone does not make all work notifiable. Non-notifiable work still has to comply.

Choose the applicable certification/notification route before work begins. An installation certificate and a Building Control notification serve different purposes; holding qualifications alone does not establish registration in a competent person scheme. Check the official document for the particular installation and any special-location boundaries.
