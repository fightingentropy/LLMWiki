# Oil

Oil is a cyclical commodity driven by supply and demand, with significant geopolitical tail risk. Erlin's handbook note gives its drivers in one line; this page expands them, walks through the boom-bust cycle that oil follows, and sets out what that means for trading. For the asset class as a whole, see [[Commodities]].

## The handbook's list of drivers

The note files oil under commodities, which it sums up as "Boom & bust cycles, cyclical, depend on supply & demand". For oil futures it gives:

> factors: OPEC decisions, Geopolitical events, GDP, policy changes (renewable energy etc)

(from [[Markets - Handbook]])

## Fundamentals

- **OPEC production**: OPEC quotas and actual output, and strategic release decisions.
- **Non-OPEC supply**: US shale, Russia and other producers.
- **Global demand**: GDP growth, transportation, manufacturing activity.
- **Geopolitical events**: wars, sanctions and supply disruptions (the Strait of Hormuz, pipelines and so on).

## Policy and technology

- **Energy policy**: support for EVs and renewable energy investment.
- **Renewable transition**: long-term demand pressure from EV adoption.
- **Technology**: the shale revolution changed structural supply dynamics.

## The oil cycle

1. **High prices** incentivise exploration and production.
2. **Supply ramp**: new projects come online and oversupply builds.
3. **Price crash**: excess supply kills exploration and production spending.
4. **Supply gap**: underinvestment creates a structural shortage.
5. **Price spike**: the cycle returns to step 1.

This is the oil version of the boom-bust cycle the handbook applies to all commodities. See [[Commodities]] and [[Market Cycles]].

## Trading implications

- **Cyclical**: correlated with growth; rallies in risk-on, falls in risk-off. See [[Market Regime]].
- **Geopolitical**: supply disruptions can spike the price regardless of fundamentals.
- **Tail risk**: a geopolitical shock could spike oil 50% or more in days.
- **Policy sensitive**: energy policy changes are slow but structural.
