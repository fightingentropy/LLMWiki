# Humor

Three notes hold the saved jokes: [[Pick Up Limes]], [[Jokes]] and [[Funny]]. Smaller bits are scattered through [[X Takes]], [[Bookmarks - Media & Inspiration]] and [[Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas]]. This page says what each archive holds, names the comic modes that keep recurring across them, marks the tonal boundaries, and lists the linked clips and memes behind the newer material. The content is crude, sexual and dark in places; it is kept in the owner's voice.

## The three archives

- [[Pick Up Limes]] — the smallest (about 2KB). Skewed toward pickup-line / comeback territory: crude dick-size comebacks ("Bitch you can open your mouth and measure it"), sexual innuendo, dating banter ("dating me rn is like investing in Nvidia in 2018"), short absurdist one-liners. The title is a wordplay on the YouTuber Pick Up Limes; the line that explains it sits in [[Jokes]]: "Throw limes in front of a girl and tell her 'can you help me?' I'm not good at 'pick up limes'".
- [[Jokes]] — large and varied (about 30KB). Heavier on Greek wordplay, name-puns (Irish, Italian, Arab, Japanese and others), long-form set pieces (the element-table chemistry bit, the ancient-Greek Σ. griphos, the palm/hand romance ending in "χείρα") and English observational or absurd one-liners. Some entries repeat within the file.
- [[Funny]] — the largest (about 44KB). Link-heavy: about thirty embedded X/Twitter URLs, YouTube timestamps, Urban Dictionary and Wikipedia links. Closer to a shitpost / crypto-twitter / hospitality-worker voice than [[Jokes]]. Multilingual fragments (Greek, Albanian, Spanish, bilingual collisions). Long-form set pieces: the "Everything is priced in" wsb copypasta, the Lt-Gen Carton de Wiart bio, Daft Punk and Kanye, the Jocko-parody motivational routine, the "negative cash flow girlfriend" breakup, the Pizza-Hut-WWF-No-Mercy time-warp. Overlaps with the other two archives.

General cultural observations live in [[Culture|Culture & Observations]]; the humor archives sit in the same domain.

[[X Takes]] is a takes note rather than a joke file, but some of its one-liners land as jokes: "Now that the funny money's gone, be prepared for 3-5 years of coffee dates"; "I listened to one episode of 'call her daddy' and no joke actually immediately invested in some Pharma companies working on new classes of antidepressants."

## Recurring registers

Across the three archives the same comic modes keep showing up. Useful as a taxonomy when deciding where a new bit belongs.

- **Greek wordplay / puns** — the dominant mode in [[Jokes]]; scattered through [[Funny]]. Puns on element names, nationalities rendered as fake names, single-word neologisms that collapse two registers (*απανθρακομακεδόνας*, *πιτσιρικαρία*, *αυτοπατίνωση*, *γλουγλουτένη*).
- **Nationality / name-pun bits** — the "Ιρλανδός που δεν κάνει χάρες; Bart O' Butsom" format. Streaks through [[Jokes]]: Punto Marafeti, Mafta Kemafta, Massimo Tonelione, Spy Putses.
- **Sexual / crude comebacks** — dick-size retorts, pickup lines, dating banter. Dominant in [[Pick Up Limes]], present in [[Jokes]] and [[Funny]]. This portion overlaps with [[Dating_and_Relationships|Dating & Relationships]].
- **Dark or nihilistic one-liners** — "You know what I realised? I don't give a shit about losing my life, you know what I give a shit about ? Losing wifi ."; "Just checked my bank account and realized I can live comfortably without working for the rest of my life as long as I die on Sunday"; "I would like to thank my spine, for supporting me all these years" (all from [[Jokes]]).
- **Long-form set pieces** — shaggy-dog stories with delayed punchlines after rows of `.` dots. From [[Jokes]]: the element-table finish *SiKW HORePSe KOUKLi MoU*, the "θα λύσω, μη λύσει ο Σ." griphos, the palm/hand romance ("Έμεινε χείρα"). From [[Funny]]: Lt-Gen Carton de Wiart's war-injury résumé, copied from his [Wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrian_Carton_de_Wiart) (shot in the face, head, stomach, groin, ankle, leg, hip and ear; "Frankly, I had enjoyed the war."); the Neapolitan nobleman who fought more than 20 duels over whether Dante or Ariosto was the greater poet and admitted on his deathbed he had never read either; the "Everything is priced in" r/wsb copypasta; the Jocko-parody motivational routine ("Shower with liquid nitrogen", "for breakfast, 6 egg shells and four banana peels", "decide to go easy today, do 5000 pushups and 2000 pull-ups"); the "negative cash flow girlfriend" breakup ("I really loved that bitch but she just doesn't generate revenue").
- **Observational absurd** — Mitch-Hedberg-shaped bits: the kid crying at the airport ("somebody claim this guys . Like it's luggage"), the shark triathlon ("it would all come down to who is the better cyclist"), the 6000 ants dressed up as rice who robbed a Chinese restaurant, Kasparov and the chequered tablecloth ("it took him 10 minutes to pass me the salt").
- **Meta / format jokes** — the Roman-numeral "I M LIVID" reveal (1 - I, 1000 - M, 51 - LI, 6 - VI, 500 - D); the SVU opening transposed to lawn maintenance ("an elite squad known as the Special Mowing Unit. These are their stories. 8:00am - Sherwood Court 'A Pain in the Grass'"); Giordano Pietrosono's 12 rules to be an Italian, where rules 1, 3, 4, 5 and 11 are all variants of "shut up" (rule 2 is the same in Italian).
- **Crypto / markets shitpost** — mostly a [[Funny]] register: "chronically on-chain"; the Voldemort-horcrux hardware-wallet brag ("i'm decentralised af... hard wallets, recovery in titanium fireproof/disasterproof storage"); "Rather be bullish and wrong than bearish and gay."; the wsb "Everything is priced in" copypasta; "You don't have an algorithmic laddered delta neutral gamma capture strat, brah? Don't even try."; "The existence of karmic debt implies the existence of karmic credit and karmic default swaps"; "Crypto and Onlyfans are the only industries where you can sit at home naked and take money from other men all day long" (see [[OnlyFans]]). Cross-refs [[Crypto Trading]] and [[Trading Philosophy]].
- **Hospitality / restaurant-worker voice** — almost exclusively [[Funny]]: "Nothing more silly than the urgency of restaurant work. Oh table 6 needs a lobster right now? Grow up."; smuggling free food from the pass ("can you wrap this for me they need it to go and I just keep it for myself hehe"); "you're literally armwrestling customers"; customers who bring their own still water and tea bags, "like they're having a picnic over here"; "In hospitality it's all about how do I convey a message in the most sophisticated, plausible deniability kind of way possible." This reads as first-person material from restaurant work, not curated jokes. The note also saves a clip of [Albanian restaurant owners](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SMImggxi1e0) answering a bad review with "come and say that to my face".
- **Link-and-URL bookmarks** — [[Funny]]'s defining feature: embedded X/Twitter URLs and YouTube timestamps, plus Urban Dictionary and Wikipedia links. These are mnemonics for something seen online rather than jokes lifted as text.

## Tone and languages

- The content is deliberately crude, sexual and dark-comedic in places, with occasional slur-laden lines. The archives are stored verbatim and are not sanitised; this page does not repeat the slurs.
- Greek, English and Albanian bits coexist without translation. Some jokes only work in Greek (wordplay), some only in English, and a handful ride on bilingual collisions: *Japan ενδεχόμενο*, *ΚΤΕΛ Αβίβ*, *Tattoo Καίσαρος* (from [[Jokes]]); *φωτόσπαθο μες στο κλαμπ* (from [[Funny]]).
- Both punchline jokes (setup, then payoff) and vibe fragments are saved. Fragments like "Brutal hippo attack", "Sound check", "Will smith husband hair lost", "Wired headphones." and a two-word fragment built on a racial slur are presumably mnemonics for something seen online.
- Cross-archive repeats: a handful of lines appear in two or three of the archives — the "math test / boyfriend" cheating line ("Oh I'm sorry I thought we were talking about things we could cheat on"), "Θα χέσω με καισαρική", the "blowjob voice" [Urban Dictionary link](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=Blowjob%20Voice), "I'm very vulnerable rn if any bad bitches want to take advantage of me", "Έλα να μου ξεβουλώσεις τ'αρχίδια". These are personal re-saves, not a deduplicated collection.

## Clip-led and image-led material

The clip-led material is mostly links rather than text. In [[Funny]]: visual non sequiturs, sports-and-hardware language collisions, finance-jargon reaction images, style parody, dark wordplay and sexual anecdotes. In [[Bookmarks - Media & Inspiration]] and [[Bookmarks - Culture & Ideas]]: visual puns, trader reactions, authentication fatigue, academic-employment humor, wildlife clips, absurd succession jokes and adult ephemera. All of it is listed under Resources below.

Two boundaries apply to this material:

- A caption's insinuation about an athlete, influencer or performer is not evidence of private conduct. Where the image context is missing, it stays missing rather than being guessed.
- The [[Funny]] note ends with a dehumanizing racial-propaganda passage, not a joke with a separable setup and punchline. It is covered only through [[Online Extremism and Evidence]]; its slurs are not repeated here and its pseudo-scientific claims are not facts. The hostile race-and-appearance memes in the bookmark pages are listed on those pages, not here; keeping them does not turn their framing into a factual claim.
