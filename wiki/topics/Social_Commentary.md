# Social Commentary

Observations on everyday society: how place, status, technology and gender shape the people inside them. [Read the original note](/source/0eceef8f6c2ea7e5a42f).

These are takes, mostly from X. The posts on gender and religion generalise from one person or one clip to a whole group and carry no more weight than that.

## Place shapes the person

> "Every country has an energy. And that energy rewires you whether you notice it or not. People move to Japan and become minimal. People move to Mexico and their entire relationship with time softens." _(@tishray)_

You are the average of the places, routines and inputs you meet most, not only of five people. "Every space you occupy is voting on who you become," so choosing where to live may matter more than a job title, "because the place shapes the plan."

- **Rooms count too.** An Austin bar that [takes your phone at the door](https://x.com/signulll/status/1926453874753175612): "you remember what it's like when conversations actually finish". _(@signulll)_
- **Climate as precondition.** Lee Kuan Yew called air conditioning "perhaps one of the signal inventions of history", because it made development possible in the tropics. The jokes followed (see Resources).
- **A changing place feels like loss.** "The city I once loved—vibrant, safe, recognisably English—is gone." _(@CF_Farrow)_ The Critical Drinker reads a high street of vape shops, nail salons and barbers as decline; vacancy and footfall data would test it.

## Status

- **New money beside old.** "Like, what do you mean you're here because you hit a 50x on Fartcoin? The dude next to you owns the water supply for eastern Belgium."
- **Status splinters.** One society-wide ladder has broken into pockets by city, profession and platform.
- **Pick games with useful side effects.** Lifting and shipping work build capacity; display spending does not.
- **Visible winners are selected.** Networks and capital lift some capable people over equally capable others.
- **The hunger to be sorted.** "a high-level rizz move that requires a fair bit of intellectual dishonesty is to basically be a real-life Sorting Hat. People want identity and a sense of purpose and tribe SO badly". Millennials "love being labeled".

## Arguing about groups

Two ideas pull against each other, and both hold. An exception does not refute a statistical claim: answering an average with "I know one who isn't" misses the abstraction. But an average does not decide the person in front of you either. Ask how big the effect is, how much the groups overlap, and whether the decision is about a population or an individual. [[Nassim Taleb]] adds that within-group variance can swamp a selection decision.

## Technology and attention

- **Tools shape the wielder.** "so can tools mend and shape the wielder." A thread on why young women dislike tech treats it as a fair question, not an irrational one.
- **Three tipping points.** "the introduction of the feed by Zuck in 2011, the next was the tiktokfication of everything (google, meta, Tiktok) then the newest wave is AI slop".
- **Passive input.** "Podcasts probably have the lowest information density of any medium ever created". Work theatre, the productivity-tool version, is on [[Personal Knowledge Management]].
- **Silence as status.** "Listening to music in general is peasant coded" ([@RoguesPhilo](https://x.com/RoguesPhilo/status/1922373194666623317)).
- **Spatial drift.** "Noticing a steady increase in people who are seemingly unaware of their existence in three dimensional space."
- **AI as mirror.** "ai exposes how much of what we call 'creativity' is remixing, how much 'insight' is regurgitation"
- **Keep chosen friction.** Outsourcing every inconvenience also removes practice at navigating, cooking and improvising. Cut waste; keep difficulty you would choose. Agents that ask for wide access to money and messages belong under [[Digital Security]].

## Gender and dating

- **The ideology gap.** Naval: "The state can substitute for what women want from men, but it can't substitute for what men want from women." See [[Gender Dynamics]].
- **Dating's next era.** "going full Latin Novella... Paragraph texts , ring the doorbell, get back together a third time, drama on the balcony. What else is life for" ([@sighswoon](https://x.com/sighswoon/status/1923147106014871588)).
- Hostile takes on childless gym-goers and "feminized" men, and satire about Islam, sit in the original note; how such posts argue is on [[Political Commentary]].

## Slow decline

An unattributed passage: "There will be no 'happening.' It's far more insidious than that." Things cost a little more each day, homes shrink, hours lengthen, fewer marry. Its ending:

> "You are stupid, ugly, and weak, but together we are free, prosperous, and safe."
> That is the collapse. The reduction of the American man into a feudal serf
