# Crypto Trading

Crypto trades as a momentum market. Market structure, moving averages, leverage and crowd perception move price more than any single fact, and its cycles run fast. Most frames here come from trader posts in [[Crypto Takes]]; the dashboards are in [[Markets - Trading]], the saved posts in [[Bookmarks - Markets & Crypto]], and the traders to follow in [[Traders Directory]].

## What moves the market

- **Perception, not truth.** "Truth doesn't necessarily move markets. Perception & Uncertainty/Certainty transition does." (from [[Markets - Trading]])
- **The watch list.** CT sentiment, stablecoin dominance and yields, funding, liquidations, options positioning, moving averages, M2 and the macro regime, catalysts, and what top traders hold.
- **Weak signals.** Raw funding predicts daily price poorly, and top-decile Hyperliquid wallets barely persist month to month, so copying the leaderboard is a poor plan. See [[Leverage and Liquidations]].

## How bull markets end

- **Dilution.** Pentosh1: "Dilution is the bull market killer. Supply is infinite in this market as we dream up a million new coins a day." The biggest gains come late, so "Greed is the parabola you sell into little by little." (from [[Crypto Takes]])
- **Late, low-conviction money.** cobie: "Low conviction money enters extremely late and offsets their lack of belief by bidding only "new projects" so they can be "early"". (from [[Crypto Takes]])
- **The real top signal.** @0xSisyphus: "Normies showing up isn't a top signal. When they start quitting their IRL jobs for crypto or starting funds that's when we need to worry."

## PvP and PvE

Rewkang splits cycles into PvP, where traders trade against each other, and PvE, where new retail money keeps arriving. Heuristics from one fail in the other: "In bull markets funding can stay higher than shorts can remain solvent." New money buys its favourite memecoins with every paycheck, and traders who short them get blown out, like the AXS, ADA and LUNA faders. "To outperform like retail you need to think like retail." (from [[Crypto Takes]])

## Supercycle or inverse supercycle

cobie called crypto a decade-long supercycle bubble, "a siren for the market top of the multi-decade Everything Bubble". GCRClassic's inverse supercycle sends it to zero for the same reasons: gambling is a vast human use case, gen z prefers memes to growth stocks, and joblessness breeds a "generation moonshot". DegenSpartan on the split:

> i think crypto is a bet on degradation of society, failure of trust and trad economies, forced speculation, desperation and human greed we are not the same

## Turning points

- **Three bear phases.** Robert Rhea, 1935: hopes abandoned, then falling earnings, then distress selling, each split by "a secondary reaction which is often erroneously assumed to be the beginning of a bull market." (from [[Business & Finance]])
- **Reflexive pumps.** "Reflexive pumps don't begin with a wave of buyers, they start with no one left willing to sell." (from [[Business & Finance]])
- **No crash signal.** Alex Krüger on altcoin crashes of 25% or more in a day: "crashes may come in the middle of a bull run, at the top, or at the bottom. That is right. There's no signal." (from [[Crypto Takes]])
- **Flush bottoms.** Husslin's checklist of forced selling is on [[Leverage and Liquidations]].

## Price discovery across cycles

Satoshi Nakamoto, 2010: "The price of any commodity tends to gravitate toward the production cost." thiccythot sees discovery move "from blunt proxies to more precise expressions with each wave": WTI first in an oil shock, then Brent; in crypto, "each cycle becoming more selective with asset quality". (from [[Crypto Takes]])

## Taking profits

- **Let winners pay for losers.** 0xaporia: when a strategy wins less than half the time, outsized winners fund the losses, so cutting green trades early starves it. @Cheshire_Cap's arithmetic: 20% winners at 5x and 80% losers at -50% is +40% EV; taking profit at 2x turns it to -20%.
- **Selling the top is the risky sale.** Zhu Su: the euphoria of a top sale leads to early rebids and overconfidence.
- Bag-holding and taking money out of the market are on [[Trading Psychology]].

## Venues and side markets

- **Discovery Bounds V2.** [[Hyperliquid]] 24/7 perps use active price bands, so price discovery runs while traditional markets are shut. @0xmev says weekend volume hit a record near $1 billion.
- **Prediction markets.** ML engineers quietly mine sports betting and Polymarket; arbitrage bots there use Frank-Wolfe and Bregman projection.
- Crypto essays such as "Rethinking Crypto Cycles" are in [[Markets - Articles]].
