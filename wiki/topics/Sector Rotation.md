# Sector Rotation

Sector rotation is the shift in market leadership between sectors as growth expectations and the [[Market Regime]] change. Different sectors outperform in different environments. The [[Markets - Brief]] daily overview asks the [[Equities]] read to cover "index internals, breadth, leadership, cyclicals vs defensives"; this page is the framework behind that line.

## Sector categories

### Cyclical sectors

Sensitive to the economic cycle; they outperform when growth is expected.

- **Industrials**: capital spending, manufacturing, construction.
- **Materials**: commodity-linked, mining, chemical production.
- **Energy**: oil and gas, power generation.
- **Financials**: interest rates, credit growth, risk appetite.
- **Consumer Discretionary**: spending sensitive to confidence and cash flow.

### Defensive sectors

Stable earnings; they outperform when growth is a concern.

- **Consumer Staples**: necessities, non-discretionary spending.
- **Healthcare**: steady demand, demographic support, less cyclical.
- **Utilities**: essential services, regulated returns, bond-like.
- **Real Estate / REITs**: inflation hedge, stable yields.

### Neutral or structural growth

- **Technology**: partly cyclical, partly structural; depends on whether it is a capex boom or a period of caution.
- **Telecom**: bond-like characteristics, stable dividends.
- **Energy transition**: structural growth, but cyclically sensitive.

## Rotation through the cycle

| Phase | When | Outperform | Underperform | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Early cycle (recovery) | Exiting recession, growth accelerating | Cyclicals, especially Industrials and Materials | Defensives (no longer needed as insurance) | Cyclicals sharply outperforming defensives |
| Mid-cycle (expansion) | Sustained growth, no recession fears | Cyclicals continue, but dispersion narrows. Leadership shift: tech and growth may start outperforming as capex and earnings growth accelerate | — | Broad market strength, most sectors advancing |
| Late cycle (overheating) | Growth too fast, inflation rising, Fed tightening | Energy (inflation), Materials (commodity strength) | Long-duration growth and tech (rate sensitivity), rate-sensitive defensives | Energy leading, tech underperforming despite earnings growth |
| Recession / contraction | Growth slowing, recession feared | Defensives (recession insurance), Staples (must-buy) | Cyclicals (earnings falling) | Defensive rally, cyclical collapse |

See [[Market Cycles]] for the cycle itself and [[Cross-Asset Analysis]] for the macro context that shows up in sector leadership.

## Trading the rotation

### Momentum approach

Trade the rotation that is already happening.

- Cyclicals outperforming: momentum continues until sentiment shifts.
- Defensives outperforming: growth concerns are intact; stay defensive.

### Mean-reversion approach

Rotate when ratios reach extremes.

- Cyclicals/Defensives ratio at an extreme: rotation risk.
- Value/Growth ratio at an extreme: rotation likely.

### Data-driven approach

Follow the data that drives rotation.

- PMI accelerating: cyclicals due.
- Earnings growth slowing: cyclicals due for a pullback.
- Credit spreads widening: defensives due. See [[Credit]].

## Metrics to watch

- **Cyclical/Defensive ratio**: chart it explicitly.
- **Earnings revisions**: earnings going up means cyclical strength is likely.
- **Economic surprises**: beats favour cyclicals, misses favour defensives.
- **Fed tone**: tightening favours defensives, easing favours cyclicals. See [[Rates]].

## Rotation in crypto

Crypto has its own sector leadership. [[Markets - Trading]] saves a Delphi Digital sector-performance view among its useful links. [[Rewkang]] lists the need to rotate among heuristics that work in PvP markets but less in PvE markets, where new money keeps buying (from [[Crypto Takes]]):

> "The second is the need to rotate. Understand that new money does not schizo trade like crypto traders. They pick their favorite meme coins and just keep sending every paycheck in to continue buying every day/week. Traders that short memecoins because they are “being rotated out of” eventually will get blown out just as those that faded AXS, ADA, LUNA last cycle"
