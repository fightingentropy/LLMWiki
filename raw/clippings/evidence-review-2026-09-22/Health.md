## Dental health

NHS guidance recommends fluoride toothpaste twice daily and at least 1,350 ppm fluoride for adults. This conflicts with the Medical note's recommendation to avoid ordinary fluoride toothpaste on a thyroid-suppression theory. The saved note supplies no clinical evidence establishing that theory for normal toothpaste use.

Source: [NHS — How to keep your teeth clean](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/healthy-teeth-and-gums/how-to-keep-your-teeth-clean/), accessed 2026-09-22.

## Dopamine synthesis

The tyrosine-hydroxylase reaction uses tyrosine, oxygen and tetrahydrobiopterin. Sunlight is not a reagent in that reaction. This is distinct from effects of light exposure on circadian timing.

Sources: [NCBI PubChem — catecholamine biosynthesis](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pathway/BioCyc%3AHUMAN_PWY66-301); [original enzyme kinetics study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6117320/), accessed 2026-09-22.

## Exercise

NHS guidance for adults aged 19–64 includes 150 minutes of moderate activity or 75 minutes of vigorous activity per week, with strength work on at least two days. Activity should fit the person's health and ability.

Source: [NHS adult physical activity guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/physical-activity-guidelines-for-adults-aged-19-to-64/), accessed 2026-09-22.

The 2019 push-up study followed 1,104 active male firefighters. Greater baseline push-up capacity was associated with fewer cardiovascular events. The widely shared 96% figure describes a comparison between groups in this observational cohort, not a reduction caused by doing forty push-ups and not a universal cardiac screening test.

Source: [Yang et al., JAMA Network Open, 2019](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2724778), doi:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2018.8341, accessed 2026-09-22.

## Supplements

NHS guidance says adults should consider 10 micrograms of vitamin D daily in autumn and winter; some groups need year-round supplementation. This is a specific public-health recommendation, not support for the note's entire supplement stack.

Source: [NHS vitamin D guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/vitamins-and-minerals/vitamin-d/), accessed 2026-09-22.

## Retatrutide and preparation claims

The FDA states retatrutide is not a component of an FDA-approved drug and has not been found safe and effective for any condition. Its statement is about US regulatory status; it is not a finding about every country's approvals. Research-vendor products and a saved injection guide do not establish clinical suitability, identity, purity, or formulation stability.

Source: [FDA — concerns with unapproved GLP-1 drugs](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-alerts-and-statements/fdas-concerns-unapproved-glp-1-drugs-used-weight-loss), accessed 2026-09-22.

The Bacteriostatic Water for Injection label describes 0.9% benzyl alcohol and directs use according to the drug manufacturer's instructions. Compatibility varies. A preservative does not validate a universal 28-day shelf life for every reconstituted drug. The original note's precise shaking, freezing and room-temperature potency-loss percentages have no supporting stability study attached.

Source: [DailyMed — Bacteriostatic Water prescribing label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=ccadcf46-6a6f-436b-9bbc-17e2983a335f&type=display), accessed 2026-09-22.
