- [Privacy critique of proposed UK child-device nudity blocking](https://x.com/mullvadnet/status/2064342870937509988) — The [8 June government announcement](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/new-plans-to-stop-children-taking-sharing-or-viewing-nude-images) asked Apple and Google to implement device-level nudity blocking for children within three months and threatened legislation if progress proved inadequate; adults could disable it through age assurance and government said processing should remain on-device without data collection. - [Is the iPhone Birth Control?](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35310) — A June 2026 NBER working paper uses AT&T's 2007–2011 iPhone exclusivity and coverage variation to estimate that early smartphone diffusion explains 33–52% of the decline in the US general fertility rate. This is a new identification claim relying on AT&T coverage as a proxy, not settled consensus; await replication and peer review. _(Discovered via vittorio (@IterIntellectus); live review 2026-07-25)_
- [Ancient DNA reveals pervasive directional selection across West Eurasia](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10358-1) — A 2026 Nature study applied an ancient-DNA time-series method to 15,836 West Eurasians and reported many hundreds of alleles affected by strong directional selection over the past ten millennia. Its complex-trait projections use effects measured in modern industrialized cohorts, and the authors say it is unclear how those relate to past adaptive phenotypes; do not substitute the tweet's intelligence-focused gloss for that limitation. _(Discovered via i/o research (@iointelresearch); live review 2026-07-25)_
