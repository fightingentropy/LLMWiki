## Self-hosted platform watchlist

OpenShip — open-source application deployment and operations platform for infrastructure you control.
https://openship.io/

Treat it as a product to evaluate, not a verified production dependency. Before adopting it, test:
- restore from backup;
- rollback under failure;
- secrets and access controls;
- monitoring and alert delivery;
- outbound-email reputation and deliverability from a clean server.
